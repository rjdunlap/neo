# 2026-11-18 — Movie Night upright on phones (PR #1833)

**Changed:** Movie Night plays upright at levels 1–3. `layout.ts` is now a pure function of the view: upright, the strip is two columns of 230 × 140 squares, the request sits above it, the little screen takes the space left between the strip and the pads, and the four sound pads and four round buttons sit up from the bottom edge, clear of the guide. Sideways and an upright tablet keep the 1024 layout (the old tall layout was a 1024-wide plan with a 1100-high control row that fell off a 1024-tall tablet view and ran off a 768-wide screen). The wrap arrow in `index.ts` uses the slot edge. `upright: true` is set and the registry list holds 68 games.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`; vitest for `src/games/movie-night` and `registry.test.ts`, including the new `upright.test.ts` (levels 1–3 on the three phone views, the captioned view and a 375 × 601 view): all passed.
**Ran:** `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames PHONE_ONLY=movie-night PHONE_BAND=prek PHONE_LEVELS=1,3` passed at 390 × 844 (both levels completed by real touches); the upright screenshot was looked at (strip and request upright, pads and buttons low; the empty middle is the space for the little screen that appears on Watch). Headless Chrome only.
