# 2026-11-26 — Little Conductor level 8, Revise for a listener (PR #2246)

**Changed:** Little Conductor level 8 (`revise` mode) for the school band (window 5–8): reuses level 7's listener sign and four dial pads (`turtle`, `rabbit`, `gentle`, `strong`), adding two prepared version buttons (`1` and `2`, `130 × 100`) and an `Undo` button (`130 × 100`). Each round prepares two ways to play (`makeAsks`, differing in one property); tapping `1` or `2` loads that version and plays its phrase, tapping any dial revises the active version away from its original and plays the revision, and `Undo` restores the active original and closes the revision-heard gate. No app score, misses or hints (`misses: 0, hints: 0`): Done opens once both prepared versions are heard whole, an active revision differs from the original, and the revision is heard whole, inviting a spoken explanation to someone nearby. Added eight `{name}` voice lines, level 8 how-to card entries, 14 rule and 8-view layout tests, and the `conductorRevise` browser case (`CONDUCTOR_LEVELS=8`).
