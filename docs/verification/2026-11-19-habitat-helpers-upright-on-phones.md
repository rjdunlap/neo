# 2026-11-19 — Habitat Helpers upright on phones (PR #1882)

**Changed:** Habitat Helpers plays upright at every level (`upright: true`, levels 1–9, two scenes). The layout was pulled out of the garden and rocky-shore scenes into a pure `layout.ts` (`gardenFrame`, `plotXs`, `trayPoints`, `choicePoints`, `needPoints`, `shoreFrame`, `placeXs`, `friendPoints`, `gatePoint`) with the sideways plan pinned identical by a test; upright the loose cards rest in up to two rows, the garden's spaces above them, visitors over the garden and the goal sign above, the shore's three places are 250 wide and its friends rest in two rows. Incidental fix: the garden's spaces were 120 apart under 124-unit touch areas, now 130 apart sideways too.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`; the full `src/games` unit tests (318 files, 14344 tests); the new per-level `upright.test.ts` (every level and round, 3 seeds, 5 views, every number of loose cards), which found two real faults before any browser run (the plot pitch, and the 3-place shore span of 780 on 768), both fixed.
**Ran:** `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames PHONE_ONLY=habitat-helpers`: school levels 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 played to completion by real touches, one sticker each (levels 1–3 share the build scene with 4 and 6 and were not played separately); screenshots of levels 6, 7 and 9 viewed. Headless Chrome only. `GAME_URL` must end in `/classic/` and the band must contain the level (school 4–9).
**Not run:** build, offline, other suites (shell and engine untouched).
