# 2026-11-27 — Dream Cards level 2 Two clues for one picture (PR #2261)

**Changed:** `dream-cards` level 2 for the school band: after her first point the giver (pet or a person) adds a second clue for the same picture, she keeps her point or moves it, and the picture turns over with a line for both clues. New rule functions in `logic.ts` (`bothFit`, `fittingTriples`, `petPick2`, `petLook`, `secondKind`, `finalKind`, `hintAction2`), ten new voice lines and their reveal variants, a level-aware how-to line, and the ghost finger at the new phases. Help in the second look glows the two clue tokens, never a picture. One difference from the definition: the first clue stays in the token row, dimmed and still tappable to hear again, rather than moving to a separate "given" place, so the layout is unchanged.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`, `npm run format:check`, `npm run lint`, `npx vitest run src/games/dream-cards src/content/barrels.test.ts src/content/game-docs.test.ts` (the definition's counts all re-derived: 68/16/0 over the 84 deal pairs, 1/10/4 over the pool, 200 triples), `BROWSER_SUITE=dreamcards` (level 2 portrait play at 390 × 844: second clue, Help glows two clues, move her point, Swap, give two clues, finish, saved score 0 misses / 1 hint, plus the unchanged level 1 play) and `BROWSER_SUITE=fingerdemo FINGER_ONLY=dream-cards FINGER_LEVELS=2 FINGER_BAND=school` (finished in 10.3 s).
