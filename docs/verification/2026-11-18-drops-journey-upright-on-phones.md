# 2026-11-18 — Drop's Journey upright on phones (PR #1827)

**Changed:** Drop's Journey plays upright at every level 1–3. Its layout moved from `logic.ts` into a pure `layout.ts` (`journeyLayout`, `stripRoom`, `STRIP_STEP`, sizes). Held upright the journey strip, the book and the leaf (level 2: the postcard) sit in one row just above the cards (every touch within the bottom 450 units, about 229 px of a 390 × 844 phone), the strip starts at the screen edge and shows five stops, the land is no taller than 1.25 times its width and the sea is drawn only to the land's floor (`Layout.seaFloor`). Sideways and an upright tablet are unchanged, pinned by tests of exact values. `upright: true` is set and the registry list holds 64 games. Incidental cleanup: two unused variables removed from `landArt`.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`; `npx vitest run src/games/drops-journey src/games/registry.test.ts` (3 files, 118 tests passed); prettier check on the changed files. The new `upright.test.ts` covers every level, every number of cards a stop deals (2, 3 or 5), on three upright phone views, the captioned one and a short 375 × 601 one.
**Ran:** `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightsweep PHONE_ONLY=drops-journey PHONE_BANDS=all` before the flag: all three levels "fits" (smallest target 115), yet the screenshot showed the book and the leaf at the very top of a tall phone and the land stretched the full height (the fix above). `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames PHONE_ONLY=drops-journey PHONE_SHOTS=1` at 390 × 844 with real touches: pre-K level 1, school levels 2 and 3; each finished with one sticker (4, 3 and 5 touches), and a screenshot after every touch was looked at. Headless Chrome only.
**Not run:** `npm run build` locally (CI runs it), `fingerdemo` (the bot and the demonstrated action are unchanged), any other browser suite, the offline suite.
