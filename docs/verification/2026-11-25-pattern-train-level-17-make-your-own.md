# 2026-11-25 — Pattern Train level 17: Make your own (PR #2187)

**Changed:** `pattern-train` gains level 17 (school), where she taps one to three of three pieces to choose what repeats, can tap a chosen car to take it back, presses the green arrow, and the train runs her unit round three times with a chime per car while the pet says it back; it always ends with no miss and no hint.
