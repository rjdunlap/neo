# 2026-11-27 — Pond Merge levels 1-3 (PR #2291)

**Changed:** Built Pond Merge (`pond-merge`, Tinker Lab, pre-K level 1 and school levels 1–3, upright-ready, ghost finger) as a new game: a handwritten fixed-step circle-pile solver in `logic.ts` (no physics library, `src/engine/` unchanged), seeded drop sequences in even-count blocks, a free exact take-back, a hint rule shared by the Hint button and the ghost finger, and a no-fail "lift" that floats the smallest friends away from a full pond. Two solver details beyond the definition (supported pieces lose most of their downward speed; touching pieces each give way by at least a quarter of an overlap) and a rule-test overlap tolerance of 3 units instead of 2 are recorded on the game's page.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`, `npx eslint src/games/pond-merge`, `npx prettier --write` on the touched files, targeted vitest (`src/games/pond-merge`, `src/games/registry.test.ts`, `src/content`: 345 passed), the `pondmerge` browser suite (level 1 in landscape at 0/0; level 3 with a drop, a free take-back, one Hint, saved 0/1; one screenshot) and `fingerdemo` with `FINGER_ONLY=pond-merge` (levels 1 and 3 finished by the ghost finger).
**Not run:** the full `npm test` (runs in PR CI), `npm run build`, the offline check, an upright-phone screenshot (the harness viewport is not an upright phone; the upright layout is covered by the layout unit test at four views), and any check on a real phone or with a child.
