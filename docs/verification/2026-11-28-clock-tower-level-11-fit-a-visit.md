# 2026-11-28 — Clock Tower level 11, Fit a visit (PR #2316)

**Changed:** `clock-tower` gains school level 11 (`visit` mode, played after level 10): a free-time sign (tap to hear again) and a time strip with the free time as a green band, three quarter-hour start cards with exactly one where the whole visit fits (edges included), then setting the chosen start on the hands with quarter snaps and ringing the bell. A wrong card draws the visit on the strip and says it is too early or finishes too late; choosing and setting share one task's misses and single hint (the fitting card glows, or yellow hands once chosen). Rules keep absolute free-time and start minutes; first round free 2:00 to 3:30 for 30 minutes with starts 1:45, 2:30, 3:15. Upright layout puts the sign, strip and a row of cards above the tower (face full size on a phone, 80% at 768 × 1024). Scoped how-to card, `autotouch()` picks then drags; `woods-batch.mjs` covers levels 1–11 and `CLOCK_UPRIGHT=1` runs the woods suite on a touch phone for upright screenshots.
**Ran:** `npm run format:check`, `npm run lint` (eslint on the changed files), `npm run typecheck`, `npx vitest run src/games/clock-tower src/content/barrels.test.ts src/content/game-docs.test.ts` (99 passed, 1 skipped); `BROWSER_SUITE=woods WOODS_ONLY=clock CLOCK_LEVELS=11` sideways and with `CLOCK_UPRIGHT=1` (free sign replay and bell before choosing are free, an early and a late pick as the two misses with the glowing hint, a wrong setting as a third miss without a second hint, four starts set by finger, saved score and sticker; sideways, 768 × 1024 and 390 × 844 upright screenshots looked at); `BROWSER_SUITE=fingerdemo FINGER_ONLY=clock-tower FINGER_LEVELS=11 FINGER_BAND=school` (clean demo round in 32.6s).
**Not run:** the full unit suite and build (left to PR CI), earlier clock levels in the browser (their code paths are unchanged), a real phone or device speech.
