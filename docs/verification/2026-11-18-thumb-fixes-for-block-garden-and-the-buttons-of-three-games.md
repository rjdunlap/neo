# 2026-11-18 — Thumb fixes for Block Garden and the buttons of three games (PR #1818)

**Changed:** Block Garden's 8 × 8 beds grow from 72 to 90 units a square on an upright phone only (`cellFor`, `UPRIGHT_CELL`), and its tray sits 72 units under the bed on a tall view instead of 40 (`TRAY_GAP`: the finger rides under a carried piece and letting go in the piece's own slot sends it home, which bounced a piece meant for the bottom row above its own slot; a real fault, found while verifying); the buttons of Island Bridges, Word Search and Marble Jump are 88 units high (44.7 px). `grownup-thumbs.test.ts` table updated: Block Garden off the `UNDER` list, which is now six games (island-bridges, logic-grid, marble-jump, species-comeback, sudoku-garden, word-search).
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`; vitest for the four game folders, `grownup-thumbs.test.ts`, `upright-batch-3.test.ts` and `upright-batch-4.test.ts` (10 files, 206 tests); prettier and eslint on the changed folders; `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames` at 390 × 844 by real touches, one sticker each: Block Garden levels 7 and 12 (7 touches each), Marble Jump levels 1 and 9 (4 and 38), Word Search levels 5 and 7 (8 and 12), Island Bridges levels 5 and 4 (4 and 28); upright screenshots of Block Garden 7 and 12, Marble Jump 9, Word Search 7 and Island Bridges 4 looked at. The first Block Garden run at level 7 did not finish in 200 seconds (147 touches): a probe showed the bot's drag of a flat piece to the bottom row above its own slot being sent home, which is the tray-gap fault above and not a flake; it passed after the fix.
**Not run:** `npm run build` and the full unit suite locally (CI runs both), offline, world, sideways or iPad browser play (the sideways and iPad sizes of Block Garden are unchanged and guarded by its layout test; the taller buttons there are covered by layout tests only).
