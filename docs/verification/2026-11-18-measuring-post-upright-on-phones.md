# 2026-11-18 — Measuring Post upright on phones (PR #1829)

**Changed:** Measuring Post plays upright at every level 1–4. Its layout moved from the scene into a pure `layout.ts` (`postLayout(view, mode)`, `BUTTON`, `TOOL_PAD`). Held upright Help, Replay and Send (level 4's Check) are in the bottom row with the pads just above, level 2's answer rows are stacked there (every touch within 410 units of the bottom edge, 208 px of a 390 × 844 phone), the board sits on them and is no taller than 1.25 times its width (0.9 at level 1), and a line separates the two friends' halves at levels 2 and 3. Sideways and an upright tablet keep their positions, pinned by exact-value tests. `upright: true` is set and the registry list holds 65 games.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`; `npm test` (391 files, 9,583 passed, 1 skipped, after `npm run game-docs`); prettier and eslint on the changed files. The new `upright.test.ts` covers every level (one per mode) and every moment of a round that shows something to touch, on three upright phone views, the captioned one and a short 375 × 601 one.
**Ran:** `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames PHONE_ONLY=measuring-post PHONE_BAND=school PHONE_LEVELS=1,2,3,4 PHONE_SHOTS=1` at 390 × 844 with real touches: each level finished with one sticker (6, 6, 9 and 6 touches), and a screenshot of every moment was looked at (the look found the stretched tool shelf and the friend standing over the other's counts); levels 2 and 3 were replayed after the divider was added. Headless Chrome only.
**Not run:** `uprightsweep` (the game was flagged before the sweep, which stops listing a flagged game; the per-level test and the screenshots stand in), `npm run build` locally (CI runs it), `fingerdemo` (the bot and the demonstrated action are unchanged), any other browser suite, the offline suite.
