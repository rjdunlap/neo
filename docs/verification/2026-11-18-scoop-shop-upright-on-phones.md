# 2026-11-18 — Scoop Shop upright on phones (PR #1841)

**Changed:** Scoop Shop plays upright (`upright: true`, added to the registry test's upright list). Its layout is now the pure `layout.ts` (`counterTopFor`, `tubSpotsFor`): held upright the counter's top is 640 above the bottom edge and the tubs stand in two rows of up to three, 144 across, the bottom row right of the guide. Level 7's friends stand nearer the edges (0.15 and 0.85) so each tap box clears its cone's, and the level 11 customer's tap box is 340 tall (was 500) so it clears Home on a short phone. Before the fix the sweep flagged all 13 levels (tubs overlapping each other and, at levels 4–9 and 12–13, the guide); its "small" on the cone and stand decorations is wobble-only taps with 110-wide hit areas, not real targets.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`; full `npm test` (after `npm run game-docs`), including the new `upright.test.ts` (every level, each lap-opening tub count, five views: phone, small, tall, captioned, short); prettier and eslint on the game.
**Ran:** `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightsweep PHONE_ONLY=scoop-shop PHONE_BANDS=all` before the flag; then `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames PHONE_SHOTS=1` at school levels 5, 12, 13, 7, 8, 9, preschool 11 and lap 10: all completed with one sticker; screenshots of levels 5 and 7 looked right. Headless Chrome only.
**Not run:** build, offline, other browser suites (shell untouched), any real phone.
