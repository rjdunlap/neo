# 2026-11-23 — Harbor Orders levels 3–4 (PR #2056)

**Changed:** Level 3 adds the Bell as a third workshop; level 4 adds lanterns, so a square steps through crates, boats and lanterns. The rules now take any number of workshops and goods. *Most-of* checks every choice among a workshop's tied top rates, and the level 4 rule rejects boards where each good has its own single best maker. Each level has its own fallback board. Added one Kokoro line ("The Bell.").
**Rules and layout:** Typecheck; `npm test` (498 files, 18,530 tests). The Harbor Orders tests cover `fewest` against a brute-force walk over every arrangement of squares with three workshops (two goods up to 3 mornings, three goods up to 2), the level 3–4 generator rows over 120 and 60 seeds, the fallbacks, tied *most-of*, the three-good surplus wording, the hint walk at levels 1–4, and the layout at three rows and six mornings in every upright and sideways view. A probe outside the suite timed level 4 round start at a mean of 12 ms and a worst case of 147 ms over 1,000 seeds on this Mac. Production build with the new clip in the precache.
