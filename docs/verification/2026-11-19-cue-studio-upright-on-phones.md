# 2026-11-19 — Cue Studio upright on phones (PR #1857)

**Changed:** Cue Studio plays upright (`upright: true`). Its layout is a pure `layout.ts` (`cueLayout`). Held upright the five buttons sit at the bottom edge right of the guide corner, the cards above them, the shelf and rows above that, and the request pad or strip and the pet between Home and that group; level 5's row (two slots, Repeat cap, two ghosts) shifts left 272 and level 6's rows plus the actor column left 262 (pet and bird stay beside their rows, dotted trails), with the strip above the pet; level 4's rows shift right 44 to centre. Sideways and the non-upright narrow layout keep their numbers exactly.
**Ran:** `npm run typecheck`, eslint and prettier; `npm test` (the one failure was the stale generated doc, fixed by `npm run game-docs`), including the new `upright.test.ts` over levels 1–6 and five views (phone, small, tall, captioned, 375 × 601), and the registry test. Ghosts (96 wide) and level 4's chips (72 wide) are scenery, excluded from the 100-unit minimum only.
**Ran:** `BROWSER_SUITE=uprightgames PHONE_BAND=school PHONE_ONLY=cue-studio PHONE_LEVELS=3,4,5,6 PHONE_SHOTS=1` passed, one sticker each; screenshots of levels 3–6 viewed (nothing off the edge, controls at the bottom; on a tall phone the pet floats mid-screen with hills behind, by design). Headless Chrome only.
**Not run:** build, offline, other suites.
