# 2026-11-26 — Little Conductor level 7, one change (PR #2244)

**Changed:** Little Conductor level 7 for the school band (window 5–7): the listener's sign shows a way to play for each half of the tune (pace and strength together), the second half differs in exactly one property, and she sets slow or fast and soft or loud with the four pads, then taps Check. A wrong Check is a miss that names only the wrong property; two misses or Show a hint glow the first wrong dial's pad (once per half). Level 3's sign flow is unchanged in behaviour; the game screen now builds the sign for either level and the dial pads for levels 5 and 7. Ten new voice lines, all carrying the child's name, and a how-to card entry.
