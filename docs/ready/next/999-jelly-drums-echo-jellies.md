**`jelly-drums` Echo jellies** (lap, before level 1: takes the next free stable ID, placed first in lap's `order` and `entryLevels`, no renumbering; new interaction on an existing game: typecheck, rule tests, one filtered scripted play of the level, `fingerdemo` at it, one portrait screenshot). A jelly sings when touched and a moment later another jelly, or the same one, answers with the same note and a ripple, and the pet claps. Nothing is a miss or a hint: every tap is answered within 0.6 s by the same jelly, a jelly wiggles after six quiet seconds, and eight taps finish the round.
