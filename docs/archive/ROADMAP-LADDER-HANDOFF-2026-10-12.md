## Short ladders as written


24 games top out at four or five levels and 23 give the school band only one or two (33 school-reaching games in all). The [short-ladder audit](IDEAS-SHORT-LADDER-AUDITS-2026-10-09.md#short-ladder-audit-2026-10-09) read every ladder and ordered the ones with a **real next decision** (something new to decide, not a bigger number, a faster pace or more to remember). Append levels, never renumber saved levels or pins. Each new level needs its `describeLevel`, scoped how-to steps, rule tests and a bot that can play it (every game has a ghost finger now, and `fingerdemo` plays each game's top level). Already built: Inchworm Measure 5–6, Picture Graph 5–7, Pet Kitchen 7–9, Pattern Train 10–12, Treasure Map 5, Critter Sort 5, Light Lab 7, Duck Pond 11, Word Monsters 8, Robot Path 11–12, Weather Wardrobe 7, Clap the Syllables 5 and Dot Link 6 ([completed work](COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md#dot-link-level-6-exactly-five-2026-10-10)).

1. **Memory Match 10–11, "Same, said differently"** — **built 2026-10-10**: number words one through ten to numerals, then three fraction pictures to their names on six cards. Each picture divides one square into equal parts; "three quarters" wraps across two lines on the 140-unit card. Level 12 (clock faces) still needs a small clock face and a legibility look; support, content and the remaining ladder are planned in the [deep dive](../deep-dives/memory-match.md#build-slices-in-order).
5. **Bouncy Launch** — **built 2026-10-10**: school level 6 shows a fixed pull, asks for a cloud prediction, then releases it; different guesses stay unscored. The pure pull-to-cloud mapping is tested as deterministic. [Build note](COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md).
6. **Rainbow Fingers 7, "Paint the pumpkin"** — **built 2026-10-10**: recall that the pumpkin is orange, then mix red and yellow before painting it. **It cannot be finished live (read from the code, not played)**: `pick()` pours into the bowl only in the `'mix'` mode, so the recall-and-mix level never makes orange (the [deep dive](../deep-dives/rainbow-fingers.md#the-game-today), gap 1; the fix is its first build slice).

**Then, from the [second ladder round](IDEAS-SHORT-LADDER-AUDITS-2026-10-09.md#round-two-the-longer-ladders-2026-10-10) (2026-10-10, proposals).** That round covers the longer school ladders the audit skipped. Each item is one level appended above the game's current top, and the detail page says what its rule tests assert.

7. **Market Stall 7, Fewest coins** — **built 2026-10-10**: pay prices from 4 to 10 with as few 1-, 2- and 5-shell coins as possible. An exact longer payment stays on the mat for a swap; only repeating that nudge is a miss.
8. **Clock Tower 7, Five-minute times** — **built 2026-10-10**: set times like 3:25 by counting by fives; small helper numbers ring the rim. The twelve snap marks are about 86 units apart at the long hand's tip (105 at the rim), a drag that snaps by angle rather than a tap target; not checked on an iPad. Support/content and interval slices are planned in the [deep dive](../deep-dives/clock-tower.md#build-slices-in-order).
9. **Frog Hop 7, Hop to ten first** — **built 2026-10-10**: split a sum that crosses ten at 10 (8 + 2 + 3), choosing the bridge on number cards before landing the rest.
10. **Seesaw Balance 10, Two steps** — **built 2026-10-10**: take the same things off both sides, then share the blocks left across two identical boxes.
11. **Robot Path 11, Where will it stop?** — **built 2026-10-10**: read a shown step or counted program, choose its stopping square, and watch the robot; a prediction is a free experiment. [Build note](COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md#robot-path-11-where-will-it-stop-2026-10-10).
12. **Robot Path 12, Fix the program** — **built 2026-10-10**: watch a short counted program for free, select its wrong slot, and add the missing step to reach the star. Three authored boards each have exactly one single-slot fix. [Build note](COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md#robot-path-12-fix-the-program-2026-10-10).
14. **Light Lab 7, Where will it shine?** — **built 2026-10-10**: three unscored predictions among reachable beam endpoints, then one ordinary plan puzzle.
17. **Mail Carrier 8, Which way is shorter?** — **built 2026-10-10**: find the neighbor's house, then compare the two routes marked with stepping stones and walk the shorter one ([build note](COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md#mail-carrier-level-8-which-way-is-shorter-2026-10-10)).

Feelings Faces 8 and Weather Wardrobe 7 (*the weather changes*) are built ([Weather Wardrobe notes](COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md#weather-wardrobe-level-7-the-weather-changes-2026-10-10)). The round's costlier steps are Photo Safari's two-clue scenes, elapsed time, two different unknowns and Garden Rows boxes; its "leave" verdicts are on the same page.

Costlier, after these: Sink or Float's *make it float* is the strongest. **Ramp Race**'s "predict where the car stops" must have its finish rule and the half-marks the model produces (such as 1.5 × 3) worked out before it is built, and a recorded-trials table is a new screen. Fourteen more need a new interaction, art or a stated model first, and twelve should stay as they are; the older-band ceilings of Opposites, Goodnight Room and Stop and Go wait for a reason to grow them ([audit](IDEAS-SHORT-LADDER-AUDITS-2026-10-09.md#short-ladder-audit-2026-10-09)); Goodnight Room's reason is the 2026-10-10 quantity direction, and its [deep dive](../deep-dives/goodnight-room.md) plans levels 5–10 and a pre-K window.
