## Standing rules

The developer wrote (2026-10-23): "There are a bunch of 'waiting on developer' questions. Can you or fable go through to make recommendations and go with them, there are a lot and doesn't really make sense for me."
So: decide every row by the best fit with AGENTS.md, docs/rework/README.md and the recorded leans. Keep a row for the developer ONLY if it needs: their money or accounts, privacy or the child's personal data, a real iPad/device or a person watching the child play, a family member's review (e.g. a language reviewer), or an irreversible step.
S1 Content review (answers every "name a reviewer / educator read / developer approves the table" gate): the building session checks every factual line, word list, card table and spoken claim against reputable sources (museums, universities, government science/health agencies, established curricula) cited in the study or definition, lists those sources in the definition, and records the check in its VERIFICATION entry. No outside reviewer or developer approval is required before build or merge. "Merges only after the developer approves X" becomes "merges after the session's source check".
   Exceptions, deferred (not waiting): practical health, body-safety, food-safety, toileting, dental or disability-specific instructions and real-care family cards are not drafted for now (pretend/screen play proceeds); Indigenous and living cultures, religion and colonization are not drafted (sessions record questions only). Translations need a fluent family reviewer and stay with the developer.
S2 Bands: no new middle band now. `school` stays 6–12; older content sits at the top of `school` levels with an "about 10 and up" note and no gate; 13+ puzzles go to `grownup` (Lantern Lake). Parents: Lantern Lake for themselves, the family table for play together, the plain-HTML grown-ups' page for cards. Wonder Woods grows first; revisit a tween band only when the school top is full.
S3 Hello song (RW07): adopt the lean, Song Maker level 2 as a stated, test-guarded exception.
S4 Physics: adopt the lean. Rule-model physics first; for ropes/blocks/drawn lines/circle piles, small hand-written deterministic solvers in each game's logic.ts (testable in Node, same result in Safari), no physics library for now; Block Topple starts with the simplified tipping rule.
S5 Second seat: adopt the lean: anonymous and uncredited, only seat 1's role read by difficulty.ts, a role-swapped round counts as supported completion.
S6 Voice: allow spoken clips generated at build time from voice-script.ts by a repository script (never hand-imported or recorded), cached offline, device speech as fallback; engine Kokoro run locally ($0, Apache-2.0, no account). The listening judgement is a device check, not a blocker.
S7 Title: keep "Puddle Island"; no public rename now (a rename would need legal clearance).
S8 New-game identity, land, band and accounting questions: adopt the definition's or study's stated lean/default. Competition between players: friendly and cooperative-first per the lean; no scores that rank the child.
S9 Anything gated "after seen on an iPad" that is not itself a device judgement: adopt the lean now and add the device check to DEVICE-CHECKS (the coordinator does that from your report).
S10 Keep for the developer (with a one-line recommended answer): RW08 cutover deleting classic/ (after they play slices 1–2; recommend yes); the first language and its family reviewer (recommend Portuguese first, reviewed by the family member who speaks it); anything spending money, creating accounts, or touching the child's data.
