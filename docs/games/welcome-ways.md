## Design notes

*Fictional toy engineering, in Tinker Lab.* Drag a wooden part into a rise/door socket, or tap a part then a socket, and tap Try. The same entrance carries every pictured friend, in order: level 1 one ramp for Roly; level 2 step or ramp; level 3 Bunny hops over either but Roly rolls only over a ramp; level 4 two ramps admit Roly and a wide door also admits Bear. Toddler 1–2, preschool 1–3, pre-K 2–4, school 3–7. Levels 5–7 (school only): 5 *Turn at the pond*, a switchback board (lower ramp, a small or big landing corner, upper ramp, the fixed wide door) where Roly rolls and Bear needs the big landing (accepted: gentle, big, gentle); 6 *A rail and a perch*, two rises and a narrow door with near and far side strips taking a rail or a perch, where Teeter (a small pig) needs a rail on two ramps in a row and Duck needs a perch (two accepted designs); 7 *Fix it for Roly*, which starts as step, step with a near perch so Teeter and Duck already come in, and Roly needs the ramps; keeping the perch earns the praise line. When distances tie, the suggested repair prefers the accepted design that meets the stopped friend's need at its socket (level 7 with no sides: Duck stops at the near socket and the hint is a perch, not a rail). A failed Try pauses at the first blocking part with a spoken reason and a pulse; the next Try starts everybody outside again. Every building action and Try is free (misses always zero). Two identical stops since the last edit, or Show me, suggest a part and destination from the nearest accepted design; wrong installed pieces can be replaced. Each distinct socket/part repair counts once, including across Undo. Removing a selected part restores a gap; Undo restores the last building edit. Cancelled drags and invalid drops preserve the board. One successful parade finishes once through the shell, including after help. The ghost finger demonstrates the step/Roly and narrow-door/Bear revisions, then the pond landing, rail and perch and Roly repairs at levels 5–7. No saved construction or later parts, human disability representation or practical building advice. [Rules and scope](../definitions/welcome-ways.md).
