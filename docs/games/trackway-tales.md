## Design notes

*After the AMNH .* Who Walked Here?* classroom activity and NPS trackway pages; [definition](../definitions/trackway-tales.md).* A strip of dried mud by the shore holds a line of footprints left by one invented gray creature nobody saw. Above it is a clear practice strip, and two or three movement cards below. A round Play button on a card replays that movement: a gray mud walker steps along the practice strip leaving ghost prints and soft thuds (drag the strip to scrub back and forth). Tapping a card puts it on the study board; Check slides its prints onto the mud and lights the columns green in the movement's order until the first mismatch flashes orange with a spoken kind (an extra mark, a missing mark, a toe the other way). Level 1 *Walk or hop* (4 columns, 2 cards). Level 2 *Which way* (6 columns, 3 cards): the magnifier opens on a mark, and tapping a mud mark moves it and says which way the toes point. Level 3 *The puddle* (8 columns, 3 cards plus Can't tell): a puddle covers columns 3 and 4, so two walkers can leave the same marks. In L3-pause (walk, walk-stand-walk) both fit and Can't tell is right; in L3-switch (walk-then-hop) one card is wrong and Can't tell is a miss. Each level's first round per session is authored (L1-walk with hop then walk; L2-left with column 5 magnified; L3-pause).

Misses: Check with a card whose prints do not fit, and a wrong Can't tell. Free: replays, scrubs, the magnifier, Check with an empty slot, and at level 3 Checking a fitting card while another also fits (the teaching moment). The second miss glows the right card, rings the telling column on both strips, replays the answer slowly and counts one hint; "Show a hint" does the same once per round. Marks are stored by screen half (a left foot is on the upper half walking right, the lower half walking left), so walk and walk-back differ only by the toes; a single-gait movement carries on to the strip's end (the four-column picture is only the start). Nothing is saved, filed in the journal or shown as real; no couch play. Layout: one centred layout serves every view (portrait is 1024x1365 logical), cards 140x240, Play 104 round. Home land: Story Grove (12 games), not the definition's Bubble Beach, whose scenery fails at ten games (the [WAITING-ON-DEVELOPER](../WAITING-ON-DEVELOPER.md) row keeps the choice open). The ghost-finger bot plays all three levels. Co-play: one replays and chooses, the other says which footprints look alike; at the puddle, say what the mud cannot tell. Off-screen: press two hands or shoes into clay or sand walking and hopping, and ask which one made a given line.
