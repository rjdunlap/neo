## Design notes

*Slices N2 and N3 of the [nature-and-sustainability study](../deep-dives/subjects/nature-sustainability.md), [definition](../definitions/second-chance-shed.md).* Used things arrive one at a time on a cart and she drags each to the place that gives it a second life: the worm bin (banana peel, apple core, carrot top, dry leaf), the reuse shelf (sturdy box, flowerpot tub, cloth bag), the mending table (teddy button, cart wheel, kite tail) or the Remake machine (squashed can, crayon stubs, bottle caps). The place plays its consequence: a sprout in the pot, Bunny naps in the box, a mended toy, a Remake chug that drops a medal, crayon or button. Each place has a pictured key, so the choice never needs reading. The island's rule is that condition decides (the box is always drawn whole); the Remake machine is the island's invention and no line claims a real plant does this. Levels: **1** *Feed the worms* (worm bin only; counts nothing, so a drop in the empty yard is free), **2** *Two places* (adds the reuse shelf), **3** *Mend it* (adds the mending table), **4** *Remake* (all four). Bands: toddler 1–2, preschool 2–3, pre-K 3–4. Mistakes and help: at levels 2–4 a drop on the wrong place is a miss and that place says its reason in its own voice; two misses on an item glow its place and count one hint (once per item, also across Start again); the pause sheet's "Show a hint" glows the item's place and counts once per item, and asked again carries the item to its place (a tween by the game, not the drawn ghost hand). Start again replays the same seeded round and keeps its misses and hints. Nothing is saved or filed. Ghost finger demonstrates drags. **Levels 5–6 (school {4–6}, slice N3, built 2026-10-31, [definition](../definitions/second-chance-shed-5-6.md)).** The whole cart is laid out at once with a bell, and things move freely among the cart, the four places and friends with picture bubbles (pencils: a cat; a bed: Bunny; a seedling: a duck). Level 5 *Someone needs it* has one friend; level 6 *Two friends* has two and the two authored cases (box/bed, tub/seedling), the pencils friend first in about two rounds in three, where the tempting greedy plan fails. A tin with a lid appears only at 5–6: shelf, Remake (a medal) or the pencils friend; on the shelf it makes buttons. Only a failed ring is a miss: ringing the same failed plan again counts nothing, a ring with things still on the cart is free, the second miss lights the glow (one hint per round), and Show a hint then Show me one (one hint each) moves one thing toward the nearest accepted plan. The sticker comes once. Deviations from the definition: every thing in a place stays grabbable (fanned 22 units apart, newest on top) so help never stalls on a buried thing, and the finale chimes instead of a spoken "the plan works", then speaks each place's made line, each friend's filled line and the closing line. Bands gain school; skills gain planning.
