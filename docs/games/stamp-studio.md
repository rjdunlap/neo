**Starts as:** pat one huge star (eight pats finish the picture), or press one or two seeded stamp kinds onto a picture (a round is seeded onto the Sea's pale water and sand, the Farm's field, sky and pond, the Garden's grass and fence, Space's night sky, the Picnic's checked cloth on grass, or the Town's street and skyline); arrow nudges after five, full paper finishes itself; from level 3 a music note plays the picture back left to right; levels 8–9 press a leaf, a duck foot or a cat paw into soft mud, each leaving its own print; level 10 follows a glowing spot, five taps, a stamp at each; level 11 asks for three animal stamps in turn before free stamping; level 12 (preschool, pre-K and school) asks on the Farm for a stamp up in the sky, on the grass and in the water; level 13 (pre-K and school) prints two animals on plain paper and asks for a stamp next to one, between them, and above or below the other

**Grows into:** three seeded kinds and a starting color at level 3; from level 4 a sets button switches freely between Plain, Sea, Farm, Garden, Space, Picnic and Town (with their own backdrops and six-stamp sets, mixing stamps on the last-chosen backdrop); choose from seven colors, arrange or bin a selected stamp, change size (small, middle, big) and orientation, tell a picture story; any creation can finish, with matching button/stamp colors and the kept picture frozen at Finish

## Design notes

*After Mario Paint and Kid Pix.* Lap level 7, *Pat the paper*, is the first lap level: one huge soft star stamp (170 units) lands under each pat with its sound, pats close together are all kept, a pat on the teal around the paper presses a star at the nearest place on it, and the eighth pat finishes the picture by itself (no miss, no hint; the star button, green arrow and back arrow are not shown, so nothing ends or undoes it early). Two simple lap modes offer one or two distinct seeded kinds from star, flower, fish and cat. Levels 1–3 start in a seeded existing color; level 3 offers three distinct seeded kinds. Later modes offer colors, moving stamps, two sizes, quarter-turn rotation and spoken garden/story invitations. Any picture is valid; the green arrow can finish after the first stamp. Buttons show the current stamping color; each stamp kind is named once per round. Undo removes the latest stamp; at levels 3+ the bin removes the stamp picked up, releasing its drag and selection. At levels 1–2 the arrow wiggles after five stamps, and the 24th stamp finishes automatically. At levels 3+ a full paper stays editable, with one spoken reminder. Coordinates stay normalized when resizing. The green arrow freezes a copy of the picture immediately; late Undo, transform, color and stamp choices cannot edit it during the celebration, and a held stamp settles at its last accepted position. That finished picture can be kept on the treehouse picture board. At levels 3–6 (not 1, 2 or 7), once a stamp is down, a music-note button left of the paper plays the picture: stamps sound left to right (ties top first) on the pentatonic scale, higher on the paper higher in pitch (C4–E5), each popping with a yellow scan bar, spaced up to 0.4 s apart and never more than 5.5 s in all. Pressing again restarts, a stamp removed mid-play is skipped, and finishing stops it. Nothing is scored, the saved picture is unchanged and the how-to ghost finger does not press it.

**Levels 8–9, Trace Prints** (`TracePrints` in `trace.ts`; added to lap `{1–8}` and toddler `{2–9}`; other bands unchanged). The paper is a soft mud tray and a maker leaves its own fresh print; nothing is checked, so there are no misses and no hints, and nothing is kept for the treehouse. Level 8, *Leaf prints* (lap): every pat presses one big green leaf (seeded tilt up to 20 degrees either way) and the leaf lifts away leaving its print; the eighth pat finishes the picture by itself. Level 9, *Two feet* (toddler): two 120-across buttons, a duck foot (chosen first, quack) and a cat paw (meow); choosing says the name once, each print has its own shape, the pair line is spoken once, and the tenth print finishes by itself. The green arrow works after the first print and the back arrow smooths the latest print. A print always stays wholly on the mud (the margin is the larger of half its width and its farthest reach, plus 5, so a tilted leaf stays in). No fossil, long-ago or stone wording. Off-screen: after a walk in wet sand, snow or a puddle, look back at your footprints together and find whose are whose.

**Level 10, Follow the sparkle (lap and toddler; lap order `[7, 8, 1, 10, 2]`, toddler `[9, 10, 2, 3]`, `entryLevels` 7–10).** A soft glowing spot (170 units across, breathing between about 162 and 178, never under 160) sits on the paper. A tap in the glow stamps at its centre; a tap anywhere else, on the paper or the teal around it, stamps where it fell (off the paper, at the nearest place on it); the glow then moves to the next spot either way, so nothing is a miss and nothing is a hint. The five spots are the paper's four corners and its middle, each nudged a little and shuffled by the seeded RNG (`sparkleSpots`, `SPARKLE`, `sparklePixel`, `sparkleTap`), at least 200 units apart on the smallest paper sideways and upright, wholly on the paper and never used twice. The fifth stamp finishes the picture through the usual wake-up, and it can be kept as usual. No green arrow, back arrow or stamp button is shown (as at level 7); the stamp kind and color are seeded per round like levels 1–3, and the stamps are not counted aloud. Toddler has no level 1, so 10 sits directly before 2 and Two feet (9) stays toddler's first level; no band's starting level changed. The ghost finger taps the glow's centre five times. The co-play tip no longer says the green arrow finishes any creation (levels 7 and 10 show no arrow); it ends "Any picture is a finished picture."

**Seven colors and three sizes (levels 3–6).** The colors run red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink; discs are 108 units across, in one row sideways and two rows on an upright phone (`colorSpots`). The size button cycles middle, big (1.5), small (0.7); an older saved 1.3 counts as big. Yellow takes a darker gold outline so it reads on the cream paper. Sizes are not spoken yet.

**Sounds, counting and the wake-up (levels 1-7, 11).** Each kind has its own sound (Pat the paper's stars ring too): the star a bell and the flower a marimba (each climbs five pentatonic steps per press, then starts round), the fish a splash, the cat a meow, the duck a quack. Levels 1-3 count the stamps on the paper aloud, one to ten, each number said once a round (after an undo a new press is silent); the count uses device speech, so a quick tap after choosing a stamp can cut its name. The green arrow wakes the stamps one by one in the order they were placed, 0.3 s apart and within 2.5 s in all, each bouncing with its sound; on a crowded paper the meows, splashes and quacks sound at most one per half second, though every stamp still bounces. Then the round finishes with the sparkle.

**Scenes and the Sea set (levels 1–3).** `SETS` and `SCENES` in `logic.ts`; `STAMP_KINDS` gains `whale`, `crab`, `starfish`, `shell` and `boat` appended after the sticker kinds, so saved sticker art cannot change. The Sea set is those five plus the fish; each has a name (`'A whale'`, all taking "A" so `animalWord` still works), a sound (whale and crab a splash, starfish a bell, shell and boat a marimba) and code-drawn art in `art.ts`. Levels 1–3 seed each round onto one of the sets (`SEEDED_SETS`: Sea, Farm, Garden, Space, Picnic or Town; Plain gives way there once every classic stamp has a set); the paper then draws `sceneArt` under the stamps (`eventMode = 'none'`, masked inside the paper's edge). A kept picture carries an optional `scene` (`StampPictureCreation.scene`, in `SCENE_IDS`); `cleanPicture` drops an unknown scene but keeps the picture, and the treehouse picture board draws the same backdrop. Nothing is scored.

**Farm set and the sets button (levels 4–6, dive slice 6).** `STAMP_KINDS` appends `cow`, `pig`, `hen` and `barn`, joining `cat` and `duck` in `SETS.farm` (`SCENE_IDS` appends `'farm'`). Each Farm stamp has code-drawn art (`cow`, `pig`, `hen` with seamless outlines, `barn` with gambrel roof and X-doors) and its own sound (`moo`, `oink`, `cluck`, and `marimba` for the barn). `sceneArt('farm')` paints a light sky with clouds, a green meadow with grass tufts, and a pond. At levels 4–6, a round sets button (showing the current set's first stamp) opens a tray of available sets (`Plain`, `Sea`, `Farm`, `Garden`, `Space`): switching sets changes the bottom stamp palette and paper backdrop while keeping every stamp already placed, so pictures may mix sets freely and save the last-chosen backdrop. Six-stamp sets size their choice buttons to 40 sideways / 36 upright (still at least 100 units across) so all six sit without overlapping.

**Garden and Space sets (dive slice 7).** `STAMP_KINDS` appends `butterfly`, `ladybug`, `snail`, `sunflower`, `tree`, `moon`, `rocket`, `planet`, `comet` and `ufo`; `SETS.garden` is `flower` with the first five on `SCENES.garden` (sky, sun, a picket fence along the horizon, grass) and `SETS.space` is `star` with the rest on `SCENES.space` (a dark night sky with small stars over a curve of ground); `SCENE_IDS` appends both. Each new stamp has code-drawn art, a name ("A UFO") and a sound (a pitched marimba or bell step each, climbing per press like the older ones). The tray grows to five (Plain, Sea, Farm, Garden, Space). Every classic stamp now has a set, so levels 1–3 seed only from the four sets; Plain stays in the tray as blank paper with the classic four, and Follow the sparkle (level 10) keeps drawing from it.

**Picnic and Town sets (dive slice 8).** `STAMP_KINDS` appends `apple`, `cupcake`, `basket`, `kite`, `balloon`, `ant`, `house`, `car`, `bus`, `bike`, `lamp` and `cloud`; `SETS.picnic` and `SETS.town` hold six new stamps each, on `SCENES.picnic` (sky, sun, grass and a red-and-white checked cloth) and `SCENES.town` (a skyline of pale blocks with lit windows over a pavement and a road with a dashed line); `SCENE_IDS` appends both. Names with a vowel take "An" ("An apple", "An ant"), so `animalWord` strips either article instead of two letters. Each stamp has code-drawn art, a name and a pitched marimba or bell step. The tray grows to seven (Plain, Sea, Farm, Garden, Space, Picnic, Town): upright it wraps to three rows of three, and sideways a row that would run into the right-hand buttons wraps onto a second row below (`stampLayout` reads `setsCount`). Levels 1–3 now seed from six sets.

**Level 11, Stamp a duck! (toddler; `order: [9, 10, 2, 11, 3]`, `entryLevels` 7–11).** Adds `'duck'` (`stampArt` with seamless outline and orange bill, `'A duck'`, `quack` sound) to `STAMP_KINDS` (`STICKER_KINDS` keeps the original four so saved stickers 0–99 stay pinned). Level 11 offers the three animal stamps (`cat`, `fish`, `duck`) in a seeded starting color and order (`roundPlan`); `animalAsks` picks three distinct asks starting away from the preselected kind (`asks[0] !== selected`). Each ask uses `ctx.instruct('stamp.ask', { animal })` ("Stamp a duck!"); matching advances the ask and enters free play after three (`ctx.instruct('stamp.free')`, revealing the green finish arrow). A wrong stamp still prints and sounds, triggers `sfx.boing()` and `ctx.say('stamp.wrong', { got, want })` ("That's a cat! Find the duck."), and counts via `missOnRequest` (`coach.ts`); after two wrong presses on an ask (`ASK_HINT_AFTER`), `hinted` increments hints and `choiceGlow` (`eventMode = 'none'`) pulses behind the asked stamp button until it is stamped. Pressing at the 24-stamp limit while asks remain replaces the oldest stamp so asks cannot lock up. The finished picture can be kept on the treehouse picture board.

**Level 12, Where does it go? (preschool `order: [3, 4, 5, 6, 12]`, pre-K `[4, 5, 6, 12]`, school `[5, 6, 12]`; dive slice 10).** The deep dive's invitation level 7, at the next free stable ID. The round is on the Farm backdrop (kept with the picture) with seven colors, moving and the bin, but no sets button (a set would change the scene the words name) and no transform or play buttons. `placeInvites` puts the three place words (`sky`, `grass`, `water`) in a seeded order, each with a suggested stamp from `PLACE_KINDS` (sky: cloud, kite or balloon; grass: cow, pig or hen; water: duck or fish), and those three stamps are the round's choices. The voice says "Put the cow on the grass!" (`stamp.places`, `ctx.instruct`). `meetsWord` uses only the stamp's centre: above the horizon is sky; the pond (`POND`, shared with the backdrop art) grown by `POND_REACH` 40 units is water; the rest below the horizon is grass, so every point is exactly one place. A pressed or dragged stamp that meets the open word sparkles and moves on to the next; one anywhere else is part of the picture, with no boing or miss, and the invitation is said again once (`stamp.elsewhere`). After 12 quiet seconds the named place glows softly, which is not a hint. The green arrow works from the first stamp. On finishing, each invitation not yet met counts as one hint (decision 2), never spoken; after all three, `stamp.placesdone` invites free stamping. The dive's *on the barn* is left out because the Farm backdrop draws no barn (barn is only a stamp), and the Farm set has no sky stamp, so the sky's suggestions come from the Town and Picnic sets. The pet telling the picture back is dive slice 9 and is not built here. The ghost finger chooses the suggested stamp and presses at `placeTarget`, then finishes.

**Level 13, Next to, between, above (pre-K `order: [4, 5, 6, 12, 13]`, school `[5, 6, 12, 13]`; dive slice 11).** The deep dive's invitation level 8, at the next free stable ID; preschool stays at 12, since a three-year-old's "between" is a person check. Two animals (`relationAnchors`: two different kinds from `ANCHOR_KINDS`, cat, fish, cow, duck, pig or hen) are printed on plain paper in orange and blue with a soft shadow, 170 units either side of the paper's centre and nudged up or down by up to 30, so they are 340 or more apart on every paper (offsets in units, not fractions, keep the rule the same on a phone). They take no touch: a press on one stamps on the paper there, and undo, the bin and dragging never reach them. `relationInvites` gives three relations in a seeded order: next to one animal, between both, and above or below the other, each with a different suggested stamp from `RELATION_KINDS` (star, flower, kite, balloon, butterfly, cloud), and those three are the round's choices. The voice reuses level 12's lines: "Put the star next to the cat!" (`stamp.places`, the relation said by `relationPhrase`) and `stamp.elsewhere`; after all three, `stamp.relationsdone`. `meetsRelation` uses only the stamp's centre: within `ON_ANCHOR` 60 units of either animal is on it and meets nothing; *next to* is within 160 of that animal; *between* projects onto the segment joining them and is at most 70 from it; *above* and *below* are at least 60 higher or lower and at most 90 to either side. The relations overlap by design (the middle is next to both animals and between them); only the open invitation counts. Everything else is level 12's path, now shared: no boing or miss, the open relation's zone glows after 12 quiet seconds (not a hint), the arrow works from the first stamp, and each relation not met counts as one hint. The kept picture includes the two animals first, so her own stamps stop at 22 (`LIMIT` less the animals) and the picture never passes `PICTURE_MAX`; the animals wake up with her stamps. *Behind* and *in front of* stay out until stamps have layers. The ghost finger chooses the suggested stamp and presses at `relationTarget`, then finishes.
