**Starts as:** a hidden picture word. Four pictured words (three letters at level 1, four at level 2) sit in a row and one is hiding; she taps letters from a small tray to try a word and every try comes back marked letter by letter with Secret Code's shapes (a green check in the right spot, a yellow dot elsewhere in the word, a gray cross not in it). The marks are the evidence for the next try.

**Grows into:** level 3 (grown-up), a hidden five-letter word from about 400 everyday answers, tried with any word on a guess list of several thousand, on an on-screen keyboard or a hardware one; a word not on the list is turned down free. Unlimited tries, no daily word, no streak, and the number of tries is never counted.

## Design notes

- **The one miss** is ignoring a mark still on screen: a letter the marks ruled out used again, a dot or cross spot used again for the same letter, or a visible check dropped. The try still plays and she gets its marks; it counts one miss (never more than one a try), the boing sounds and a spoken hint names the letter. Tries that have scrolled off cost nothing. After two misses the letters the marks rule out dim and the light bulb glows. The bulb is the hint: one letter of the hidden word in its place (the first spot no try has checked), counted once a press.
- **School sets** are four distinct words from the level's pool, one hidden, redrawn until the tray holds at most 12 letters and a solver that only tries candidates (the first still allowed) needs at most three tries. A test enumerates every combination, and finds that a candidate-only solver can need four on an unlucky set, which is why the generator redraws rather than the rule promising three for all.
- **Words** are plain lists in `words.ts`. The answers are hand-chosen everyday five-letter words; the guess list adds the five-letter entries of the system's `/usr/share/dict/web2` (Webster's Second, public domain), regenerated by `node scripts/word-code-guesses.mjs`, less a block list. The definition named the ENABLE list; it was not available offline when this was built, so this is a stand-in, and the list can be swapped without touching the rules. The reference solver (`solverTries`: a recorded opener, then the alphabetically first answer still consistent) reaches every answer within six tries, and a test keeps it so.
- **Layout** is pure (`layout.ts`) with a per-level test over the landscape view and the upright phone views. School keeps 100-unit targets; the grown-up keyboard is seven columns of 96 × 88 (44.7 CSS pixels at 390 × 844), hung 176 up from the bottom edge on an upright phone so the guide's corner is clear.
- **Reuse:** the mark badge is `markArt`, moved out of Secret Code's `index.ts` into `secret-code/marks.ts` so both games draw the same check, dot and cross.
