## Design notes

*After route-following walks and spatial-word books; [definition](../definitions/adventure-walk.md).* The companion walks to a friend's picnic past three landmarks (a brook, reeds, a log, a boulder or a puddle, three per round from a seeded shuffle), and at each one two glowing ways past are drawn: over or under, through, across, around. Screen-only: the companion does all the walking and nothing asks the person to move. Level 1 lets her tap either way (free, so a round counts 0 misses and 0 hints); levels 2 and 3 follow the friend's call (a duck, cow or pig), one call per stop at level 2 and two at once at level 3 with the friend's badge to replay them (replays are free). Any wrong tap counts a miss and the walker turns back with the reason; two misses at one stop light the asked way and count one hint (at most one per stop), and Show a hint (the pause sheet) does the same. Start again restarts the walk. Home is Barnyard (Rainbow Meadow and Story Grove failed their scene decor tests); region Story Grove. Known gaps: no cross-round landmark bag (all five landmarks appear across seeds), and the device speech for "across" and "around" is unchecked.

**Levels.** Level 1 *Pick a way* (toddler, preschool): either way is right. Level 2 *Come this way* (preschool, pre-K): follow one spoken way. Level 3 *Two at once* (pre-K, school): follow two calls, then the third stop's. Level 4 *The way home* (pre-K, school 2–4/3–4) is built (2026-11-17): the walk out is level 1 exactly (nothing counted), then the picnic shrinks into a map (home door, three landmark tiles in route order, the picnic, one dotted path; shapes `sweep`, `zigzag`, `hook` from the round's seed) and she taps the landmark the walk home comes to next, three times. A tile that is not next is a miss ("Not yet! Follow the path from the picnic.", the companion steps once toward the right one); two misses on a leg or Show a hint light the next tile and say "We get to {place} first." (one hint per leg); a tile already passed only wiggles. The companion goes back past each landmark the way it came and walks to its door itself; footprints on each tile show the way walked. School covers 3–4 until level 5 is built. Level 5 *Which trail?* (school) is [defined](../definitions/adventure-walk.md#levels-45-aw3), not built: she chooses among three drawn trails that differ only in their ways.
