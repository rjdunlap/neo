## Design notes

*After toy gardens and Viva Piñata.* Tap the soil and a seed grows into a smiling flower that sings when tapped. Level 2 adds the cloud: plant, then make it rain. Later levels plant the color asked for (a wrong packet shakes, as a miss), then exactly 2–5 seeds before the rain, then two colors with a number of each. Pre-K level 6 asks for one more of a color than the flower sign shows; level 7 shows a two-by-three array and asks for those six flowers; pre-K order is 6, 7, 9, 10, 11. Level 9, Rows of colors, shows a sign of two rows by three (top and bottom rows differ): choose a packet (free; it lifts with a ring), then tap beds; a seed of another color replaces a planted one, the same color takes it out; the rain judges by position, and a wrong layout is a miss with "The top row needs red." Level 10, Columns of colors, asks for three columns in two or three colors the same way. Level 11, Same in every row, shows flowers, empty pots and a numeral per row (1 or 2 each): plant that many in each row in any beds, every placement is accepted, and the finish says "2 and 2 makes 4". At levels 9–11 two misses glow the wrong or empty beds (at 11 the short row's free beds or the over row's extra seeds). Level 8, Grow it up, is the lap entry (lap order 8, 1, 2): one big bed, and any touch anywhere grows the flower one stage (seed, sprout, leaves, bud, open); a touch on the open flower puts it in a vase and plants the next seed, and three flowers end the round with no misses or hints (a touch mid-growth snaps the previous stage). Flowers come in five kinds (blossom, daisy, tulip, sunflower, bell) that vary per request; packets, sign and seed tags show the kind, but only color and number are judged, and the free levels pick a random kind per bed. A picture sign shows what was asked and each planted seed has a marker in its color. Tapping a planted seed takes it out and the new count is said aloud; tapping a packet when every bed is planted says so (not a miss) and points to the cloud or a seed to take out. At level 1 each bed grows on its own, so a tap on another empty bed plants at once and the round ends when no bed is still growing. Two misses make the sign and the beds still to plant glow. A seed grows in stages (stem, two leaves unfolding, a bud swelling, then the smiling head opening), and rain darkens a bed's soil, which dries back slowly once the flower is up; this is presentation only. For co-play the tip names the weather seat: one plants, one taps the cloud and says "Rain, rain... it grows!"
