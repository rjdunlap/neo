## Design notes

*Levels 1–7 · [definition](../definitions/caravan-days.md) · china-india-silk-road slices CS2, CS3 and CS4.* She helps a camel on a long road: kneel and load it, count how far it can walk on its water, and plan a road of stops, the way caravans once did. Toddler plays level 1, preschool levels 1–2, pre-K 2–3, school 3–7. Nothing is kept; the sticker is the round's only reward. Music is `STYLES.hub`.

**1 Kneel and load:** she taps the camel so it kneels, taps each bale to load it (2 at toddler, 3–4 from preschool; a bale tapped while it is on the camel hops off again, free), then taps the camel again: it stands with bells, walks to the oasis and drinks. Nothing is counted (0 misses, 0 hints); after eight idle seconds the next useful thing glows (uncounted), and a bale tap while the camel stands wobbles the bale and says to tap the camel first. **2 Can we reach it?:** two roads per round, each a fork with 1–2 suns on one branch and 3–4 on the other (the side is seeded) and a waterskin of two drops. Tapping a sun, a road or the skin counts aloud (free). Tapping the near oasis walks the camel sun by sun, a drop per day, then the long drink and the skin refills. **3 Day by day across the map:** two maps per round; home at the left, the city at the right, two branches of 2–3 oases each, legs of 1–2 suns except exactly one 3-sun leg on one branch. She taps stops in order to plant numbered flags (the next stop, or one oasis beyond it on the same branch; a flagged stop takes its flag and the later ones out, free; a stop the road does not go straight to wobbles, free). Go is live only once the city is flagged (otherwise it says to plan to the city, free). Go walks the plan leg by leg, the skin refilling at each flag; the city's gate opens, bells, the long drink. **4 Swap at the stop (school):** a one-way road home → oasis 1 → oasis 2 → city; every stop shows a want bubble from the start, three different wants per road, and the camel walks in to oasis 1 on entry. At each oasis the friend takes the camel's good and holds out two offer cards; she taps one (free swapping, tapping a want bubble names it), then Go walks to the next stop. Wanted: the friend takes it and the road goes on; not wanted: the friend shrugs, the pet names both goods, and the camel walks back (one miss). The city's bubble is a decoy at oasis 1. **5 Worth more far away (school):** two three-bale loads and price boards (rows like 1, 2, 4 or 1, 3, 5, totals at most 15); tapping a load shows its shells at the glowing named stop and speaks the total (free; every load looked at in a case keeps its shells shown), price boards are free looks. Go sends the load last looked at; Go before any look is free and asks her to look. The better load reverses between the near oasis and the far city across a round's cases. Shells are counters for one case; nothing accrues.

**4 Downhill all the way:** water arrives from a spring on the left to a fountain on the right. Pre-K picks one of two whole channels (one climbs by at least two in one stretch); school sets two piers with up and down arrows (road 1 starts with a hump, road 2 with a dip). **Open the spring** is the commit. **5 Over the valley (school):** drag arches across 3 and then 3–4 spans and an abutment onto each earth end (one end usually rock; the second valley may have two earth ends), then open the spring.

**Misses and help.** Level 1 has none. Level 2: the far oasis is one miss (the camel walks two days, the skin runs dry, it shakes its head and comes back); after the second miss on a road the near oasis glows, one hint per road; Help is the same glow and the same one hint (during a walk Help only speaks, uncounted). Level 3: an over-long leg is one miss (the camel walks two days, runs dry and comes back to its last flag; the earlier flags stay, and the camel ambles back if a reached flag is later taken out); after the second miss on a map the next good stop (or the flag to take out, or Go) glows, one hint per map. Counting, planting and taking out flags and refused Go taps are free. Level 4: a good the next stop does not want is one miss (the camel walks back); after the second miss on a road, or asked-for help, the right offer glows, then Go, one hint per road. Level 5: the smaller load is one miss (both sums spoken, the camel walks home and the look clears); after the second miss on a case, or asked-for help, the better load glows, one hint per case. Swapping offers, looks and an early Go are free.

**6 Mountains or sand (school, `pass`):** two maps per round, each with a pass road (3–5 one-sun legs plus a drawn snow day waited at the hut, the coat a visible cost) and a sand road (4–6 days). She taps a road band (free; it names the road, with the snow-day or coat line) and a day card 3–7 (free), then Go. Right if the card equals the chosen road's days (either road accepted). A wrong count is one miss: the camel stops on that day with the city still ahead, or arrives early, then walks home; the road and card are kept. After two misses, or asked-for help, the pet counts the chosen road's suns and snow day and the right card glows (one hint per map). The suns, inns, snow cloud and coat are scenery inside the road band, not separate looks. **7 A well every day's walk (school, `wells`):** two roads per round of 12–16 hoofprint marks on a two-row switchback (portrait also two rows), 5–7 green sites, a day stick of four marks as a free look. She taps a site to dig or fill a well (free) and taps Done; Done with no wells is free. A stretch over a day is one miss: the camel goes dry a day past the last water, the stretch glows red-gold, and it walks home. After two misses, or asked-for help, the next site glows (`wellHint`, one hint per road). A valid placement with extra wells is accepted, not a miss, and the fewest placement is ghosted; shade trees sprout on a valid walk.

**History.** Two lines are spoken once at the start of levels 2 and 3 and printed on the card: level 2 "Long ago, on the long road from the east, the stopping places were a day's walk apart, so there was water at the end of every day"; level 3 "Nobody walked the whole road. Each caravan went a few stops and handed its load on." Source-checked 2026-10-27 (UNESCO Silk Roads pages put caravanserais within a day's journey, about 30–40 km apart; National Geographic's Silk Road entry describes relay trade through middlemen, few traders going the whole way). The oases carry island names (Palm Water, Red Rock Well, Little Spring, Blue Tile City); no real place, people, date or the name "Silk Road" is on screen or spoken. Voice lines `cd.*` (counts from bounded slots One–Four, suns One–Five, too-far 3–4, too-long 3–5).
