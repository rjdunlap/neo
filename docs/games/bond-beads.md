## Design notes

*Slices 2–4 of the [chemistry study](../deep-dives/subjects/chemistry.md), [definition](../definitions/bond-beads.md).* Round beads with small hand nubs (an H bead has one, O two, C four) sit on a tray; she drags one bead onto another, or taps one then the other, to link hands, and taps a link to break it. Undo and Start again are always there. A tally strip shows what the beads are (now; at level 2 before and now). Level 1 *Hold every hand*: three trays (O1H2 first, then two of O3, O2H2, O4); the round ends itself when every hand is held and the beads form one piece. Misses and hints are always 0; a stuck tray (no free hands can reach each other) pulses a link to break and the line is said once, never counted. Level 2 *Copy the shape*: two pictured cards (the chain H–O–O–H first, then one of the C1H4 cross, the O3H2 chain or the C1O2H2 loop), she builds and presses Check. A wrong Check is a miss only if the build changed since the last one; the second miss glows the bead or link to change and counts one hint per card, and Show a hint does the same; a further wrong Check plays a supported finish with no further count. Level 3 *Take it apart, build two*: two built shapes are open on the table (H–H with H–O–O–H or H–O–O–O–H, either direction; the first puzzle is authored) and a card pictures the two shapes to make (two H–O–H, or H–O–H and H–O–O–H); she breaks and links until the tray holds exactly those two, then Checks. Start again returns to the opening shapes; a round is two puzzles (the authored first, then the 7-bead pair in a random direction). Level 4 *The same beads, two ways*: loose beads with a card of two shapes the same beads make (the 7-bead C1 O2 H4 set first, then the 6-bead C1 O3 H2 set); she builds either and Checks, the made shape fades on the card with a tick, then she rearranges the same beads into the other and Checks again, either order. Each phase is its own card for the one-hint rule; miss and hint accounting is as level 2 (linking, breaking, Undo and Start again never count), and a further wrong Check plays the supported finish. The 6–7-bead layout puts beads at equal arc length on an ellipse with searched jitter, at least 160 units apart with link midpoints 30 apart (passes landscape, portrait, phone and notch views; nothing shrinks); the two-shape card stacks its pictures in the landscape column and sets them side by side in portrait. Level 5 *Build named shapes*: an N bead (three hands, blue, triangle mark) joins the kit; three cards (water first, then ammonia and methane in seeded order) each show the shape, the pet says its name and what it is made of (`bondbeads.named.*` lines; the word "atom" is said once, at the level start), she builds and Checks as at level 2. Level 6 *Double holds*: a pair of beads may now hold two links, drawn as two parallel bars; one tap on either bar breaks one link, so a double becomes single. Three cards: oxygen, carbon dioxide, then hydrogen and oxygen from the O2 H2 tray (the single-link H–O–O–H also uses every bead and gets a neutral "not the card" line; the card needs a double); the new hint is "These two need another link." Accounting is unchanged from level 2. The layout now handles 2–7 beads. Level 7 *The same atoms, rearranged*: two puzzles, each opening with the starting pieces built (level 3's flow, doubles on) and a card picturing every product piece: hydrogen, hydrogen and oxygen built on the table → two waters (authored first), then two waters → hydrogen, hydrogen and oxygen; a before/now tally shows the same atoms before and after. A Check at the unchanged opening is not a wrong build but gets `bondbeads.named.breakfirst` ("Every hand is held. Break a link to free some hands.") and the hint names a link to break; a match says `named.rearranged` ("The atoms rearranged. Nothing added, nothing lost!"); the instruction is `named.instruct.rearrange`; accounting is as level 2. The card now pictures up to three pieces (layout test 0–3 card pictures). Levels 1–4 name no substance; levels 5–7 name only water, ammonia, methane, hydrogen, oxygen and carbon dioxide, checked 2026-11-06 against PubChem (CIDs 962, 222, 297, 783, 977, 280), and the how-to card states the model limits (flat shapes, hands are not electrons).

Bands: school 1–7. Level 8 (methane and oxygen into carbon dioxide and water, 9 beads) is cut: the 9-bead ring does not fit a landscape iPad table (see the [definition](../definitions/bond-beads.md#level-8-cut-2026-11-06)). Home Tinker Lab, region `tinker-lab`. A bead released anywhere floats home: the definition's "Tidy" (moving beads) is not built. A Kokoro clip for the title "Bond Beads!" and a ghost-finger bot that plays levels 1–7. Known gaps (2026-11-06): no iPad or device speech check; whether a school-age child reads the nubs as hands and the tally, and whether seven beads and two cards are comfortable on an iPad in portrait, is unchecked, as is whether two bars read as a double hold and are tappable, device speech of "ammonia" and "carbon dioxide", and whether a 10–13-year-old reads the tally. Co-play: look at a shape together, break it, build another and say what stayed the same. Off-screen: build something from snap-together blocks, take it apart, build something different and count the blocks each time.
