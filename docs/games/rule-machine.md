## Design notes

At levels 3–4 the next tray or prediction opens while the machine names the hung card. At level 5 the second wanted car appears while the machine reads back the first correct result. Tap the guide to hear the current request while the feedback is playing.

*pre-K level 1, school levels 2–6 · levels 1–6 · after the algebra-balance study; [definition](../definitions/rule-machine.md).* A machine with a hopper and an out chute; she watches what goes in and what comes out, then says what the next one will come out as. Art is code-drawn. The out-table is in → out columns and the game never draws or says an equal sign. After every prediction the right answer rings green beside her pick, right or wrong.
