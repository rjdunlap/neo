## Design notes

*Levels 1–3 (level 4 not built) · [definition](../definitions/timeline-trail.md) · time-and-change slice T2.* The pet's clothesline runs between a long-ago post (left, a faded sepia picture of the place's first stage) and a now post (right, the bright last stage). Three made-up island places have four stages each: a shore (empty shore with one rock, little dock, dock with a boat shed, dock with a boat and a lantern), a tree (sapling, young tree, big tree, big tree with a swing) and a nest (egg, hatchling, young pet with a first scarf, the pet now at the treehouse door, drawn from her companion). Places come from a shuffled bag, so all three appear before any repeats. No number, date or time word appears; stages are told by what the picture shows.

**1 Long ago or now?** (preschool, pre-K): two pictures of one place, at least one stage apart, wait in the tray. Tap the older one, or drag it to the left post (dragging the newer one to the right post also counts); the other flies to the other post. **2 Hang it between** (preschool, pre-K, school): two of three stages already hang; drag the loose one (first, middle or last) into the right one of three gaps. **3 Grow the line** (pre-K, school): the first stage hangs and the other three wait in a seeded order; each drop is judged against the cards already hung, so there is exactly one valid gap per card. A finished line plays as a flipbook in a big frame (each stage turns in on a rising pentatonic marimba), the pet cheers, and the round gives one sticker. Nothing is kept or filed. **4 A clue inside** (T3) is not built.

**Misses and help.** A card dropped in a wrong gap slides back with a boing and a reason from what the picture shows ("This picture already has the boat shed, so it comes after the little dock on the shore"). One miss per distinct wrong drop; the same card in the same wrong gap again repeats the reason and counts nothing more. After two misses on one card its valid gap glows and `hints` goes up (at most once per card); the pause sheet's Show a hint lights the gap for the card in hand (or the oldest loose one), the same single hint per card. At level 1 the only miss is calling the newer picture "long ago". Free: tapping a loose card to hear its caption (levels 2–3), releasing a card away from every gap (it floats home, nothing said) and the pet's tap. Start again restores the round's first state, keeping misses and hints. Ordinary adaptation. Co-play: while she hangs a picture, ask what changed between this picture and that one. Off-screen: look at a baby photo and a recent photo of the same person, or plant a seed, and talk about what looks different. No couch version.
