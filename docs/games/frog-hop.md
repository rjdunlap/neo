## Design notes

*After number-line hopping games and Frogger.* A number line of lily pads. Levels: find a number; one more and one less; add by hopping; take away by hopping back; how many hops between two numbers (number cards); a line to 20 that slides to keep the start and answer in view; then split a shown sum that crosses ten by first choosing the hops to 10 and then landing the rest; level 8 is the mirror, a take-away from 11 to 16 (taking away 4 to 8) that first chooses the hops back to 10 and then lands the rest. At level 7 the sign first shows the whole sum (such as `8 + 5`), then the split (`8 + 2 + 3`); level 8 reads `13 − 5` and then the split the same way; the pet explains the total and each part. A sign shows the sum (`4 + 3`, `7 − 3`, `3 → 8`). Wrong-way hops and one-too-many or one-too-few hops get specific spoken feedback. Two misses bring a counted demonstration, the frog hopping and counting aloud, then the answer glows. Each question records at most one hint even when both stages at ten need help. On card questions, the second pad tap prompts and bounces the cards for free. Five answered questions finish the round for one sticker, including with help.

**Fraction Landings (levels 9–10, school only, skill `fractions`).** Level 9 `frac-hop` (quarter and half hops along one whole): the sign gives a start and a hop size, and she taps the pad where the frog lands; only 0, 1 and 2 are labelled, and answers can be mixed numbers. A wrong pad is a named miss (`fracMiss`), the one-whole bracket shows the size, and a hint counts once per question. Level 10 `frac-match` (two hop sizes, one lily): the frog lands with one size and plants a flag, goes back to zero, and she picks the card for how many hops of the other size reach the flag (the first question is always ½ × 2 → ¼). A wrong card (`matchMiss`) falls through to "Count the {size} hops from zero to the flag" (`hop.frac.flag`), then the usual two-miss glow. Both levels are appended to the school ladder (now 3–10, no `order`) and are not couch stops (the couch is unchanged). [Definition](../definitions/frog-hop-fraction-landings.md).

**Say the numbers, dots and flowers (dive slice 2, 2026-11-14).** On levels 3, 4 and 6 a counted hop says the pad it lands on and a tally dot rises over the frog per hop; a right answer says `hop.landed` and the frog croaks. Levels 5, 7, 8 and the fraction levels keep counting 1, 2, 3. Levels 1–2 draw dots under each numeral (fives grouped) and level 1's sign shows the target's dots; landmark flowers sit on 5, 10, 15 and 20 from level 3, never on 0 or the fraction line. Dots and flowers are a scene layer, so the sticker, icon and hit areas are unchanged.

**Over ten, your way (level 12, school only, mode `your-way`).** No number cards: a sum or take-away across 10, forward from 5–9 or back from 11–15 by 4–8 (two of five go back; no landing or split repeats twice running). A tap on 10 is a free stop: the frog counts the hops there and the sign reads e.g. `8 + 2 + ?`; a second tap on 10 is a squeak. A tap on the landing answers directly. A wrong landing is named (measured from 10 after a stop) and, after her stop, "From 10, {n} more." Two misses: the frog hops to 10, says "{a} to ten, {n} more.", hops the rest, returns, and glows the landing. The ghost finger and couch bot stop at 10, then land. The right answer says "8 plus 6 is 14."

**Hop by tens (level 13, school only, mode `tens`).** Pads read 0, 10 … 100 and the sign shows `30 + 20`; questions stay in pad-index space and `padValue` multiplies only what is shown or said. Starts 10–70, 1–4 tens on or back (two of five go back; never the same landing twice running); every round slides the line to show 100 at least once. The ask is "Start at 30. Hop 2 tens." (separate "one ten" lines); counted hops say the tens ("forty… fifty") with a tally dot, and the landing line names the value. Wrong side and off by one ten are named misses; two misses demonstrate and glow, as at levels 3–4. Flowers fall on 50 and 100, and the "100" label fits its pad at four views. Device speech of the tens words is unchecked.

**Big hops and little hops (level 14, school only, mode `hops`, [definition](../definitions/frog-hop-big-and-little-hops.md)).** An open line with no pads: only the frog's number and the flag's are written. A big lily (+10), a small lily (+1) and a back lily hop the frog; every hop says its landing, leaves a faint arc (long for ten, short for one, to scale) and is free until the frog lands exactly on the flag. The scene is its own class, `HopsLine` in `hops-scene.ts`, chosen by `create`; `logic.ts` holds `hopsQuestions` (start 12–58 and distance 11–45, neither a multiple of ten, flag at most 100, the first question 47 → 72, four questions), `routeLanding`, `overshoots`, `shortestRoute`, `routeWords`, `openLine` (the window: the ten at or below the start up to a ten past the flag's ten, so a hop past the flag still lands on the line; at most 70 numbers, capped at 110, shrunk to fit the board) and `hopsLayout`. A hop past the flag lands, is one miss (two charged a question) and lights Back; going past the end of the line is a free squeak. A second overshoot or Show a hint plays the fewest-hops route from where she stands (from the start if she is past the flag) and counts one hint; ten little hops on a question pulse the big lily once, free. The closing line names her own route. Not a couch stop. The browser step is in `woods-batch.mjs` (`WOODS_ONLY=hop HOP_LEVELS=14`).

**Where does it go? (level 15, school only, mode `estimate`).** The scene is its own class, `EstimateLine` in `estimate-scene.ts`, chosen by `create`. Four questions a round: three unscored guesses on a bare 0–100 line with only the ends written (37 first, then seeded: never 0, 50, 100 or a multiple of ten, at least 15 apart), then one placement. She taps the line or slides the frog there; then the marks by tens and their numbers appear, the frog says "Right on it!", "Very close!" or "Close!" (closeness within 3, 8 or 18; nothing extra when far) and hops to the real place ("37 is here"). A guess is never a miss and never a hint, so those questions report 0 and 0 (two rounds step her up, as Light Lab 7). The fourth question places a ten (20, 30, 40, 60, 70 or 80) on a line marked in tens with no numbers but the ends; a place snaps to the nearest mark, a wrong mark is one miss with a clue, from the second wrong mark (or Show a hint) the right mark glows (one counted hint per question), and the third wrong mark is a supported finish. The ghost finger taps the line at the true place. Rule tests ("Where does it go? (level 15)" in `logic.test.ts`): question rules, the linear map and reachability, closeness defined and symmetric, layout at four views.

**Hop along (level 16, lap and toddler, mode `along`, [definition](../definitions/frog-hop-entries-and-g1.md#16-hop-along-lap-and-toddler)).** The first of the four entries below level 1, appended at ID 16 and listed in `entryLevels`; lap and toddler play only 16, and `school` is now the literal 3–15. Its own scene, `AlongPond` in `along-scene.ts`: five big lily pads 1 to 5 with their dots, at a 150 pitch (one row when the bank and five pads fit, otherwise the bank and pads 1–3 above pads 4–5 on an upright phone), and an earthy bank left of pad 1 where the frog starts. The whole pond is one tap target resolved to the nearest pad (`nearestPad`, ties to the lower), so every pad owns at least a 120 square; the bank is its own target above the pond. A tap hops the frog pad by pad, each landing saying its number (`count`) and playing `sfx.pop(5 + pad)`, a rising pentatonic step; a tap on the frog's own pad is a croak and splash; the bank hops it home pad by pad and says "Home! Ribbit, {n}!" with `n` the hops it took home. Each touch-down shows a ripple; taps during a hop are not queued. The sixth tap's hops end the round with a big splash, "6 big hops! Splash!" and `{ misses: 0, hints: 0 }`. The ghost finger and guided first play tap pads 1, 3, 5, 2, 4, 5. There is no Show a hint: the definition's pause-sheet ghost finger on pad 5 is left to guided first play, which already points at the next pad. The lily pad art (`Pad`, `drawPad`, `drawDots`) moved to `pad-art.ts` so both scenes draw the same pad. Rule and round tests in `along.test.ts`.

**Hop to the dots (level 17, toddler, mode `dots`, [definition](../definitions/frog-hop-entries-and-g1.md#17-hop-to-the-dots-toddler)).** Level 1 with dots for a child who cannot read numerals yet; the toddler's second entry (toddler plays 16–17, `entryLevels: [16, 17]`). Its own scene, `DotsPond` in `dots-scene.ts`, on level 16's pond: the bank and the big pads at the 150 pitch (`alongLayout` now takes a pad count), each pad with its numeral and, under it, its dots drawn larger than on the main line (radius about 10, `PAD_DOT_SCALE`) on a white backing inside a deeper water panel (`dotsPanel`). A wooden sign at the top shows only dots, each 40 units across (`signDots`, the pads' five-frame grouping scaled up), and the voice says "Hop to 3! Find the pad with 3 dots." Five questions (`dotsQuestions`): the first two on pads 1–3 (pads 4 and 5 hidden and untappable), the rest on 1–5, never the same number twice running and at least one 4 or 5 on the long line; the frog starts on the bank every question. Each pad owns its own tap box, 140 wide from 70 above its centre to 120 below so it covers the pad's numeral and dots (`dotsPadBox`); open water only ripples, so a stray tap is never a miss. A right pad: the frog hops pad by pad saying each number with level 16's rising notes, then "3! Ribbit!" and leaps home for the next sign. A wrong pad: the frog hops there counting, says "That is 4. 3 is back this way!" (or "further along!"), and leaps home in one hop. Two misses glow the right pad (one hint per question, `hintCharge`); with the glow on, the next tap on any pad hops the frog to the right one, the supported finish (level 1 itself has no such third-tap finish). The ghost finger taps the target pad. The bank drawing moved to `pad-art.ts` (`drawBank`) and the pond's sky, pad scale and perch are shared from `along-scene.ts`, so both entry scenes draw the same pond. Rule and round tests in `dots.test.ts`.

**Which pad lost its number? (level 18, pre-K, mode `missing`, [definition](../definitions/frog-hop-entries-and-g1.md#18-which-pad-lost-its-number-pre-k)).** The missing number between two known ones, read off the line; pre-K plays 1, 2, 18, 3, 4 (`order`, and `entryLevels: [16, 17, 18]`). It runs in the main line's scene, not a scene of its own: a run of 4 to 6 pads (start 0 to 4, end at most 9) with one interior numeral under a code-drawn leaf, the frog on the run's first pad, the sign showing the run with a "?" ("1 2 ? 4 5 6"), and three number cards below. Five questions (`missingQuestions`): never the same run twice running, five different hidden numbers; the cards are the answer, one of its two neighbours (both are on the run) and one other number 0 to 9 that is not an end of the run, shuffled. The voice asks "Which number is hiding under the leaf? It comes after 2." A right card blows the leaf off, and the frog hops the whole run saying every number, the first and the uncovered one included. A wrong card shakes and the pet says "That is 4. Look at the pads on each side."; when that number is already showing on the run it adds "4 is already here!" (the definition named only the shown neighbour; any shown number gets it, since it is just as true). Two misses lift the leaf's corner so the numeral peeks out and glow the right card (one hint per question). A pad tap is free; the second one repeats the question. The run takes its own layout (`missingLayout`): centred in one row sideways; upright one row up to five and two rows of three for six, above the card row and clear of the guide's corner. The ghost finger taps the answer card. Rule, layout and round tests in `missing.test.ts`.

**Scenes (dive slice 6, 2026-11-14).** Each round draws one of four scenes after the questions are made, so a seed's questions are unchanged: the lily pond, stepping stones in a brook, a flower path (white daisies, so the pink landmark blooms on 5, 10, 15 and 20 still stand out) and an evening pond (dusk sky, light ground so numerals read, drifting fireflies). Art only: `hopLayout` places pads, sign and number cards the same in every scene, clear of the guide corner at four views. The sticker and hub icon keep the lily pad.

**Count up to take away (level 11, school only, mode `count-up`).** The sign shows `13 − 9`; the frog stands on the smaller number, a flag marks the bigger, and she taps a number card 1–6 for the hops between. Big number 11–20, difference 1–6; three of five rounds cross ten, and no difference or flag repeats twice running. A wrong card names one too many (counting the starting pad) or one missing; two misses count the hops aloud and glow the card. The right answer says "4 hops! 13 take away 9 is 4."

**A right answer never holds the next question (U7 fix batch 8, 2026-11-12).** At every level the right answer's line (`hop.yay`, level 9's `hop.frac.also`, level 10's `hop.frac.same`) starts and the next question follows at once: the frog's leap (and the window's slide) play over the line and input opens with the sign showing. The question's spoken ask (`hop.find` and the rest, `hop.frac`, `hop.frac.match`) waits for the line to end, or is said at her first pad or card tap; tapping the guide meanwhile says that waiting ask (`Game.sayWaitingAsk`), not the last question's. The fraction lines play over the next leap rather than being dropped. The last question's line is heard out before `hop.done`.

**On the couch.** Its six trip stops play levels 3, 5, 7, 8, 11 and 12 (add, gap, make ten, back over ten, count up and your way); the ring moves to a pad or card and the bottom button chooses, and the spoken lines say "Move to …, then press the bottom button" instead of "Tap…". The fraction, tens and scene-only work stays touch.

**Defined next (2026-11-28, [definition](../definitions/frog-hop-entries-and-g1.md)).** Four entries at IDs 16–19 (*Hop along* for lap and toddler, *Hop to the dots* for toddler, *Which pad lost its number?* and *Who is further along?* for pre-K between levels 2 and 3, placed with `order` and `entryLevels`) and the grown-up window's G1 *Hops of a hundred* at ID 20 in a new `grownup` band (13, 14, 15, 20). Each is a separate build.
