**Starts as:** get pretend parade props ready (ribbon on the pole, beater with the drum), pack them in the bag and watch the companion pull them in a parade

**Grows into:** level 1 packs the prop the card shows; level 2 makes a bare prop ready first; level 3 does two such jobs in any order; a bare or wrong prop in the bag is a miss, two on one job light its next step and count a hint; tapping works as well as dragging

## Design notes

*After pretend parades and dress-up bags; [definition](../definitions/ready-for-adventure.md).* The companion is going to the island parade and a picture card shows what it wants to bring. On a broad bench lie the props, some not ready yet: a bare flagpole beside its ribbon, a drum beside its beater. She drags (or taps to lift, then taps the target) the ribbon onto the pole and it becomes a flag, then drags the finished prop into the bag; when the bag holds everything on the card it zips and the companion pulls a wagon across the screen using exactly what was packed (the flag waves, the drum booms a pentatonic beat, the bell rings; a tap skips to the end pose). Level 1, *Pack the flag*, has finished props only: pack the one the card shows (flag, drum or bell, against a ball or another finished prop, seeded). Level 2, *Make it ready*, starts the requested prop bare with its part loose on the bench (flag with ribbon or drum with beater): attach, then pack. Level 3, *Two useful jobs*, asks for both flag and drum, whose four actions (ribbon on, flag in, beater on, drum in) can happen in any of six valid orders, with a status rail that ticks as jobs finish.

Misses and hints: a bare prop or loose part into the bag, a part on the wrong prop (level 3) and the unshown prop at level 1 each count one miss and float back with a spoken reason; any other release, tapping the card, bag, a packed prop or the companion is free. Two misses on the same job glow that job's next action and count one hint (misses on different jobs never combine); Show a hint (pause sheet) glows the next action and counts one hint, and tapping a glowing object makes the companion do it, counting nothing. Packed is final at these levels. One shell sticker, with or without help; the how-to card says an assisted round is supported completion. Start again restarts the same bench.
