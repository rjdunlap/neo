## Design notes

*Over-under making; [definition](../definitions/little-loom.md).* Eight accepted rows make a six-thread scarf. **1 Zoom the shuttle:** tap the loom; lifts alternate and the beater packs automatically. **2 Pick the yarn:** choose any of three reusable marked yarn pots, then tap the fixed shuttle pad. Every stripe schedule is valid. **3 Two pedals:** latch either pedal, then send the shuttle; subsequent rows use the other lift. Pedal exploration and unselected shuttle cues are free. A repeated submitted lift slips back for one miss without losing rows or direction. Two slips glow the other pedal; manual/automatic help counts at most once per row. Replay stays free. Busy taps are ignored; cancellation before release weaves nothing.

Toddler 1–2, preschool 2–3, pre-K 3–4, school 4–5. **Levels 4–5:** each of three authored samples per level has exactly one accepted plan; rows are judged against the sample row at their index. A repeated or float-breaking row slips (one miss, not woven); an allowed but different row is woven, pulses the sample row and costs one miss, and Undo row (free) takes it back, restoring pedal and yarn. Two misses at one row index glow the pedal or yarn once per row; over a standing mismatch help pulses Undo and counts no hint. Eight rows finish only if all match. Keep stays today's 48 cells. The two-pedal restriction is the island toy's rule. No lap/school/grownup registration. Each completed scarf gets one sticker through the shell, even with help. Optional shell Keep saves exactly 48 palette indices on the shared picture board, with previous-picture undo, profile isolation and backup/restore. No wardrobe or unfinished draft. Three clean ghost-finger plans demonstrate the same taps. Take turns choosing yarn or pressing pedals and sending; find a stripe on your clothes together. Actual iPad speech/touch/over-under reading and child enjoyment remain untested.
