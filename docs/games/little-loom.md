## Design notes

*Over-under making; [definition](../definitions/little-loom.md).* Eight accepted rows make a six-thread scarf. **1 Zoom the shuttle:** tap the loom; lifts alternate and the beater packs automatically. **2 Pick the yarn:** choose any of three reusable marked yarn pots, then tap the fixed shuttle pad. Every stripe schedule is valid. **3 Two pedals:** latch either pedal, then send the shuttle; subsequent rows use the other lift. Pedal exploration and unselected shuttle cues are free. A repeated submitted lift slips back for one miss without losing rows or direction. Two slips glow the other pedal; manual/automatic help counts at most once per row. Replay stays free. Busy taps are ignored; cancellation before release weaves nothing.

Toddler 1–2, preschool 2–3, pre-K 3. The two-pedal restriction is the island toy's rule. No lap/school/grownup registration. Each completed scarf gets one sticker through the shell, even with help. Optional shell Keep saves exactly 48 palette indices on the shared picture board, with previous-picture undo, profile isolation and backup/restore. No wardrobe or unfinished draft. Three clean ghost-finger plans demonstrate the same taps. Take turns choosing yarn or pressing pedals and sending; find a stripe on your clothes together. Actual iPad speech/touch/over-under reading and child enjoyment remain untested.
