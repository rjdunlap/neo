## Design notes

*Levels 1–7 · [definition](../definitions/helper-house.md) · robots-automation slices RA2, RA3 and RA4 (levels 5–7).* A house, a friend critter and a twelve-step day: the sun goes dim, bright, dim at dusk, dark, dim at dawn and ends bright. She drags a sensor (an eye for a friend, or a sun sensor) into a socket and a helper (the door or the lamp) follows a rule card; a pulse runs sensor, card, helper. Watch starts the day (and pauses or resumes it), Step runs one step then pauses, Undo takes back a placement.

**1 Wake the door:** the eye goes into the door socket (the window socket is a decoy that reads far); the card "friend near, door opens" is given. Three friends arrive at distinct steps 1–10, at least two apart, and the door shuts behind each; the goal is all three coming in. **2 Lamp at dusk:** one slot on the lamp post, and the bench holds an eye and a sun sensor; the card "dark, lamp on" is given, and the lamp must be on at every dark step (the eye reads far all day). **3 On and off:** the sun sensor is fixed with "dark, on" given and an empty row "[chip] then off" to fill from dark, dim and bright chips (drag a chip in, tap a chip, or tap the row to cycle). Bright then off and dim then off are accepted; dark then off fails (a later row wins, so the lamp never lights) and the empty row fails (the lamp is still on at step 11). **4 Chilly nook** (school only; the school band is levels 2–5): a heater sits in a nook beside the house and a fixed thermometer reads cool (0–2), warm (3–4) or hot (5–6) by fill height and colour. Two empty rows, `[zone] → on` and `[zone] → off`, take three zone chips (drag a chip, tap it to fill the first empty row, or tap a row to cycle). Warmth starts at 1; each step the heater adds 1 when on, else warmth falls 1 (kept 0–6). The goal is warmth 2–4 after every step from step 2 on. Only cool → on with warm → off is accepted; cool → on with hot → off overshoots to 5 (too warm); warm or hot → on, or a cool → off row, never warms. The friend shivers, is comfy or is startled. **5 Where does it look?** (school only): the sun sensor starts under the lamp, with a roof socket above it and a shed socket beside it; the bench holds nothing else. Under the lamp the sensor reads the lamp's own light as bright whenever the lamp is lit, so at dusk the lamp lights, the sensor sees bright, the lamp goes off, and it flickers. Only the roof is accepted. The shed reads dim and bright late, keeping the lamp on into the morning (lamp-on-at-morning); the bench is sensor-blind; a dark step with the lamp off is lamp-off-at-night. Failures: sensor-blind, lamp-flickers, lamp-off-at-night, lamp-on-at-morning. Every failure glows the sensor, and Show me moves it to the roof in one press.

**6 The comfy line** (school, range 2–7): the nook's thermometer becomes a seven-mark warmth gauge (0 at the bottom, 6 at the top) with the comfy band 2–4 hatched beside the lizard's comfy face. One line sits at a gap, dragged by a 110 tab in the left lane or set by tapping the gauge column; the given rows say warmth below the line turns the heater on and warmth above turns it off, so the heater never keeps its state. It starts at the top (gap 6). Before the first day only, an unscored guess ("Will the nook warm up faster?": a running hare for faster, two flames for the same, 124 buttons) is closed by either button, Watch or any change to the line; after the first day the truth line says the heater warms one step at a time wherever the line is, and that day's climb stays as faint dots on the gauge. Gaps 3 and 4 are accepted (gaps 5 and 6 overshoot to too-warm, 1 and 2 never warm); Show me moves the line to the nearer accepted gap (4 from above, 3 from below). **7 Calm heater** (school): an on line (flame tab, left lane) and an off line (crossed-flame tab, right lane) that cannot cross; between them the heater keeps its state. Both start at gap 3. A click jar of eight cups takes a pebble and a click for each switch, and extras roll out; more than eight switches fails as `heater-busy`. Only on 3 and off 4 is accepted (the comfy single lines 3 and 4 switch ten or more times); on boards with the on line at gap 1–2 and the off line at 5–6 the day reports whichever failure the nook reaches first. The glow prefers the on line for never-warms and the off line for too-warm and heater-busy; Show me sets the off line first, then the on line.

**Misses and help.** `misses` is always 0: a failed day is an observation with the reason spoken (a friend waiting, the sensor blind, the lamp on in the morning, the lamp off at night). The same failure on two completed days glows the piece (the sensor and socket, or the open row) and counts one hint once until the piece changes. Show me places the needed piece (at level 4 it fixes one wrong row a press) for one hint (a press on an already right board says so and costs nothing). After three failed days the pet offers "Shall I set it up?" (a green tick), which places the accepted board, plays the day and counts one hint. One sticker per round. Taps are guarded during the finale. Ghost finger: drag the eye or sun sensor to its socket and press Watch; at level 3, Watch once, drag bright into the row, Watch. At level 4: Watch, drag cool then tap hot, and Watch. At level 5: Watch, drag the sensor to the roof, Watch. At level 6: drag the line to gap 4 and Watch (the bot skips the guess). At level 7: Watch, drag the off line up to 4, Watch. Misses stay 0; the same failure twice glows the failing row (the on row for never-warms, the off row for too-warm, unless that row is already right) for one hint. Whether the pulse reads on an iPad, how a five-year-old reads the pictures, and the day's pace remain open.
