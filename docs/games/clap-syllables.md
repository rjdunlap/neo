## Design notes

*After classroom syllable clapping and Rhythm Heaven.* The pet says a word whole and claps its beats ("but-ter-fly") with beads lighting up, and the child claps along on big hands. Then she claps a word by herself (a ring round the hands pad fills over the 1.5 s pause after her last clap, so she can see the word is about to be counted; a wrong count brings the pet's demonstration; a second leaves the beats showing), sorts pictures into 1-, 2- and 3-clap bins, and hears some claps and finds the picture with that many beats. Preschool plays 9, 1, 2: level 9, Clap along (short), starts with two distinct words, one with one beat and one with two, before level 1’s three-word guided round. Each tap lights the next bead with no timing or count mistakes; a quiet moment repeats the whole word and its claps freely. Pre-K plays levels 2–4 and school 5, 6, 8. At school level 5 she sorts eight long-word pictures into four bins, including watermelon and helicopter with four claps each. At level 6, Beat train, three pictures sit in a jumble above a train of three cars with the engine on the right; she drags each picture into a car, fewest claps at the back (left) to most by the engine, and two pictures with the same count go either way round. Each round is three trains of three pictures with at least two different counts (one to four) and no picture twice. A drop on a wrong car is a miss: a boing, "{word} has {count}. Let us clap it, then find its car!" and the claps; the second and later misses add a hint and glow the picture that belongs in the first empty car. Dropping in empty space or onto a loaded car costs nothing. A loaded car shows beads for its count, and the full train claps each car in order, says "Fewest claps to most. All aboard!" and chugs away. At school level 8, Longer or shorter?, four pairs of pictures have different counts. The pet says and claps both whole words, then asks for more or fewer claps (alternating from a seeded first request). Tap the matching picture; replay repeats both counts and the request freely. A wrong choice adds a miss, gently boings and repeats both counts; the second and later wrong choices add a hint and glow the exact answer. The ghost finger chooses that same answer. Level 7 is reserved and unavailable; saved IDs and pins retain their meaning. Thirty-eight words have one to four beats (nine added 2026-10-14 with code-drawn pictures: pizza, rainbow, pencil, window, spider, tomato, strawberry, pineapple, caterpillar; a test keeps out words whose beat count varies by dialect). A clap is a count, not a timing, so the game counts claps in a burst rather than using Sound Garden's rhythm judge. Friends' names were left out: counting a child's own name would need a syllable counter the game does not have. Whether device speech says each word clearly is unchecked. The background loop is silenced, so her claps are the only beat.

**A count never holds the next word (U7 fix batch 20, 2026-11-12).** The word's count starts with the caption and the next word comes after the existing beat; its ask (and clap-along's demonstration) waits for the count, and her first clap names the word and counts. At levels 4 and 8 the right picture's count works the same way, and any first tap before the claps or the comparison says the ask instead of being judged, even the right picture, since the answer depends on hearing the claps. A wrong tap before the ask is heard is not a miss. The last count is heard out. The ghost finger waits for an ask.
