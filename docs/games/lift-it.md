## Design notes

*After Tinybop Simple Machines and NGSS 3-PS2 (forces); [definition](../definitions/lift-it.md).* Haul a crate up to a friend on the tree's ledge by dragging the rope's end in strokes; the drag is measured from pointer movement, and a held crate never slides back. Wheels dragged from a tray onto the branch hook and/or the crate's hook give four rigs: `direct` (push), `fixed` (branch wheel, pull down), `movable` (crate wheel, two strands) and `both`. The ideal rule is paws = ceil(w / strands) and knots = h × strands, so two strands halve the paws and double the knots; every working rig counts. A rig that cannot lift the crate stretches and wobbles ("too heavy"), not breaks. Levels: 1 Pull! (a branch wheel is already hung), 2 Push or pull, 3 Too heavy, 4 Twice the rope (a knot counter, and a free guess at the knots), 5 Who pulls? (the request names ground or ledge pullers), 6 By the numbers (paws and knots cards before the haul). Preschool 1–2, pre-K 2–4, school 3–6. Misses: none at levels 1–4. At 5–6 the first tug per rig per parcel on a rig that cannot lift or ends at the wrong pullers is a miss (pressing the greyed "nobody up there" handle counts the same), and a wrong number card is a miss while the right one lights. After a parcel's second miss the best rig's hooks (or the right card) glow, one hint per parcel; the pause sheet's "Show a hint" pulls a stroke with her at levels 1–2 (one hint per request, no drawn pet paw, a spoken line) and glows at 3–6. Speech stays on the ideal rule. One sticker per round; nothing is filed in the journal. Music is `STYLES.paint`. Co-play: one pulls, the other counts the paws or knots aloud. Off-screen: lift a bag with a rope over a chair back, then count how far the hand travels. The ghost-finger bot hangs the best rig's wheels, answers cards and hauls in strokes. Not built: the replay button from level 3 (replay the last haul with knots counted aloud). Nothing is saved and there is no couch play.
