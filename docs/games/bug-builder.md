## Design notes

Two guided levels show pale matching shape outlines; two copy levels use a separate small model; three mirror levels ask for matching wings, ending with two columns and six spots. Reusable stamps return to the tray. The final palette contains repeated shapes and colors, requiring both attributes to match. Drops outside a spot are exploration, not mistakes. The tokens come from a pool of twelve, drawn by seed, so a round's colors are distinct (plans of four or fewer also keep every shape and color different), and a copy board is never symmetric. On the second wrong drop the hint glows the matching spot and also rings the twin across the line (mirror levels, with the line "Look across the line at its friend.") or the same place on the little model bug (copy levels). On completion at levels 5–7, both wings and their stamps fold across the middle together, meet their twins and open again; a faint model wing stays visible. This uses the existing finish wait and does not change misses or hints.
