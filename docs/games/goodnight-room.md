## Design notes

*After.* Goodnight Moon *and bedtime routines.* A seeded bedroom (lamp, clock, fishbowl, teddy, kitten and puppy), barn (lantern, cow, pig, duck, bunny and cat), tent (teddy, bunny, puppy, campfire, lantern and toasting pot), or boat cabin (duck, cat, anchor, clock, lamp and fishbowl), with spacious floor, wall and shelf slots. The tent has sleeping bags and a fire that banks down; the cabin has plank walls and a porthole. Each room has one lamp or lantern. Tap each to say goodnight: it gets sleepy eyes, a "z" floats up, the room darkens a little and stars appear in the window. When everyone is asleep, a soft chime ends the round. Level 1 shows four of the six friends, chosen by the round's seed, and level 2 may open with up to two friends already asleep (tapping one is a soft tick). Levels 3–4 name who to say goodnight to, then two in order. Level 5 (preschool and pre-K) asks for the living animals in any order: kitten, puppy and fish in the bedroom, or duck, cat and fish in the cabin, among a lamp and clock, with no ambiguous teddy or extra prop. The two objects settle together afterwards. Level 6 (pre-K) asks for everyone with the lamp or lantern last: any order of the other five works. An early light tap keeps it on and explains that it helps us see, with a glow on an awake friend after two misses. Level 7 (pre-K) asks for 2–4 goodnights among six awake friends. Any friends work, each chosen goodnight is counted aloud, and a numeral tally with filled dots follows the count. At the requested count the other friends stay awake briefly, then settle together for the finale. No misses or hints in this free-choice mode; tapping a sleeping friend adds nothing. Level 8 (pre-K) asks for the orange or brown friends in one seeded four-friend bedroom. Brown accepts teddy and puppy, with kitten and fish as decoys; orange accepts kitten and fish, with lamp and teddy as decoys. The orange round excludes the puppy's orange ears, and the fish's shelf is blue in color rounds to avoid a brown decoy. Both matches work in either order. A wrong color names the tapped friend's color and repeats the requested color; two misses glow a remaining match. The other friends settle together afterwards. Level 9 (pre-K) asks for everyone but the lamp or lantern: put the other five friends to sleep in any order and leave the night-light awake. Tapping the light is a gentle miss explaining that it stays awake; two misses glow a remaining friend. The night-light keeps its warm glow through the finale, and the closing voice says that the other friends are asleep. Level 10 (pre-K) is a goodnight tour of three distinct rooms: always the bedroom first, then two of the barn, tent and boat cabin (seeded). Each room has all six friends awake and she taps everyone; there are no wrong answers, so misses and hints are always 0. When a room is asleep a big pulsing yellow arrow appears in the middle of the screen ("Everyone here is asleep. Follow the big arrow!"); tapping it fades to night and sets out the next room ("Now the barn. Tap everyone…"). After the third room the stars fill the sky (at least 48) and the voice says everyone everywhere is asleep. One sticker ends the whole tour. Pre-K spans 5–10; younger ranges are unchanged. Each friend rings its own bell note when she says goodnight (a fixed pentatonic step per friend in that room, not a note that rises with the count); the finale is unchanged. One sticker ends the whole room. A wrong friend, or the right two out of order, is a gentle miss with a glow after two; tapping someone already asleep is just a soft "shh".

Level 11, *One big sleepy friend* (2026-11-13, PR #1613), is the lap entry (lap order `[11, 1, 2]`; an entry level, so a saved 11 carried into toddler starts at 2; no saved level or pin renumbered). Four friends from a random room, all awake; one big friend stands in the middle and a layer over the whole room takes every touch. A touch anywhere tucks it in with its bell and "Goodnight, …!", it settles small into its own spot, and the next friend tiptoes in from the side. Nothing can be a miss or a hint. The goodnight line never holds the next friend; the last is heard out before the finale. The ghost finger touches the floor.

**A goodnight never holds the next tap (U7 fix batch 19, 2026-11-12).** At every level the tapped friend's goodnight (or level 7's count) starts with input open and her next tap cuts it, the tally keeping the total. In the request modes the next request opens at once and its ask waits for the goodnight, is said by the guide, and is dropped at a right tap that finishes the request; a wrong tap before she has heard the ask is not a miss, and the ask is said instead. The last goodnight is heard out before the finale. The ghost finger waits for an ask.
