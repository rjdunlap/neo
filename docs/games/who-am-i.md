## Design notes

*Slice Z3 of the [zoo-animals study](../deep-dives/subjects/zoo-animals.md), [definition](../definitions/who-am-i.md).* Friends stand on the barn's stage; the pet gives a clue, spoken and shown as a picture sign ("I have feathers"). Level 1 (pre-K 1, school 2): three friends, one clue, and she taps the friend the clue fits. Level 2 (pre-K 2): four friends and two clues; she turns over every friend a clue rules out (it half-lifts and ducks behind its card) and the last one jumps out. Level 3 (school 3): six friends from at least four groups and three clues, at least two of them from covering, babies and breathing. The cast is the six mammal critters, duck, owl, penguin, frog, turtle and fish. Misses: at level 1 a friend the clue does not fit; at levels 2–3 a face-up friend the clue keeps (its card half-turns back and the reason is spoken). After two misses (per round at level 1, per clue at levels 2–3) the answer glows or the ruled-out friends shimmer, one hint; the pause sheet's "Show a hint" does the same and counts once. Tapping the sign or a clue thumbnail, and peeking at a face-down card, are free. Taps are held while a clue changes. Ghost finger at every level; no journal entry. With the allowed features the level 2 answer is always a mammal, frog or turtle (a bird or the fish is picked out by its first clue) and the level 3 answer is always the frog or the turtle; a rule test brute-forces this. Level 4 (the bat and surprise rounds) is not built.
