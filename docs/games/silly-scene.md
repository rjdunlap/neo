**Starts as:** Tap the fish in the tree to hear why it belongs in the pond and watch it splash back into the water.

**Grows into:** Find two impossible things in a scene, then spot them even when something silly could really happen, like a dog wearing a hat.

## Design notes

- Each level opens with an authored scene. The pond opens level 1, the farm opens level 2, and the beach opens level 3. Later rounds use the same three backdrops and a seeded mix of props. The two finds in levels 2–3 always break different rules about place, parts or direction.
- Slots are 120 × 120 logical units in both orientations. A star stays at each find while its repair plays and the pet says what makes it impossible. Level 2 and 3 show how many stars remain. The companion repeats the instruction when tapped. One sticker follows the last repair and spoken reason.
- Tapping ordinary scenery, the companion or a found thing is free. An ordinary prop or the silly-but-possible prop counts once until the next find. Two distinct misses or Show me highlight the leftmost unfound thing, with one hint counted per find.
- The walking-fish reason compares the pictured feet with fins, since some fish use their fins to move along a surface.
