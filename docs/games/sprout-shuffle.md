## Design notes

*Levels 1–8 · [definition](../definitions/sprout-shuffle.md) · genetics slices G2–G3, [Hidden Sprouts](../definitions/hidden-sprouts.md) G5 level 4, G6 level 5 and level 6, [Patchwork Conservatory](../definitions/patchwork-conservatory.md) G7 levels 7–8.* Two parent plants, each carrying two copy tiles (spot or plain), and a sprout slot between them. She builds a possible sprout by putting one copy tile from each parent into it (tap a tile under a plant, or drag it to that plant's own slot; the other tile of the tray swaps in free, and a drop on the other parent's slot springs back silently, not a miss). Four closed buds then show the possible routes, and opening them shows every look that could grow.

**1 One route, then every route:** AA × aa. The built sprout uncurls spotted, four closed buds appear, and she opens all four; no wrong answer is possible. **2 Which sprouts could grow?:** Aa × aa (sides, tile order, petal colours and the missing bud are seeded). She builds one route, three buds open and the missing one lights its two source tiles (a wrong tile shakes, no miss), her sprout shrinks into its matching bud, then two choice cards (spotted, plain) and a leaf Check, which appears only once something is chosen. **3 Could be, or always?:** two spotted parents (Aa × Aa). She gives the sprout one copy from each plant as before, then all four buds start closed and she builds each in turn by tapping its two lit source tiles (three spotted, one plain; the plain bud's paths stay lit longer). Two claim cards follow, "Spotted sometimes. Plain can grow too." and "Always spotted." (choosing one lets go of the other), and the leaf Check. A seed-packet **Try one** button right of the leaf draws a random route with `ctx.rng` and regrows that route's bud with its paths lit; it is free, never counted and never gates Check (the drawn route grows in its bud card, because the claim cards stand in the sprout's place). Check with "always" is one miss, a boing, and the plain route replayed ("This way, each plant gives a plain copy..."); a second miss glows the plain bud (one hint). Accepting "sometimes" ends with "Spotted most ways, plain one way...".

**4 Which pairs always make spotted?** (about 10 and up, reached by stepping up or a pin): a seeded friend asks for a bed of spotted sprouts. Three pair cards (from the definition's pools A/B/C; sides and tile order seeded) each show two plants over their copies; a yellow magnifier above each card opens that pair's four-bud fan (free, replayable; bud taps regrow). Tapping a card chooses it (speaking its plants' copies left then right) and the leaf Check follows. A wrong Check is one miss: a chosen pair that can make plain opens its fan and replays the plain route with a glow; a missed always-spotted pair opens with all four spotted. A second wrong Check glows the accepted cards (one hint). Show me chooses the accepted cards (the first use is one hint) and she presses the leaf. Pool C accepts two pairs. The lines are on device speech (no clips).

**5 Hidden Sprouts** (about 10 and up, reached by stepping up or a pin; `src/games/sprout-shuffle/hidden.ts`, rules in `logic.ts`: `compatible`, `candidates`, `couldTell`, `contradicts`, `hiddenSetupFor`, `checkCandidates`, `drawSprout`, `tryRoom`, `hiddenNextStep`). A spotted plant's tray is covered by a seed packet. She taps a watering can and three spotted sprouts grow onto a record label beside a plain `aa` tester. She chooses every candidate card (two spot copies; one spot copy and one plain) that could fit and presses Check: "both" is the right answer, with a "can't tell yet" banner. She waters again: in the one-spot-copy case one plain sprout grows and only that card fits; in the two-spot-copies case six spotted sprouts still leave both. Then the packet peels open on the true copies. A wrong Check is one miss with a counterexample fan (four ways of the left-out or ruled-out candidate with the tester; the plain sprout pulses); a second miss in a choice glows the fitting cards and the deciding plain sprout (one hint per choice). Show me selects the fitting cards (one hint per choice). Watering, card names and **Try one** (a free seed-packet draw from the true copies, record capped at 8, room kept for the second watering) never count; a Try one plain sprout clears the current choice and says so, so a free draw never causes a miss, and if it settles the case before the first Check the round goes straight to the reveal. The lines are on device speech (no clips).

**6 Which plant could tell us?** (about 10 and up; same files; `TESTER_LEVEL`, `TESTERS`, `cantTellSprouts`, `testerRecord`, `pickStep`, `hiddenSetupFor(rng, level)` with a `bench`). A bench holds two known plants, spotted `AA` and plain `aa` (order seeded); tapping one plants it beside the hidden plant (swapping is free). Watering with `AA` grows three spotted sprouts and both candidates' fans open side by side (every way spotted); the pet says it can't tell, the record clears and the plant returns to the bench, never a miss. Watering with `aa` grows the authored six-sprout record (a plain sprout only in the one-spot-copy case) and she chooses once, graded as level 5's second choice; in the ambiguous case the pet says "Even the plain plant couldn't tell us this time." Then the packet reveal. Show me in the pick plants the plain plant (one hint); in the choice it chooses the right cards (one hint). Try one is available in the choice. Ghost finger: the plain tester, the can, the cards, the leaf. The lines are on device speech (no clips).

**7–8 Patchwork Conservatory** (about 11 and up, reached by stepping up or a pin; `src/games/sprout-shuffle/conservatory.ts`, rules in `logic.ts` section "Levels 7–8", art in `art.ts`). Two independent markings: round spot tiles and petal-shaped rim tiles (thick rim-colour edge or dotted outline; letters B/b for grown-ups); the rim colour is seeded red or teal, never a petal colour. A friend's brief card (pressing repeats it) asks for sprouts that are spotted and rimmed. A bench of plant cards (3 at level 7, 4 at level 8) goes into one of two pots by tap or drag; tapping a potted plant sends it back, and a third plant swaps into the most recently filled pot. A spots lens and a rim lens (130 units) each open that marking's four-bud fan over the bench, dimming the other marking's tiles; the close button or the panel closes it. The leaf Check grades. **Level 7:** only `AA bb` × `aa BB` always works (a trap plant shows both traits); a failing Check is one miss with the failing marking's lens opening on the way it fails, and the potted plant that hides the failing copy pulses and is named; a second miss glows the working pair (one hint). **Level 8:** two working pairs; after the first the bed fills, the pots empty and the pet asks for another way, a Finish star appears (pressable once the bed is done); failing Checks then and re-planting the found pair ("That's the plan we found") are free, and the other pair grows a second bed and ends the round. Show me plants a working pair (the definition's helpPair rule), one hint before the first plan. Voice `sprout.pc-*` and `sprout.help.conservatory` on device speech (no clips). Ghost finger: level 7 pots `AA bb` and `aa BB`, opens both lenses and Checks; level 8 the same with `AA bb` and `Aa BB`, then Finish. The toy assumes declared independence of the two markings (no linkage), noted on the how-to card.

**Misses and help.** A wrong Check is one miss with a boing, and the missed route's bud replays and is spoken ("a plain copy and a plain copy..."). A second wrong Check glows that bud and the missing card and counts one hint once. Show me does the next step (gives a copy, opens a bud, taps a source tile, or chooses every makeable look and pulses Check; she still presses Check) and counts one hint the first time it is used in each part of the round (once per round at level 1; at level 3 one hint per phase, and Show me picks "sometimes" for her to Check). The ghost finger never presses Try one. One sticker through `ctx.finish({ misses, hints })`. Music is the paint style.
