## Design notes

*After tactile pretend-kitchen play; [definition](../definitions/recipe-workshop.md).* A counter holds pots of pretend fruit mash (berries, apple, orange) and a white base spoken as "yogurt", a big bowl and a spoon. A tap on a pot plops a dollop into the bowl as its own coloured layer; the spoon (tap, or drag it onto a bowl) stirs every layer into one blend; a star or leaf topper (level 1 and 3) sits on top; Serve hands over the bowl and the pet holds up exactly what she made. Pretend food only: nothing is cooked, cut, tasted or advised. Region `cozy-village`, but the home land is Painted Canyon (Cozy Village has no room for decoration at twelve visible games; see the definition's decision 1).

Levels (toddler 1, preschool 1-2, pre-K 2-3): 1 *My bowl* any non-empty bowl serves, nothing is a miss or hint; 2 *Follow the picture* one fruit and the base in either order, mixed after the last, with Undo (no topper); 3 *Bowls for two* the pet and a seeded friend each want a star or leaf on top; tap a bowl to choose it, and mixing folds a topper in.

Misses and hints: adding, mixing and Undo are always free; only a Serve that does not match the card is a miss (an empty Serve at level 1 is free). Two misses on one bowl glow the next step and count one hint; Show a hint counts at most one per bowl. Drags (a pot, topper or spoon onto either bowl) act there and select it; a missed drag floats home.
