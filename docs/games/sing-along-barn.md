**Starts as:** tap anywhere to sing the next note of Old MacDonald, Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Au Clair de la Lune, Rain, Rain, Go Away, The Itsy Bitsy Spider, Bounce High, Bounce Low or Ring Around the Rosie, Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star or The Wheels on the Bus (one per round); a friend comes out for the verse and answers

## Design notes

**Fun action:** tap anywhere and the song moves one note on; a friend comes out of the barn for each verse and answers. **Learning:** song order and animal words; making choices. Lap, toddler, preschool and pre-K; home land Barnyard (moved from Music Mountain in RW13 slice 1). No wrong answers below level 8: `misses` and `hints` are 0 there (level 8 judges one rhyme pick), and a song she plays along to is not offered to the tune plaque.

Each round sings one of **ten songs**, drawn from the round's seed (`pickTune` in `logic.ts`; a song is a `Tune`: opening, verse, answer phrase). From level 5 she chooses the song herself (see Levels); below that the seed draws it. **Old MacDonald** is sung in two forms. Levels 1–5 sing the **short Old MacDonald**: the first two lines once, then "And on that farm he had a …, E-I-E-I-O" for each friend, with the moo-moo lines and the repeated first line left out; a grown-up singing along should sing that short form, as the how-to card and co-play tip say. Level 6 (pre-K) sings the **whole verse**: after "E-I-E-I-O" comes "With a moo-moo here and a moo-moo there, here a moo, there a moo, everywhere a moo-moo" (the friend sounds on every moo; the syllable `~` is its own sound word), then "Old MacDonald had a farm, E-I-E-I-O" again (`SingPlan.full`, `OLD_MACDONALD_FULL`, `tunesFor` in `logic.ts`). The moo-moo melody (G G C C C / G G C / C C C G A G) was written from memory of the tune's shape, not a printed score (open in [DEVICE-CHECKS](../DEVICE-CHECKS.md)). The tune is "Old MacDonald Had a Farm" in C (C D E G A, with a pickup on the G below), so it needs no exception to the pentatonic rule; each note is one sung syllable. A row of beads shows the line being sung (one bead per note; sung ones fill, the next breathes). The opening is "Old MacDonald had a farm, E-I-E-I-O"; each verse is "And on that farm he had a …" (the friend answers in its own voice on the last note) and "E-I-E-I-O", after which the friend settles on the bottom row and the next one comes out. The finale is the last "O": everyone answers once, confetti, and the round ends with one sticker.

**Levels.** 1: one friend (a seeded draw), 25 taps. 2: two friends. 3: she taps two of five friends to ask them in (a free choice; barn windows light, a second tap on the same friend does nothing, the others leave), and they sing in the order she chose. 4 (pre-K): three verses, and she picks who sings each one. She taps anywhere to sing the opening; at the end of the opening and of each verse the five friends come out ("Who will sing next? Tap a friend!", `barn.next`) and she taps one (a free choice, never a miss). The song grows by that friend's verse, so it plays on one tap per note as before: three Old MacDonald verses are 51 notes. A friend who has sung waits in their own slot of the row and only says hello when tapped, and a tap anywhere else while choosing does nothing. The round ends after the third verse with one sticker; `misses` and `hints` are 0. 5 (pre-K): the same flow, but first she chooses the song (`chooseTune` in the plan): eight cards (scaled 0.5 so the row fits portrait), a cow for Old MacDonald, a hot cross bun for Hot Cross Buns, a lamb for Mary Had a Little Lamb, a moon for Au Clair de la Lune and a cloud for Rain, Rain, Go Away (sixth card) a spider for The Itsy Bitsy Spider (seventh card) a red ball for Bounce High, Bounce Low and (eighth card) a ring of pink roses for Ring Around the Rosie, each sounding its first notes when touched; any pick is right and never a miss (`barn.tune`). Then she taps anywhere to sing the opening, and three verses follow with her pick of who sings next, as in level 4; `misses` and `hints` stay 0. 6 (pre-K): like level 5, with two friends and the whole Old MacDonald verse (47 notes per friend, 59 for one, 106 for two; Hot Cross Buns, Mary, Au Clair, Rain and Itsy are unchanged); `misses` and `hints` stay 0. The how-to demonstration sings two verses only at levels 4 and 5, and at level 6 the short form with one friend, to stay inside its 75-second limit (the full verse at the card's finger pace overran it), so it does not show the moo-moo lines. Plans, the melody, the song as a flat list of notes, the seeded draw and the helper's next invite are in `logic.ts`; tests assert one note per syllable, only pentatonic notes, one answer per verse in order, seeded variety and that every friend can turn up.

**Level 7, *Finish the line* (toddler and preschool; toddler `{1..7, order [1,2,3,7]}`, preschool `{2..7, order [2,3,7]}`; pre-K and lap unchanged).** Two seeded friends; the tune is a seeded draw from the four whose verse ends on a picturable word (`GAPS` in `logic.ts`: Hot Cross Buns "bun", Rain, Rain "play" (a ball), Itsy Bitsy "spout" (a water spout), Twinkle "sky" (the night sky); the others end on words that do not picture, and Au Clair is French). Before each friend's last note the song pauses: the beads stop breathing and the word's picture pops up on a card beside the barn under the roof eave. A touch in the first 0.8 s (`GAP_HOLD`) only wobbles the picture and hums the note softly, so mashing cannot skip it; after that any touch sings the word, the pet says it (`barn.finish`, "{word}!"), and the picture jumps and goes. Instruction `barn.finish.how`. Nothing is wrong, so `misses` and `hints` are 0. The bot waits out the pause (`GAP_HOLD` + 1.2 s) and taps. Co-play: sing the line and stop before the last word, and wait.

**Level 8, *Which one finishes it?* (preschool and pre-K; preschool `{2..8, order [2,3,7,8]}`, pre-K `{3..8, order [3,4,5,6,8]}`, topping pre-K's ladder; toddler and lap unchanged).** Two seeded friends and a seeded tune from Rain, Rain (gap "play", rhymes with "away"; a ball, decoy apple), Twinkle ("sky", rhymes with "high"; the night sky, decoy moon) and Ring Around the Rosie ("posies", rhymes with "rosie", said once in the opening; a posy, decoy bun; a right pick sings both syllables); the rhyme table is `RHYMES`/`RIMES`/`rhymes()` and the side of the rhyming picture is a seeded draw (`rhymeSides`). Rain and Twinkle stop once per friend's verse. At each stop the song pauses, two picture cards (154 by 140 on screen) stand either side of the barn and the pet says the line up to the missing word (`barn.rhyme.line.*`). A touch in the first 0.8 s only wobbles the cards; a tap elsewhere repeats the line and is never a miss. The rhyming picture sings the word and the song goes on. The other picture bounces back with a boing and the pet says e.g. "Apple? Away ... play! Those sound the same at the end." (`barn.rhyme.miss.*`): a miss, counted once for touches within 0.6 s. A second miss at one stop glows the rhyming picture (one hint). `finish()` reports the real misses and hints, so this is the first level of the game that adapts on them. Instruction `barn.rhyme.how`. Rhymes are hand-written ARPAbet rimes, not a dictionary. The demonstration bot picks the rhyming picture every time. Co-play: sing the line, stop, and ask which sounds the same at the end.

Taps never wait on speech or animation: any touch plays a note at once, and mashing still finishes the song. Taps sit on a full-screen layer (cooldown 90 ms); the friends in level 3 are touched at the body (about 110 screen units) so neighbors never share a touch, including in portrait. The ghost finger invites friends in turn and then taps once per note. Co-play: the grown-up sings and she taps; off screen, sing it with stuffed animals, one for each verse.
