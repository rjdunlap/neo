## Design notes

*After food-web models in early-grade science and the study's feeding-record idea; [definition](../definitions/food-web-workshop.md).* Three big stations sit in the meadow, each a plant or creature (clover, caterpillar, bird; dandelion, snail, bunny and others across rounds), with two picture cards along the top, each one thing that was seen ("We saw the caterpillar eat clover leaves"). Tapping a card replays it for free: a small food symbol floats from the food into the eater's mouth. She drags a food badge (just under its station) onto the eater and an arrow grows from food to eater with dots flowing along it. A drag that makes an arrow not on the cards is allowed; building is exploring until she presses Show our web. Undo (the round arrow, top right) takes back her last change.

**Levels.** Level 1 *Make the picture*: no arrows, two cards of a chain; make both arrows. Level 2 *Turn the arrow*: the same kind of chain arrives with one arrow pointing backwards; she drags the food's badge onto the eater and the arrow swings round. Level 3 *One food, two eaters*: one plant feeds two creatures, so two arrows leave the plant.

**Misses and help.** Show our web (green button, bottom right) is the only thing that counts a miss: one per failed check, with the boing, one spoken line naming the fault (an arrow the wrong way, a card with no arrow yet, an arrow no card showed) and that fault's card replaying; after two failed checks the fault's card and stations glow. The pause sheet's "Show a hint" mends one fault (the arrow grows, turns or fades) and counts one hint; repeated hints always reach a passing web. A passing check plays the web once, says "Our food web! The arrows show where the food goes.", then one sticker. A drag refused during the miss line ticks. No journal entry; nothing is saved. Bands: school plays levels 1-3 (a younger child finds it behind Rainbow Meadow's signpost); region tinker-lab.
