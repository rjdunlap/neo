## Exact finite case

Three distinct reusable pieces have masses 1, 3 and 9 **blocks**. Parcel masses are known labels, in fixed introductory order 2, 5, 8, 13 blocks; no hidden unknown or guessed mass. Each parcel stays on the left pan. Each weight can be in the tray (0), alongside parcel on left (−1 contribution to measuring the parcel), or alone on right (+1). Equal-arm centres are fixed. Legal balance iff `parcel = sum(right weights) - sum(left weights)`. All pan positions have identical leverage. No duplicate pieces, negatives as physical weights, splitting, buying or picking a weight set.

| Parcel | Weights with parcel, left | Weights right | Exact equality | Minimum placements from empty pans |

| 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 + 1 = 3 | 2 |
| 5 | 1, 3 | 9 | 5 + 1 + 3 = 9 | 3 |
| 8 | 1 | 9 | 8 + 1 = 9 | 2 |
| 13 | none | 1, 3, 9 | 13 = 1 + 3 + 9 | 3 |
