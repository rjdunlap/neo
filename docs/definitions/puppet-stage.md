# Puppet Stage, levels 1–3 (definition)

Defined 2026-10-27 as slice 1 of the [theatre-and-puppetry study](../deep-dives/subjects/theatre-puppetry.md#build-slices-in-order) ([packet row TP1](../deep-dives/subjects/RECOMMENDATIONS.md#theatre-and-puppetry-recommendations)), [PR #1027](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/1027). Decisions 1–6 of the study were taken on the developer's [October 23 delegation](../archive/DECISIONS-2026-10-23.md#standing-rules) (S8: adopt the lean; S6 and S9: a listening judgement is a device check, not a blocker): a new game, Music Mountain, puppet voices as device-speech `pitch`/`rate` presets, nothing kept, the director's chair defined only after play, the craft card at TP5. **Nothing is built.** Levels 4–6 (TP2, the story cards and the premiere) are a separate definition after this batch is played.

**Fun action and purpose:** touch the stage floor and a stick puppet rises there, says hello in its own voice and follows her finger; tap it and it bobs and talks again. The purpose is the kindergarten theatre core, *a character with a voice*: first the puppet is simply in her hand (level 1, nothing counted), then the pet names a puppet and she makes that one talk (level 2, by name, then by voice), then one puppet holds a line and she chooses the sleepy, tiny or big way to say it (level 3). It is the island's only activity that puts a character under her finger and lets her choose how it speaks: Story Theater chooses and watches, Movie Night places sounds on fixed footage, Cue Studio orders cards the companion performs, Feelings Faces names feelings, and Peekaboo Barn pops an animal from a door but never hands it over. No line claims that puppet play develops language, feelings or self-control (Lillard 2013, in the study); the grown-ups' card says what she does: she gives a puppet a voice.

## Overlap and reuse

- **Identity check** (the study's condition): the activity stands only if *a puppet in her hand, speaking in a voice she chose* is central. Level 1 is that and nothing else; level 2 asks which puppet the name or voice means; level 3 asks which voice fits the asked mood. If the building session finds level 1 plays as Peekaboo Barn with a stick, the ladder restarts at level 2 as the first level and the lap window is dropped rather than padded.
- **Peekaboo Barn** supplies the lap precedent: touch anywhere, something pops up and says hello, a round that ends by itself after a few touched reveals. **Who Am I?** supplies the plank floor and red valance already drawn in code (its `resize` draws a floor at 72% and a scalloped curtain across the top); Puppet Stage draws its own proscenium in the game's `art.ts` because it needs a **slot** the puppets rise through and **side curtains** that close. **Story Theater** (`src/games/story-steps/theater-art.ts`) shows the cast idiom to copy: a `Critter` from `CRITTERS` with `alive = false`, scaled, as a flat actor. **Feelings Faces** supplies the six `Mood` faces (`setMood` on a `Critter`: happy, surprised, sing, sleepy, sad, calm); level 3's bubbles show the puppet's own face in the mood, and ask for the voice, never the feeling's name. **Movie Night** supplies `music: silenced(STYLES.x)` and Take a bow; its shown-only premiere is TP2's, not this packet's.
- Reuse `onTap` for the puppets, bubbles, curtains, tassel and Bow; `draggable` (`src/engine/drag.ts`, `lift: 0` so the stick stays in her hand rather than 40 units above it) for a risen puppet; `ctx.particles` for the clap and the bow's confetti; `src/audio/notes.ts` for the puppets' sung notes; `view` for layout in both orientations. No save field, render texture or new engine helper.
- **The one shared change (decision 3):** `src/audio/voice.ts` gains `VoicePreset { pitch: number; rate: number; volume?: number }` and `sayIn(id, preset, vars?)`, which formats the line as `say` does but **always** uses device speech with the preset (never a rendered clip, since the clips are the pet's single voice) and keeps the existing muted-environment timing, the turn counter, ducking and the no-`end` timeout. `GameContext` gains `sayIn`, wrapped in `GameScene` with `whileHere` like `say`. The pet's `say`, `instruct` and the shell's lines are untouched and keep their clips. `voice-choice.ts` keeps ruling out novelty voices; a preset never changes the chosen voice.
- **Puppets and stage** are code-drawn in the game's `art.ts`: a `Puppet` container is a `CRITTERS` head (`bunny`, `bear`, `duck`, `cat`, scaled about 0.5 as Story Theater's actor) on a flat paddle body with two stubby arms and a stick below; the stage is a proscenium arch, two red side curtains, a painted backdrop (meadow by day; a tassel at the arch's right swaps it for night, free at every level), a plank floor with a long dark slot, and a front row of three small seated critters below the floor. A mask above the floor line makes a puppet appear to rise *through* the slot; the puppets park under the floor at four home spots in a fixed order (Bunny, Bear, Duck, Cat, left to right).

## The model (`logic.ts`)

- **Puppets.** `PUPPETS = ['bunny', 'bear', 'duck', 'cat']` with a base voice each: Bunny `{ pitch: 1.5, rate: 1.05 }`, Bear `{ pitch: 0.7, rate: 0.85 }`, Duck `{ pitch: 1.8, rate: 1.15 }`, Cat `{ pitch: 0.95, rate: 0.9 }` (the pet speaks at `1.15` / `0.95`, so no puppet shares its voice), and a sung note each as a pentatonic step (Bunny 7, Bear 1, Duck 9, Cat 4) for the second tap's "la".
- **Voice presets (level 3), absolute and fixed** so "sleepy" always sounds the same whichever puppet holds the line: `sleepy { pitch: 0.8, rate: 0.65, volume: 0.8 }`, `tiny { pitch: 1.9, rate: 1.2, volume: 0.85 }`, `big { pitch: 0.6, rate: 0.85, volume: 1 }`. **Margins:** every pair of presets differs by at least `0.2` in pitch *or* `0.2` in rate, and in at least two of pitch, rate and volume; every preset differs from every puppet's base voice by at least `0.2` in pitch or `0.15` in rate (so "the ordinary way" is never one of the three). The face and the body carry the mood too: sleepy droops with the `sleepy` face and eyes half shut, tiny shrinks to 0.7 with the `surprised` face, big grows to 1.3 with the `sing` face. If the iPad's voice ignores `pitch` (the device check), rate and volume still separate all three and the table is retuned by constants only.
- **Levels.** `plan(level, band, rng)`:
  - **1 *Pop up*** (lap and toddler): no asks. `popsToBow = 3`, `secondsToBow = 30`; at lap only, `autoBowAfter = 6` pops (Peekaboo Barn's lap rule: the round ends by itself after six touched reveals). Nothing is counted.
  - **2 *Make Bunny talk*** (toddler and preschool): a seeded pair of puppets on stage and four asks. At toddler every ask is **by name** (`{ kind: 'name', puppet }`). At preschool the four asks are two by name then two **by voice** (`{ kind: 'voice', which: 'tiny' | 'big' }`: tiny is the higher-pitched puppet of the pair, big the lower); a voice-ask pair is drawn only from pairs whose base pitches differ by at least `0.5` (Bunny–Bear, Duck–Bear, Duck–Cat, Bunny–Cat), and before a voice ask each puppet says a short hello in turn, left then right, so the voices have just been heard. No three asks in a row name the same puppet; every ask names a puppet that is on stage.
  - **3 *Say it like*** (preschool and pre-K): one seeded puppet, one of four authored lines, three bubbles in a fixed order (sleepy, tiny, big) and four asks (`{ kind: 'way', way }`), each way at least once, no two in a row the same. The round's first bubble tap is a **free try** (the puppet says the line that way, nothing counted); the first ask follows it.
- **Answer rules.** `answer(ask, tapped)` returns `right` or `wrong(reason)`: for a name ask, the tapped puppet is the named one; for a voice ask, the tapped puppet is the pair's higher (tiny) or lower (big) base pitch; for a way ask, the tapped bubble is the asked way. `wrong` names what was tapped (`ps.im.{puppet}` or `ps.was.{way}`). `hintTarget(ask)` is the asked puppet or bubble.
- **Bands and ranges** (`BandLevels`, level numbers stable across bands so TP2 appends 4–6 without renumbering): `lap: { min: 1, max: 1 }`, `toddler: { min: 1, max: 2 }`, `preschool: { min: 2, max: 3 }`, `prek: { min: 3, max: 3 }`. No `school` window in this packet (TP2 adds `prek: { min: 3, max: 5 }` and `school: { min: 4, max: 6 }`). No `order`.

## Screen and controls

- **Stage.** Landscape: the proscenium fills the middle of the view, arch at the top, side curtains left and right (each at least 110 wide, full stage height; a tap on either closes both over the puppets, a second tap opens them; puppets keep their places), the backdrop behind, the floor line at about 60% of the view with the slot along it, the three front-row seats below the floor at the centre, the voice bubbles (level 3) in a column at the right, and Bow at the bottom right above the corner buttons; the shell's guide stays bottom left and the corner buttons stay clear. Portrait: the stage spans the width, the floor at about 55%, the bubbles in a row beneath the slot, the front row beneath them, Bow bottom right; nothing shrinks. Planned sizes: puppets 140 × 200 touch (head and paddle together, `hitArea` in local units), the floor touch surface the whole backdrop from the arch to the slot (a tap anywhere raises a puppet at that x), bubbles 130 × 130, Bow 130 × 110, curtains 110 wide, tassel 100 × 100. Risen puppets stand at least 160 apart; `layout(level, view)` is audited at the lands test's four views (landscape, portrait, two phone insets).
- **Level 1 flow.** A touch on the floor raises the nearest parked puppet (by home x) at that x within one frame, saying hello in its voice; while her finger stays down the puppet follows it (drag); on release it bobs. Up to two puppets stand at once: a third touch sends the longest-standing one down the slot and raises the nearest free one. A tap on a standing puppet bobs it and it speaks again (the `ps.again.*` line, then on later taps a sung "la" on its note, alternating). After three pops or thirty seconds, **Bow** appears; at lap the show bows by itself after six pops. Bow: the standing puppets bow, the front row claps, a short fanfare, `ctx.finish`.
- **Level 2 flow.** The pair rises at the start, each saying hello. The ask through `ctx.instruct` ("Make Bunny talk!" / "Who has the tiny voice?"); a tap on the right puppet makes it bob and say its talk line in its voice, the front row claps, the next ask follows. Tapping the guide repeats the ask, as the shell does everywhere (no extra button). After the fourth right answer, Bow.
- **Level 3 flow.** The puppet rises and says its line in its own voice; the pet says "Tap a voice to try it"; her first bubble tap plays the line that way (free). Then the asks ("Say it the sleepy way"); a tap on a bubble plays the line that way with the matching face and size; right: clap and the next ask; after four, Bow.
- **Touch-down feedback.** The puppet lifts and tilts on touch-down and bobs on release; a bubble inflates on touch-down; a curtain swings; the tassel swings and the backdrop crossfades; Bow dips.
- **Speech.** Asks and the three instructions through `ctx.instruct` (the pet's voice, with clips where rendered); every puppet line through `ctx.sayIn` with the puppet's base voice (levels 1–2) or the asked preset (level 3). The guide never speaks in a puppet voice. Feedback names what she tapped ("I'm Bear!", "That was the big voice"), never a judgement of her.
- **Feel.** `music: silenced(STYLES.hub)` (the puppets sing and speak; the registry test's list of games where she makes the music gains this one); claps from `ctx.particles` and a short Web Audio clap; a pentatonic "la" per puppet; a two-bar fanfare at the bow. Palette colours; reduced motion raises puppets without the bob.

## Accounting, help and finishing

| Level | Counts a miss | Counts a hint | Never counted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | nothing | nothing | pops, drags, taps, curtains, tassel, Bow |
| 2 | a tap on the other puppet while an ask is open | the glow on the asked puppet after the second miss in a row on one ask; at most one per ask | taps on the asked puppet after it answered, hellos, curtains, tassel, repeating the ask |
| 3 | a tap on a bubble other than the asked one while an ask is open | the glow on the asked bubble after the second miss in a row on one ask; at most one per ask | the free try, tapping the puppet (it says its line the ordinary way), curtains, tassel |

- **Supported finish:** the glow stays until the right tap, so every round reaches Bow and one ordinary sticker through `ctx.finish({ misses, hints })`, help or not. Nothing is kept; the show is gone at the bow (decision 4). Leaving early owes nothing.
- **Adaptation:** one wrong puppet or bubble in a round is still smooth (at most one miss); two glows or four misses in a round are struggling. Level 1 reports zero misses and zero hints every time, so a lap or toddler child moves up only when the band's range allows (toddler to level 2). Assisted completion is one sticker, not evidence of mastery. Nothing here claims a three-year-old understands moods or voices; it claims she made the named puppet talk and chose the asked voice.

## Rule tests (`logic.test.ts`)

- **Voices:** the three presets are pairwise distinct by the margin rule above and each differs from all four base voices by the base margin; no base voice equals the pet's `1.15` / `0.95`; every pitch is within `0.5`–`2` and every rate within `0.6`–`1.3` (device-speech ranges that stay intelligible); the voice-ask pairs are exactly the pairs whose base pitches differ by at least `0.5`, and `tiny`/`big` resolve to the higher/lower of any such pair.
- **Plans:** over 300 seeds per band and level, level 1 has no asks and zero countable actions; level 2 draws two distinct puppets, four asks, every ask naming a puppet on stage, no three in a row the same, voice asks only at preschool and only on an allowed pair; level 3 draws one puppet and one of the four lines, four way asks covering all three ways with no two in a row the same; the same seed gives the same plan.
- **Answers:** `answer` is right exactly for the named puppet, the pair's higher or lower pitch, or the asked bubble; `wrong` names the tapped puppet or way; `hintTarget` is the asked target; following `hintTarget` from any ask finishes the ask in one tap, so every round is reachable with at most four hints.
- **Bot:** the ghost finger's intent for every reachable state (pop, drag, tap the named puppet, tap the asked bubble, Bow) is a tap or drag on an existing input; the bot finishes every level at every band.
- **Layout:** `layout(level, view)` at the four views: every input at least 100 units, puppets at least 140 wide, curtains at least 110, risen puppets at least 160 apart, nothing under the bottom-left guide or corner buttons, portrait stacks rather than shrinks.
- **Registry, lands, how-to and off-screen tests** pass with the new game: Music Mountain 6 → 7 games (`LAND_SPOTS` is 13, one page), the card scoped per level, no "later levels" wording, `music` a silenced style listed in the registry test.

## Ghost finger, how-to card, voice and registration

- **Ghost finger:** `touchDemo: true`, `autotouch()` through the public inputs, reading `game.state`. Level 1: tap three floor spots (a puppet rises at each), drag one puppet a hand's width, tap Bow. Level 2: tap the asked puppet after each ask. Level 3: one free bubble tap, then the asked bubble after each ask. The bot never taps a curtain, never misses on purpose and never calls finish; it re-reads the layout after every resize.
- **How-to card** (`src/content/howto.ts`). Goal: "Make a puppet pop up and talk in its own voice." Steps: "Touch the stage floor: a puppet pops up there and says hello. Finish: `{ from: 1, to: 1, text: 'After a few pops, tap Bow; the puppets bow and the round ends.' }`, `{ from: 2, text: 'After four right answers the puppets bow and the round ends.' }`. - **Registration:** ID `puppet-stage`, name "Puppet Stage", spoken title `game.puppet-stage`, region `music-mountain` (the subject row's "arts" is this region on the island), home land **Music Mountain** (6 games today, 7 with this one; the lands test proves one page; Story Grove is the recorded alternative by decision 2 and already pages at sixteen). ## Open decisions

Each has a default the build uses; none blocks the batch.

1. **Land.** Default: Music Mountain (7th game, one page). Alternative by decision 2: Story Grove (pages at 17; allowed, never shrinks).
2. **The preset table.** Default: the values above, as constants in `logic.ts`; the device check may retune any number without touching a rule, and the margin tests guard the retune.
3. **Puppet lines and clips.** Default: `sayIn` always speaks with the device so a puppet voice is never the pet's rendered voice; if the better-voice work later renders per-preset clips, `sayIn` can look them up by text and preset without a game change.
4. **The second tap.** Default: a spoken line, then a sung "la", alternating. If the play-through finds the "la" thin, every tap speaks.
5. **Two puppets up at level 1.** Default: at most two standing, the oldest goes down. One at a time if a one-year-old finds the swap confusing (a one-line change).
6. **The lap auto-bow.** Default: six pops end the lap round by itself, as Peekaboo Barn's lap level does; Bow still appears from the third pop for a child who taps buttons.
7. **The free try at level 3.** Default: the round's first bubble tap is free and the asks start after it. If a child taps the puppet repeatedly instead, the first ask starts after three puppet taps.
8. **`sayIn` versus an options bag.** Default: a separate `sayIn(id, preset, vars?)` so the pet's `say` keeps its signature and clips. An options argument on `say` is the alternative if a second caller appears (Story Weaver's *Read it in a voice*, TP7).
