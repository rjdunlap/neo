# Lost Layers, levels 1–2 (definition)

Defined 2026-10-18 as slice **D6** of the [deep-time study](../deep-dives/subjects/deep-time.md#build-slices-in-order), from its [older and parent case](../deep-dives/subjects/deep-time.md#lost-layers-one-older-and-parent-case), its [audience table](../deep-dives/subjects/deep-time.md#an-answer-for-every-audience) and the [IDEAS row](../IDEAS.md#candidates). Docs only: no game, ID registration, voice line or save field exists yet. **Ready to build:** every open call below has a default the build can use. The cliffs, the creatures and the island's three rock rules are invented for the island, so no geologist is needed before this slice; the studies' reviewer decision gates only a later case that names a real place, creature or age.

**Fun action and purpose:** two sea cliffs on the island show their rock layers side by side. Gull Cliff is a whole record, six layers with a fossil picture in some of them. Shell Cove is a broken one: three pieces of rock with wavy lines between them where the sea took rock away. She slides Shell Cove's pieces up and down beside Gull Cliff until every fossil lines up with its twin and no wavy line is squeezed shut, and stamps each arrangement that fits as a **story card**. The question is where one fossil's time went at Shell Cove: into the plain mud layer, where no fossil happened to be kept, or into the rock the sea washed away. At level 1 the honest answer is **Can't tell**, because two stories fit, and that is the answer that finishes the round. At level 2 she chooses **one more look** that could separate the two stories, and only then picks the story that still fits. The learning goal is reading a record across places while keeping its order, and telling what the evidence shows from what it cannot show: a missing fossil is not proof that nothing lived then. The study's anchors are NGSS 4-ESS1-1 (evidence from patterns in rock layers) and the middle-school idea that rock strata organize relative time; they are content anchors, not a claim the game teaches them.

## Overlap

Lost Layers owns **two places and one record laid against another**: the order inside each cliff is given and never edited, the pieces slide as rigid blocks, and the decision is "where did a time go at the second place, and can the rocks say?" ## The model

Rules are stated as **the island's rules**, in the pet's voice, on the how-to card and in the first instruction. They are a toy of relative time, not geology: numerical ages, overturned or tilted rock, faults, thick layers that span several of the other cliff's rows, a creature that lived at two times, and real index-fossil reliability are outside this model and the how-to says so in one line.

1. **Layers stay in order.** Both cliffs are labeled undisturbed: lower is older, and the order inside a cliff never changes.
2. **The same picture means the same time.** Each island creature lived at one time only, so a layer with the curl shell at Shell Cove is the same time as the layer with the curl shell at Gull Cliff.
3. **A wavy line is rock the sea took.** At least one of the other cliff's layers belongs in that gap. A plain layer, with no picture, could be any time: not finding a fossil is not finding out that nothing lived.
4. **(Level 2) The dark stripe fell on the whole island in one day.** Every bit of rock from that time carries the stripe; rock without it is not from that time.

- **Pictures** are four invented creatures named by shape, each a flat symbol in one dark ink: `curl` (curl shell), `fan` (fan fern), `ribbon` (ribbon fish), `dot` (dot snail). A **layer** is `{ picture: Picture | null, stripe?: boolean }`; a plain layer has `picture: null`.
- **Gull Cliff (A)** is `rows: Layer[]`, six layers bottom to top, no wavy lines, each picture at most once. **Shell Cove (B)** is three pieces bottom to top, `[p1, plain, p3]`, with a wavy line above the first and above the second (`gaps: [0, 1]`).
- **A case** is `{ level, pictures: [p1, p2, p3], layout, stripe? }`. The seed draws three distinct pictures of the four; `p2` is the **lost** one, present at Gull Cliff and absent from Shell Cove's pieces. The two layouts of Gull Cliff:

  | `layout` | Gull Cliff rows 1–6 (bottom to top) |
  | --- | --- |
  | `low` | `p1`, plain, **`p2`**, plain, plain, `p3` |
  | `high` | `p1`, plain, plain, **`p2`**, plain, `p3` |

  At level 2 the `p2` row also carries the **dark stripe**, and Shell Cove's plain piece has a hidden `stripe` the seed sets true or false.
- **A placement** is `rows: [r1, r2, r3]`, the Gull Cliff row each Shell Cove piece sits beside (1–6, bottom to top). **`valid(case, placement)`** holds when: the rows are strictly increasing (rule 1); every pictured piece sits on the row with the same picture (rule 2; the cases guarantee that row exists); between two pieces separated by a wavy line at least one row is skipped (rule 3); a plain piece may sit on any row, plain or pictured; and, after a level-2 look, a striped piece sits on the striped row and an unstriped piece does not (rule 4). **`alignments(case)`** enumerates every valid placement over the 20 ways to choose three rows of six.
- **A story** names where `p2`'s time went at Shell Cove: `story(case, placement)` is `in-the-mud` when the plain piece sits on `p2`'s row, else `washed-away` (the row lies in a wavy gap). At level 1 both layouts give exactly two alignments, `[1, 3, 6]` and `[1, 4, 6]`, one of each story; the plain piece on row 2 or 5 squeezes a wavy line shut. At level 2, after the look, exactly one alignment remains: `stripe: true` keeps `in-the-mud`, `stripe: false` keeps `washed-away`.
- **Looks** (level 2) are three cards: `lens-mud` (look inside Shell Cove's plain piece), `lens-wavy` (look at a wavy line) and `lens-shell` (look inside Shell Cove's bottom piece). **`informative(case, look)`** is whether the look's result changes `alignments`; only `lens-mud` is. `lens-wavy` shows the sea-worn surface and nothing else; `lens-shell` shows `p1` again.
- **Ties**: `ties(case, placement)` gives, for each pictured piece, a tie to its twin row, **green and level** when side by side, **red and slanted** when not; for each wavy line, a band that is **red** when no row is skipped; and, after a level-2 look that found the stripe, a dark tie from the plain piece to the striped row. A placement is valid exactly when nothing is red.
- **A round** is one seeded case per level: the three pictures, the layout, the left-to-right order of the three look cards and, at level 2, the hidden `stripe`. The rules, the symbols and the row count never vary with the seed.
- **Story cards** are in memory for the round only. Level 1 starts with none; level 2 starts with both already stamped, since finding them is level 1's work.

## Screen and controls

- **Landscape (1024×768 design area):** **Gull Cliff** is a column 180 wide at x 300–480, six rows of 100 each from y 80 to 680, with its name on a sign above and a small "lower is older" arrow beside row 1. **Shell Cove** is a second column position at x 560–740 on the same rows, drawn as a faint outline of six empty rows; its three **pieces** (180×100 each, a dark-ink picture or plain mud, a wavy edge drawn on the top of the bottom piece and both edges of the middle one) sit on it. The **tie zone** is the 80 units between the columns. At the right, x 780–1000: the **It fits** button (200×100, green) at the top; two **story-card slots** (200×120 each) under it; and the **Can we tell?** panel at the bottom (a 200×100 button per stamped story and a 200×100 **Can't tell** button). The pet is the corner guide at bottom-left with 150 units kept clear; nothing touchable sits left of x 300 or below y 680 on that side. Every target is at least 100 logical units after scaling.
- **Starting position:** the bottom and top pieces start on rows 1 and 6 with green ties (the pet says their twins already line up); the middle piece starts snapped on row 2, so the lower wavy band is already squeezed red: the first thing to fix is in plain view, and the pet's first line says why. At level 2 it starts on the lower of the two fits.
- **Dragging a piece:** an object drag (`src/engine/drag.ts`, riding 40 units above the finger); on touch-down the piece lifts with a shadow and a soft stone scrape. While dragging, the ties and bands redraw live, so she sees red turn green as she passes a fit. On release over the column the piece snaps to the nearest free row (a bottom or top piece can be moved too: its tie goes red and slanted, which is allowed and uncounted); released anywhere else it returns to where it came from. `pointercancel` and leaving the screen return it too.
- **It fits:** submits the current placement. All green: a chime, the placement's story card stamps into the next slot (a 200×120 picture of Shell Cove with a ghost of `p2` drawn in the plain piece or in the gap, captioned by the pet's line), and the pet names the story. Anything red: a gentle boing, the red tie or band pulses, the pet names it (see accounting). A placement already stamped: the card wiggles and the pet says it is already there; nothing counted.
- **Can we tell?** (level 1, after the first card): tapping a story's button claims that story is the one; tapping **Can't tell** claims the rocks cannot say. See accounting for which is right when.
- **Looks (level 2):** the three look cards (150×120 each) sit in a row under the It fits button, with a **Look slot** (160×130) and a **Look** button (200×100) beside them, replacing the story-card slots until the look is done (the two stamped cards move down into the Can we tell? panel as its buttons). Tapping a card previews it: the lens icon hovers over the place it would look and the pet says what that look can see; free. Dragging a card into the slot and tapping Look commits it: the **lens** (a 300-unit round inset at top center, as in Fossil Dig) opens on that place and shows what is there. `lens-mud`: fine mud with a dark stripe through it, or fine mud with no stripe; a dark tie draws (or the pet explains the absence) and the stamped card that no longer fits dims only after she makes her claim, not before. `lens-wavy`: a worn wavy surface, "the rock is gone." `lens-shell`: curl shells (or whatever `p1` is), "we already matched that one." Tapping anywhere closes the lens; the slot frees for another card unless the look was informative.
- **Portrait (768×1024):** the two columns move to x 230–410 and 470–650 with the same 100-unit rows from y 60 to 660; It fits, the story slots and the Can we tell? panel go in a tray across the bottom (y 700–1000) to the right of the pet's corner; look cards share that tray at level 2. `layersLayout(view, level)` returns every rectangle and a test asserts the sizes at the four views.
- **Sound and music:** a stone scrape on lift, a soft click on snap, a short pentatonic pair when a tie goes green, a chime on a stamped card, a glass ring when the lens opens; the land's music keeps playing (not `silenced`).

### First rounds

- **Level 1 (`low` layout, curl shell / fan fern / ribbon fish):** Gull Cliff: curl shell, plain, fan fern, plain, plain, ribbon fish. Shell Cove's bottom piece (curl shell) and top piece (ribbon fish) sit on rows 1 and 6, green ties. The pet: "Two cliffs, same island. Lower is older. The curl shell and the ribbon fish already line up. Where does this mud go? The wavy lines are rock the sea took, so leave room." She drags the plain piece to row 2: the lower wavy band squeezes red. It fits: boing, the band pulses, "The wavy line says rock is missing here. Leave at least one layer's room," one miss. Row 3: everything green. It fits: chime, a card stamps: "This fits! Then this mud is fan-fern time, and no fern was kept in it." The Can we tell? panel opens with that card and Can't tell. She taps the card: boing, the piece slides itself to row 4, all green, "This fits too. Then fan-fern time is in the rock the sea took." The second card stamps (with help), one miss. "Two stories fit. Can the rocks tell us which?" Can't tell: "Right. Both fit, so these rocks can't say." The round finishes with misses 2, hints 0.
- **Level 2 (`high` layout, dot snail / curl shell / ribbon fish, `stripe: true`):** both cards are stamped and the piece sits on row 3. The pet: "Two stories fit. Could one more look tell them apart? Tap a look to see what it looks at." She taps `lens-wavy`: the lens hovers over the wavy line, "That's where the sea took the rock. Nothing is left to see there," free. She drags `lens-wavy` into the slot anyway and taps Look: the lens opens on a worn surface, boing, "The rock is gone, so this look can't tell us," one miss; the slot frees. She drags `lens-mud` in, Look: the lens opens on fine mud with a dark stripe, a dark tie draws to Gull Cliff's row 4, "A dark stripe! The same ash fell on the whole island in one day. So this mud is curl-shell time." She drags the piece to row 4 to see it green (free), then taps the "in the mud" card: "Yes. The stripe puts curl-shell time in this mud, not in the gap." Misses 1, hints 0.

## Accounting, help and finishing

- **Never a miss:** dragging any piece anywhere, including off its twin's row; dropping a piece back; looking at ties; previewing a look card (tap); It fits on an already-stamped placement; Can't tell at level 1 with only one card stamped (the pet says "Maybe. Is there another way it fits? Try sliding the mud," and nothing is counted); Can't tell at level 2 before any look ("Not yet. Could a closer look tell us? Pick a look"); closing the lens; repeating the instruction; tapping during feedback (ignored).
- **A miss:** It fits with anything red (the red tie or band pulses and the pet names the rule it breaks: squeezed wavy line, a picture off its twin, or order); at level 1 claiming one story when the other fits (the piece slides to the other fit, it stamps with help, and the pet says both fit); at level 2 committing an uninformative Look (the lens shows why it cannot help); at level 2 claiming Can't tell after the informative look, or the story the look ruled out (the piece slides to that story's row and its red tie or stripe mismatch pulses).
- **Glow and hints:** three steps, **arrange** (level 1), **look** (level 2) and **claim** (both). After two misses on a step the target glows: a fit not yet stamped (the piece's outline on that row), the `lens-mud` card, or the right claim button; `hints++` once per step. **Show a hint** in the pause sheet (`Scene.pauseActions`) does the same for the current step and counts at most once per step; asked again it only repeats the words. **Start again** rebuilds the same seeded round and keeps misses and hints.
- **Finish:** `ctx.finish({ misses, hints })` after the correct claim, with or without help: one ordinary sticker from the shell. The story cards are drawings for the round; nothing is filed or saved ([decision 5](#open-decisions)).

## Rule tests (`logic.test.ts`)

- **Pictures and columns:** four distinct pictures; every case draws three distinct ones with `p2` present at Gull Cliff and absent from Shell Cove's pieces; Gull Cliff has six rows with each picture at most once and no wavy lines; Shell Cove is `[p1, plain, p3]` with wavy lines above pieces 0 and 1; both layouts appear across 500 seeds per level, as do all four pictures in each role.
- **Validity:** `valid` rejects a non-increasing placement, a pictured piece off its twin row, and a wavy line with no row skipped; it accepts the plain piece on a pictured row; at level 2 it enforces the stripe both ways.
- **Level 1 ambiguity:** for every seed, `alignments` has exactly two members, `[1, 3, 6]` and `[1, 4, 6]`; their stories differ and cover both `in-the-mud` and `washed-away`; rows 2 and 5 for the plain piece are invalid for the wavy-line reason specifically.
- **Level 2 resolution:** before the look, `alignments` has the same two; after `lens-mud`, exactly one, whose story is `in-the-mud` iff `stripe`; both outcomes occur across seeds; `informative` is true for `lens-mud` only, and the other two looks leave `alignments` unchanged.
- **Ties:** every tie is green and no band red exactly when `valid`; a level-1 placement with the plain piece on row 2 has a red lower band and on row 5 a red upper band; a bottom piece moved to row 2 has a red slanted tie.
- **Hints:** `glowTarget(round, step)` is an unstamped fit for arrange (and the remaining one once a card is stamped), `lens-mud` for look, and the correct button for claim (Can't tell at level 1; the surviving story at level 2); following each glow finishes its step.
- **Accounting:** the scripted level-1 round above gives misses 2, hints 0; two It fits presses on row 2 then the glow gives hints 1 and a further request leaves it at 1; Can't tell with one card changes nothing; the scripted level-2 round gives misses 1, hints 0; two uninformative Looks then `lens-mud` gives misses 2, hints 1.
- **Determinism:** the same seed gives the same case, look order and stripe.
- **Layout:** `layersLayout` at the four views for both levels: rows 100 tall, pieces 180×100, every button, slot and card at least 100 units, the pet's corner clear, both columns, the lens and the tray inside `view`, no card overlapping a column.
- **Registry, lands and how-to tests** pass with the new game, including Bubble Beach still fitting on one screen at every band with five games ([decision 2](#open-decisions)).

## Open decisions
