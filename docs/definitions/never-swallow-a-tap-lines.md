# Never swallow a tap: which lines hold (U7d, classification)

*Status: the 25 games ready for upright play (the pool a phone's Today's six draws from first) are classified, read at `origin/main` `a3ac3b70`, 2026-11-10. Bubble Beach's 13 other games are classified too, read at `origin/main` `66d124ec`, 2026-11-11: seven hold a cut line (fix batches 3 and 4, not built). The other 120 games with an awaited line are listed as candidates, not read. Fix batch 1 is built (2026-11-10); batch 2 is not.*

This is [R4](never-swallow-a-tap.md#r4-a-tapped-name-never-holds-input)'s list: per game, the spoken lines the game **awaits**, sorted by whether R4 lets them hold input. The rule is R4's: a line that names or answers what she just tapped never holds input; an instruction, a hint after a miss and a step's spoken result may.

## How it was read

- **The search.** In each game folder (tests left out): `await` on `ctx.say`, `ctx.sayIn` or the game's own `say`/`speak` wrapper, and a line with `.then(` chained on it. A line started with `void` holds nothing and is not listed.
- **The reading.** For each awaited line: what was just tapped, whether `busy` (or the game's own gate) is set while it plays, what waits behind it, and the line's words in `voice.ts`.
- **What this is not.** Nothing was played, and nothing was timed. A game whose pacing comes from a tween or a timer rather than a line is outside R4 and not listed.

## The four kinds

| Kind | What it is | R4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cut** | A tapped thing's name, a right answer's confirmation or a free choice's reaction, awaited with input closed and more of the same board or round behind it | Fix: start the line, reopen input (or start the next ask) at once |
| **Between boards** | Praise for a finished board, awaited before the next board of the same round fades in. Nothing is left to tap, so no tap is swallowed, but she waits out the line (a muted line too) before she can play on | Not R4's case as written; a second, optional batch |
| **Closing** | The round's last line, awaited before `ctx.finish`. The board is done and the result follows | Leave |
| **Held** | An instruction or its companion sentence, a hint after a miss, a demonstration's narration, or a step's spoken result the next step builds on | Leave; R5 shows the hold |

## Fix batches

Each is the existing-game bug-fix tier (typecheck, the game's rule tests, one filtered regression play; `fingerdemo` only if the bot's timing changes), ; batch 1 is built, batch 2 waits for a playtest note, and batches 3 and 4 (Bubble Beach) are ready to build.

1. **`animal-snack` and `far-lights`, the cut lines (built 2026-11-10, PR #1488).** The right answer's line starts and the next ask or the next lights follow at once (after the existing eat or lift motion, not after the line). The next ask's own line cuts the confirmation, as any new line does; in `far-lights`, where the confirmation is the lesson, the next lights are shown and input opens while it plays. Hints and results stay held.

   *As built.* `animal-snack`: in *who* rounds `snack.yum` starts after the eating and the next ask follows at once; on a right count the bell rings, a 0.4 s beat passes (`BELL_BEAT`) and the next ask follows. `far-lights`: one helper, `right(line, motion)`, serves all six right-answer lines; the next lights follow the motion (a 0.35 s bob at levels 3–4; the curtain's 0.6 s lift plus the existing look at levels 5, 6 and 8), input opens while the lesson line plays, and the board's instruction is said when the lesson ends, skipped if a hint or wrong-answer line came meanwhile. Departures from the plan: (1) on the last ask or board the line is still heard out, because nothing is held back and the closing line would cut it; (2) `far-lights` level 7 still waits for the line (in parallel with the lift and look), since its next board opens with its own held opening look that would cut it; (3) the voice line `far.curtain` is removed, because saying it at the lift would cut the lesson line. Each browser suite gained one R4 assertion (`far-lights-play`: next pair live within 1.5 s of the right tap at level 3; `woods-batch`: Animal Snack's next ask live within 1.2 s of the bell).
2. **Between boards, optional.** In the eight games above, the next board's fade starts as the praise starts, so the praise plays over the 0.3 s change. Worth doing only if a phone playtest finds the pause between boards slow: the praise is also the beat in which she looks at the finished board (`picture-logic` adds a 1.2 s look on purpose), so this batch waits for a [playtest note](../OBSERVATIONS.md) rather than being built on the rule alone. Bubble Beach adds two: `domino-run`'s ring lines and `fruit-slice`'s `slice.cover-yes`.
3. **`puddle-lens`, `sea-lift` and `sink-float`, the cut lines (not built).** The three with several cut lines each. `puddle-lens`: the pressed answer's name starts and the answer is judged at once, so the verdict's line cuts the name as any new line does; a right answer's line plays over the 0.5–0.6 s beat and the next drop or question, as `far-lights`' lesson lines do, and the instruction said again waits for it. `sea-lift`: the friend's thanks starts, the fade follows the hop rather than the line, and the next friend's ask comes at once; `sealift.aboard` plays over the ride up. `sink-float`: `sink.right` or `sink.surprise` starts and the next thing appears at once (the water's own line and the pair's rule stay held); `sink.salt-right` plays over the lift-out and the shaker's exit. In each, the last ask's line is still heard out before the closing line, as in batch 1.
4. **`melt-freeze`, `tide-line`, `drift-cards` and `fruit-slice`, the cut lines (not built).** One line each: `meltfreeze.same` plays over the thing's exit and the next request; `tl.right` plays over the next strip, and `tl.next-strip` is said when it ends (or dropped, if it adds nothing); `drift.landed` starts and the next card is held, or the next sea shown, at once; `slice.line-yes` plays over the next board's arrival at level 7. The last board's line is still heard out.
