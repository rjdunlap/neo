# Swing Pals level 9: Paint the beat (definition)

**Built:** Level 9, *Paint the beat* ([PR #1999](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/1999)).

*Game `swing-pals` · preschool, pre-K and school · defined 2026-11-22 from [dive slice 5](../deep-dives/swing-pals.md#build-slices-in-order) and the dive's [*Paint the beat* idea](../deep-dives/swing-pals.md#what-makes-sense-here), on the [decision of 2026-11-17](../archive/DECISIONS-2026-11-17.md#games): a creative level, its painting kept only on request through the saved-creations path every other creation uses. It extends the [levels 1–6 definition](swing-pals.md), whose model, pushes and hooks it reuses unchanged unless a line below says otherwise.*

**Fun action and purpose.** A little paint pot hangs under her friend's seat and drips as the swing goes. A paper strip rolls slowly under the swing, so every swing back and forth paints one wiggle. She pushes to make it swing (and wiggle) wider, and taps a hook to hang the rope short, middle or long whenever she likes; each rope paints in its own colour. When she says it is done, the strip turns sideways and is framed as a picture: the short rope's wiggles sit close together, the long rope's spread out. Learning goal: **the beat leaves a record she can compare after the swing stops** (NGSS 3-PS2-2's "observations… as evidence" of a pattern of motion). The same length of paper holds two short-rope wiggles for every long-rope wiggle; a bigger push paints a wider wiggle but never a closer or further one. Nothing is wrong, nothing is scored, and she may keep the painting in the treehouse.

**Overlap checked.** [Rainbow Fingers](../games/rainbow-fingers.md) is free finger painting; here she never paints with her finger, the swing does, and the marks are a measurement of its beat. Levels 3 and 7 of this game compare two ropes side by side while they move; this level compares them on paper after they have moved, so the comparison waits for her. [Draw the Pattern](../games/draw-the-pattern.md) and the music games make a pattern she plans; here the pattern comes from the swing's physics and she only chooses the rope and the push. The pendulum-painting reference is in the [dive](../deep-dives/swing-pals.md#similar-games); its 2D rosette is not used, because the strip under a side-on swing is what turns a beat into evenly spaced wiggles.

## Level ID and play order

- **ID 9**, name *Paint the beat*, plan `mode: 'paint'`, one swing. The dive's *A row of three* sketch takes the next free ID when it is defined.
- **Bands** (`LEVELS` in `logic.ts`): preschool `{ min: 2, max: 9, order: [2, 3, 9] }`, pre-K `{ min: 3, max: 9, order: [3, 7, 9, 4] }`, school `{ min: 4, max: 9, order: [4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9] }`. Lap and toddler are unchanged. `entryLevels` stays unset: every band that lists 9 lists it, so a birthday keeps her level.
- **Placement is intended.** A painting round has no misses and counts hints only from the pause sheet, so a round without help is always smooth and two of them step her on. At preschool it comes after *Short rope, long rope* (she has seen the two ropes, now she keeps a record of them) and is the top. At pre-K it sits between the race (7) and the first matching level (4): the painting makes the beat visible and still before she has to match it. At school it is a calm top after *Twice as quick*, where counting wiggles on the paper is the same 2 : 1 she just matched. Grown-ups can pin it at any of the three bands.

## The model

Everything in the [levels 1–6 model](swing-pals.md#the-model) holds (hooks 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 s; `period(hook)` takes nothing else; waiting pushes from level 2 on; `pushWaits(9)` is true) with these additions.

- **The pot follows the seat.** A drip lands at the seat's sideways position, `seatAt(layout, col, hook, angleAt(…)).x`, so the wiggle's width is the rope's real reach at the swing's height: a long rope at the same height paints wider, and a bigger push paints wider on any rope. The wiggle's **spacing** along the paper is `PAPER_SPEED × period(hook)`, which takes nothing but the hook: weight and height cannot change it (the rule test of the level 1–6 model, now visible on paper).
- **The paper rolls only while the pot drips.** It moves at `PAPER_SPEED` units per second toward her while the swing's height is above `DRIP_MIN` (2°), and stops when the swing is still, so blank paper never runs away and nothing happens while she watches or thinks.
- **Settling.** At this level an unpushed swing settles by the existing `SETTLE` factor (height × 0.85 each swing) until it is below `DRIP_MIN` and stops. This departs on purpose from levels 1–6, where an unpushed swing below its bell keeps its height: those levels have a goal she could lose, and this one has none. The narrowing wiggles are what a real pendulum painting shows, and a settled swing loses nothing she made (the paper keeps it). The dive's "swings that die away and lose a reached goal" stays left out: there is no goal here to lose.
- **Height** runs to `BELL_ONE` (25°), the one-swing cap; there is no bell or leaf goal at this level. A push raises the height by a quarter of the cap, as at level 2, and a tap while a push waits merges into it. The first push from rest lands at once.
- **Changing the rope.** Tapping a hook moves her rope there in 0.4 s. The pot lifts for the move, so the paper keeps a small blank seam; the swing keeps its height and restarts from the back point (`t0` = the next back time on the new hook), so no wiggle is half one rope and half another. Tapping the hook she is already on does nothing but brighten it. A small picture of the new hook (short, middle or long, in that rope's colour) prints at the paper's edge at each seam, so the finished painting labels its sections.
- **Colours.** Each hook has its own paint colour from `palette.ts` (three distinct, readable on the paper's cream): the colour is the rope's, not a choice, so the sections are easy to tell apart when the painting turns sideways.
- **The roll is bounded.** A full roll is `ROLL_SECONDS` (24) of dripping. When the roll is full the paper and the drip stop, the swing goes on swinging, and the pet says "The paper is full! Tap the tick when you're ready." **The paper never ends the round**: only her tick does. 24 s holds about 24 short-rope wiggles or 12 long-rope ones, room for three sections.
- **Section facts.** `sections(trace)` lists the painted sections in order, each with its hook and how many whole wiggles it holds (`wiggles = floor(painted seconds / period(hook))`). `comparesRopes(trace)` is true when two sections on different hooks each hold at least two whole wiggles.
- `logic.ts` gains plain functions: `dripAt(layout, hook, A, t0, t)`, `paperStep(trace, dt, swing)` (advances the paper only above `DRIP_MIN`, stops at a full roll), `changeHook(trace, swing, hook, t)`, `sections(trace)`, `comparesRopes(trace)`, `keptPainting(trace)` and `paintLayout(view)`.

## Screen and controls

- **Layout** (`paintLayout(view)`, built on `swingLayout(1, view)` with the seat raised to leave room for the paper; tested at the four standard views and the upright phone view the upright tests use). The paper is a cream strip on the grass under the swing, as wide as the long rope's reach at 25° each side plus a margin, from just below the pot to the bottom of the play area. Drips land at the strip's top edge; the paper scrolls down toward her, so the newest wiggle is at the top. The **worked example below is computed from the layout code, not measured on a device**: landscape 1024 × 768 with the seat at about y 500 gives a long-rope scale of about 438, a long reach of about ±185, a paper about 430 wide and 200 tall centred under the swing, clear of the pet's corner; at `PAPER_SPEED` 60 the short rope's wiggles are about 60 units apart and the long rope's about 120, so the visible paper shows about three short wiggles or one and a half long ones. Upright on a phone the swing moves right as far as it must for the paper to clear the pet's corner (the one-swing layout already shifts right to clear it), or the scale shrinks; the rule tests below decide, not this example.
- **Targets.** The three hook targets of her swing, as at levels 4–6 (120-unit circles at least 100 units apart, `SHORT_SHIFT` keeping them apart). The **tick** (`RoundButton`, 120 units, bottom right, clear of the paper and the shell's buttons) appears once the paper holds at least one whole wiggle. **Every other tap in the play area pushes**, as at levels 1–2. A tap on a hook or the tick is never also a push; hit areas never overlap (rule test).
- **Touch-down feedback.** A push pops the mitten at once (and, while it waits, the soft ring at the back point, as at level 2). A hook brightens under the finger. The tick sinks under the finger. Each drip makes a tiny plip, pitched by the rope's pentatonic note, quiet enough to be a texture rather than a beat to follow.
- **The reveal.** On the tick the swing settles, the paper rolls back up, turns sideways and shrinks into a wooden frame (as Rainbow Fingers frames a finished painting), time running left to right. Sections are labelled by their little hook pictures. The pet names what the painting shows (see Voice), then the round finishes.
- **Upright on a phone.** The level plays upright: the swing, the paper, the hooks and the tick fit the upright view by the layout test, and the reveal frames the painting inside the view. Phone play comes first ([current focus](../READY.md)); the swing's existing `upright.test.ts` gains level 9.
- Pixi `Graphics` only, colours from `palette.ts`; music `STYLES.paint`, not `silenced`: she is not making the music.

## First round

The round is one painting.

- **Authored start:** a duck on the **long** rope, still, the paper blank. "Push the swing, and the paint pot will paint its wiggle." Her first tap swings the duck and the first drips land; the long rope's wide, slow wiggle starts rolling down the paper.
- **The invitation.** Once the long section holds three whole wiggles, the short hook gently pulses once and the pet says "Try the short rope!" This is an invitation, not a hint: it counts nothing, it does not repeat, and she may ignore it. If she changes to the middle rope instead, the pet's next line names that rope's wiggles instead.
- **The first time a section reaches two wiggles** on each hook, the pet names it once: "Short rope: tight little wiggles!", "Middle rope: in-between wiggles", "Long rope: big slow wiggles!".
- **Done.** She taps the tick whenever she likes after the first whole wiggle. If the painting compares two ropes, the reveal line names it: "Look! The short rope painted two wiggles where the long rope painted one." (or the matching line for short and middle, middle and long). If it holds one rope only: "What a swingy painting!".
- **Later rounds** start on a seeded hook with a seeded friend (the full rider pool); the invitation names a hook she has not painted with yet this round. Friend weight never changes anything, and a heavy friend is not mentioned.

## Accounting, help and finishing

- **Never a miss.** No tap, rope, push, height or painting is wrong; there is no target pattern. The level counts no misses at all.
- **Show a hint** in the pause sheet: the pet gives the next two pushes with her (as at levels 1–3) and counts `hints++` once per request. Asking before the first push also starts the swing. Asking after the paper is full only says "Tap the tick when you're ready."
- **No glow** (nothing to find), and the invitation above is never counted.
- **Finish:** `ctx.finish({ misses: 0, hints, creation: keptPainting(trace), discoveries })`. One ordinary sticker, with or without help. The shell offers to hang the painting; **nothing is kept unless she says so**, through the picture board every other painting uses.
- **The kept painting** is a `PaintingCreation` of `dab` marks (`src/content/creations.ts`): the drips, laid sideways, in their rope colours, resampled evenly in time to at most `PAINTING_MAX_MARKS` (320) and never fewer than `PAINTING_MIN_MARKS`. A full roll is laid out in as many equal rows as keep `aspect` inside the save's 0.5–2 clamp (two rows at the worked example's size), so `cleanPicture` never distorts it. A drip painting is honestly a dotted line; the dab radius is chosen so a short-rope wiggle still reads as a wiggle at the picture board's size, and stays inside `cleanMark`'s 0.006–0.12 bounds. The seam hook pictures are not kept (marks are dabs only). **No save-format change**: dabs with numeric colours already pass `cleanMark`, and the picture board already redraws them.
- **Journal.** A finished round where `comparesRopes(trace)` holds files `swing-pals:longer-slower` once, through `showsSlower` gaining the paint round. A painting on one rope files nothing: the entry files only what the painting showed.
- **Leaving early** keeps nothing and owes nothing, as every round.

## Rule tests (`logic.test.ts`)

- **Spacing is the beat.** For every hook, consecutive wiggle crests on the paper are `PAPER_SPEED × period(hook)` apart; short to long is exactly 1 : 2 and short to middle 1 : 1.5; the spacing is the same at any two heights and with any friend.
- **Width is the push.** The drip's sideways reach is `ropeOf × sin(A)`: it grows with each push, never shrinks from a push, and is wider on a longer rope at the same height.
- **The paper waits for her.** Below `DRIP_MIN` the paper does not move and no drip is added; an unpushed swing settles by `SETTLE` per swing and stops; the paper never moves past a full roll; nothing in `paperStep` or `changeHook` finishes the round.
- **Changing ropes.** A hook change keeps the height, adds a seam with no drips during the 0.4 s move, restarts at the next back point on the new hook, and records the section boundary; re-tapping the current hook changes nothing.
- **Sections and the journal.** `sections` counts whole wiggles per section; `comparesRopes` is true only with two different hooks of two or more wiggles each; one rope, or a second rope with one wiggle, files nothing.
- **The kept painting.** For a one-wiggle painting, a three-section painting and a full roll: at most 320 and at least `PAINTING_MIN_MARKS` dabs; every dab passes `cleanMark` unchanged (a round trip through `cleanCreation` returns the same painting); the aspect is inside 0.5–2; each section's dabs are in its rope's colour; on a full roll every short-rope wiggle keeps at least eight dabs.
- **Layout.** `paintLayout` at every view: the paper is at least 180 units tall and inside `view`; the short rope's wiggle spacing is at least 48 units on the paper; the paper, the swing's arc at 25°, the hooks and the tick clear the pet's corner and the shell's buttons; hooks are at least 100 units apart, the tick is 120 units, and no hit areas overlap.
- **Bands.** The three windows above, with `order` placing 9 where stated; registry, lands, how-to and voice tests pass.

## Ghost finger, how-to card, voice and registration

- **Ghost finger** (`autotouch()` at level 9): taps once to start the swing, taps once a little before each back point until the height reaches the cap, lets the long section paint three wiggles, taps the short hook, pushes up again, lets four wiggles paint, then taps the tick. It never needs a wrong action, because there is none. The same bot drives the scripted play.
- **How-to card** steps `{ from: 9, to: 9 }`: "Push the swing. The paint pot paints a wiggle on the paper as it swings"; "Tap a hook to change the rope. A short rope paints tight wiggles, a long rope wide ones"; "A bigger push paints a wider wiggle, but not a closer one". Finish `{ from: 9, to: 9 }`: "Tap the tick when your painting is done. You can hang it in the treehouse." Note `{ from: 9, to: 9 }`: "Nothing here is ever wrong; the paper only rolls while the swing moves, and only the tick ends the painting."
- **Voice lines** under `swing-pals.*`: `instruct-paint`, `paint-try-short`, `paint-try-middle`, `paint-try-long` (the invitation), `paint-short`, `paint-middle`, `paint-long` (first two wiggles of a section), `paint-full`, `paint-compare-short-long`, `paint-compare-short-middle`, `paint-compare-middle-long`, `paint-done` (one rope). `{pet}` optional. The clip renderer picks them up through the existing `swing-pals` prefix.
- **Describe level:** `describeLevel(9)` "Paint the beat: the swing paints its wiggle on a rolling paper; a short rope paints tight wiggles. Nothing is wrong, and she can keep the painting."
- **Co-play:** one pushes, the other chooses the next rope; on the finished painting, count the wiggles of each rope together. **Off-screen:** a grown-up hangs a paper cup with a small hole (sand or watery paint) from a string over a long sheet of paper and slides the paper slowly under it; then shorten the string and do it again.
- **Rework couplings:** none required. The painting uses the saved-creations path as it is; nothing is unlocked.

## Open decisions

None block the build; each has a default.

1. **Where 9 plays.** Default: preschool, pre-K and school as above. Alternative: preschool and pre-K only, leaving school's top at 8.
2. **Rope colour or her colour.** Default: each rope paints its own colour, so sections read apart. Alternative: she picks one pot colour for the whole painting (three more targets); left out to keep the screen to hooks, push and tick.
3. **The invitation.** Default: once per painting, after three long-rope wiggles, never counted. Alternative: no invitation, leaving the rope entirely to her.
