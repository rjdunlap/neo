# Trackway Tales, levels 1–3 (definition)

Defined 2026-10-19 as the [deep-time study's](../deep-dives/subjects/deep-time.md#build-slices-in-order) slice **D4**, from its [one bounded replay case](../deep-dives/subjects/deep-time.md#trackway-tales-one-bounded-replay-case), its [audience table](../deep-dives/subjects/deep-time.md#an-answer-for-every-audience) (S and M rows) and the [IDEAS row](../IDEAS.md#candidates). Docs only: no game, ID registration, voice line or save field exists yet. **Ready to build** (deep-time D5): every open call below has a default the build can use. The mud, the footprints and the one creature that made them are invented for the island and the study card says so, so no paleontologist is needed before this slice; the study's reviewer decision gates only later real trace, gait or dinosaur content.

**Fun action and purpose:** a line of footprints is pressed into a strip of dried mud by the shore, left by one small two-footed island creature nobody saw. Beside it, two or three **movement cards** each propose a way it could have moved: walked, hopped, walked the other way, stopped to look, walked then hopped. She taps a card and a gray toy creature **replays** that movement on a clear practice strip above the mud, stamping ghost prints as it goes; she can drag it back and forth to scrub. When one card's prints look like the mud's, she sets that card on the **study board** and presses Check: the ghost prints slide down onto the real ones, matching marks light green in order, and the first mark that does not match flashes orange. At level 3 a puddle hides part of the trackway and a **Can't tell** card is right when two movements leave the very same marks. The learning goal is **evidence and inference**: footprints constrain what happened without showing everything, a claim is kept only when its replay reproduces every visible mark, and "the marks can't tell us" is a real answer. No species, speed, chase, mood or age is ever inferred; the pet asks "How did it move?", never "Who was it?"

## Overlap

- **Chain Reaction** owns running a machine and watching the result, then changing a part and running again. Trackway Tales borrows the run-and-compare rhythm (a replay is deterministic and repeatable) but nothing is built: the cards are fixed hypotheses and the comparison is against a record.
- **Story Steps** owns ordering pictures and, at level 11, choosing between two endings from clues. Here nothing is ordered and the evidence is a spatial pattern, not a strip of pictures; "we can't tell" is never an option in Story Steps.
- **Photo Safari** owns noticing a described thing in a scene. Nothing here is photographed or searched for; the whole trackway is in view from the start.
- **Fossil Dig** (deep-time D1, [defined](fossil-dig.md)) owns uncovering one impression and comparing a visible feature to a reference. Trackway Tales never uncovers anything and compares a *sequence* of marks to a *replayed process*; its toe magnifier is the same pattern as Fossil Dig's edge magnifier and the build can share the inset.
- **Stamp Studio's** proposed *Trace Prints* (deep-time D3) makes fresh marks for fun. Here the marks are given and the making is a replay of a hypothesis.
- **Robot Path** owns programs she writes and runs. The movements here are authored cards; she chooses, never composes.
- **Duckling Parade / Roundup** own steering a creature. The ghost creature is scrubbed along a fixed line, never steered.
- **Critter Sort** owns sorting by a rule; **Opposites** owns this way/that way as a word pair. Level 2's direction clue is read from the toes of the marks, not from a word.
- No shipped game replays a proposed process against recorded evidence or accepts "can't tell" as the right answer. A later trackway (a turn, two creatures) is Trackway Tales content, never a new registration.

## The model

- **The strip** is a lane of `cols` numbered columns (4 at level 1, 6 at level 2, 8 at level 3), each column 110 logical units wide, with a **left** and a **right** foot position (40 units above and below the lane's center line). A **mark** is `{ col, side: 'L' | 'R', heading: 1 | -1 }`: `heading` is the way the toe bump points (right, 1, or left, -1). An **observed trackway** is the set of marks in the mud; marks under a **puddle** (a column range, level 3 only) are not observed.
- **A movement** (one card) is `{ id, name, heading, segments: Segment[] }` where a `Segment` is `{ gait: 'walk' | 'hop' | 'stand', count }`. **`stamp(movement, cols)`** walks the columns deterministically from column 0 (or from the last column when `heading` is -1): `walk` leaves one mark per column, alternating sides starting with `L` and continuing the alternation across segments; `hop` leaves an `L` and an `R` mark in the same column, then skips a column; `stand` leaves nothing and advances nothing. Every mark carries the movement's `heading`. The toy model is stated on the study card: "a walk is one foot then the other, a hop is both together, standing still leaves no mark".
- **`fits(movement, observed)`** is true when `stamp(movement, observed.cols)`, with marks in the puddle columns removed, equals the observed set exactly (same columns, same sides, same toe headings). **`firstMismatch(movement, observed)`** is the lowest column (in the movement's heading order) where the stamped and observed marks differ, with the kind of difference: an extra mark, a missing mark or a reversed toe. Fitting is an exact set equality, never a similarity score, and the tolerance is disclosed: a mark is in a column and on a side, nothing finer.
- **`accepted(case)`** is the set of cards for which `fits` holds. The correct answer is that card when exactly one fits, and the **Can't tell** card when two or more fit. It is computed, never authored; the authored cases below are tested to produce the intended set.
- **The maker** is one gray two-footed island creature, "the mud walker", drawn only as a toy silhouette on the study card and as the ghost on the practice strip. It has no species, no name a child must learn and no face to read a mood from.
- **The authored movements:**

  | `id` | Spoken name | `heading` | `segments` | Marks on 4 columns |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `walk` | walked | 1 | walk ×4 | L0 R1 L2 R3 |
  | `hop` | hopped | 1 | hop ×2 | L0 R0 L2 R2 |
  | `walk-back` | walked the other way | -1 | walk ×4 | from the far end: L3 R2 L1 R0, toes left |
  | `hop-back` | hopped the other way | -1 | hop ×2 | L3 R3 L1 R1, toes left |
  | `walk-stand-walk` | walked, stopped to look, walked on | 1 | walk ×4, stand ×1, walk ×4 | the same marks as `walk` on 8 columns |
  | `walk-then-hop` | walked, then hopped | 1 | walk ×3, hop ×3 | L0 R1 L2, then L3 R3 L5 R5 L7 R7 |

  `walk` and `hop` leave the **same number of marks** over the same columns, so a count never gives the answer away.

- **The authored cases** (`{ id, level, observedFrom, puddle?, cards }`; the observed trackway is `stamp(observedFrom)` minus the puddle):

  | Case | Level | `observedFrom` | `puddle` | `cards` | `accepted` | Why it is fair |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `L1-walk` | 1 | `walk` | none | `walk`, `hop` | `walk` | `hop` fails at column 0 with an extra mark; same mark count |
  | `L1-hop` | 1 | `hop` | none | `walk`, `hop` | `hop` | The same pair with the other maker, so the answer is not always the walker |
  | `L2-left` | 2 | `walk-back` | none | `walk`, `walk-back`, `hop-back` | `walk-back` | `walk` fails only by toe heading (the clue is the toes); `hop-back` fails by an extra mark |
  | `L2-right` | 2 | `walk` | none | `walk`, `walk-back`, `hop` | `walk` | The mirror case, so "the other way" is not always right |
  | `L3-pause` | 3 | `walk` | columns 3–4 | `walk`, `walk-stand-walk`, `hop`, Can't tell | `walk`, `walk-stand-walk` → **Can't tell** | Standing still leaves no mark, so the two walkers stamp identical trackways; `hop` fails at column 0 |
  | `L3-switch` | 3 | `walk-then-hop` | columns 3–4 | `walk`, `hop`, `walk-then-hop`, Can't tell | `walk-then-hop` | After the puddle the marks are paired: `walk` fails at column 5, `hop` at column 0. The marks *do* tell, so Can't tell is wrong here |

- **A round** is one case per level, chosen by the seed between the level's two cases, with a seeded card order (left to right) and, at levels 2–3, a seeded choice of which mark starts pre-magnified so one toe is already large. `stamp`, `fits`, the movements and the mark drawings never vary with the seed; no mark is mirrored or moved to make a case.
- **The study board** holds one card slot and, after a correct Check, the stamped line "fits every mark" or "two fit: can't tell". It is in memory for the round only.

## Screen and controls

- **Landscape (1024×768 design area):** the **mud strip** (900×170) across the middle, a dried-mud color with the observed marks (each a 70-unit pad with a toe bump; left-foot marks above the center line, right-foot below); the **practice strip** (900×170) directly above it, pale and clear, with the gray ghost creature parked at its start and, at level 3, the puddle drawn as a blue oval over the same columns on both strips; the **movement cards** (each 190×190, a picture of the movement as three or four little prints and a 100-unit round **Play** button at its bottom) in a row below the mud strip, from 150 units in toward the right so the pet's corner stays clear; the **study board** (260×190, a notebook page with one card slot) at the right end of that row with **Check** (a 104-unit green button, dim until the slot holds a card) beneath it. All targets are at least 100 logical units after scaling.
- **Replay:** tapping a card's Play (or the card itself when no card is lifted) runs the ghost creature along the practice strip over about two seconds, stamping ghost prints with a soft thud per contact (two thuds together for a hop, a quiet rest for a stand); the ghost prints stay until another card plays. Touch-down feedback is the card lifting a little and the ghost taking its first step at once.
- **Scrub:** dragging on the practice strip moves the ghost creature along it, forward or back, stamping and unstamping as it passes each column (custom pointer handling along x, with palm rejection, `pointercancel` and leaving the screen handled; the hand is the creature's position, `lift: 0`). Scrubbing is free and never counted.
- **Choose:** tapping a card lifts it into the study board's slot; tapping another swaps it; tapping the slot returns it. The Can't tell card (level 3, a card showing two little ghosts and a question mark) lifts the same way.
- **Magnifier** (levels 2–3; present at level 1 without being needed): tapping a mark in the mud opens a 300-unit round inset at the top center showing that mark large with its toe bump plainly pointing one way, and the pet says which way. Tapping another mark moves it; tapping elsewhere closes it. Looking is free.
- **Check:** submits the slotted card. The ghost prints of that card slide down onto the mud and are compared mark by mark in the movement's order: each matching mark lights green with a rising pentatonic step; the first mismatching column flashes orange, the pet names the kind of difference (an extra mark, a missing mark or a toe the wrong way), and the card drops back. With the right card, every mark lights green, the study board stamps its line, the ghost creature does one small bow on the practice strip and the round finishes. With Can't tell when two fit, both fitting cards replay side by side on the practice strip, every mark lights green under each, and the board stamps "two fit: can't tell".
- **Portrait:** the two strips keep 170 tall but shrink to 700 wide with 85-unit columns (marks stay 70 units; eight columns still fit), stacked at the top; the cards go in a 2×2 grid (2×3 with Can't tell) beneath, the study board and Check to their right, the pet in its corner. `trackLayout(view, level)` returns every rectangle and a test asserts the sizes at the four views.
- **Sound and music:** a soft thud per ghost contact, pentatonic steps for green marks, a low wooden knock for the orange mismatch, a bow chime at the finish; the land's music keeps playing (not `silenced`, nothing here makes music).

### First rounds

- **Level 1 (`L1-walk`, cards hop then walk left to right):** four marks in the mud, one foot then the other. The pet: "Something walked here before the mud dried. How did it move? Tap a card to see." Tapping the hop card's Play: the ghost hops twice, both feet together, two thuds at a time; two pairs of ghost prints sit above the mud, side by side, plainly unlike the mud's zigzag. Tapping the walk card's Play: the ghost steps left, right, left, right; the ghost prints zigzag like the mud. She lifts the hop card into the slot anyway and presses Check: the hop prints slide down, column 0 lights green on the left mark, then the extra right-foot mark flashes orange, a wooden knock: "Hopping leaves two marks together here. The mud has one," one miss; the card drops back. She lifts the walk card, Check: four green lights in a row, "One foot, then the other. It walked!" The board stamps "fits every mark", the ghost bows. Finish.
- **Level 2 (`L2-left`, cards walk, hop-back, walk-back; the mark in column 5 pre-magnified):** six marks, toes pointing left. The pet: "Which way did it go? Look at the toes." The magnifier already shows column 5's toe pointing left. Tapping the walk card's Play: the ghost walks rightward and its ghost prints zigzag like the mud's. She lifts walk and Checks: the prints slide down; the first mark lights green for its place, then the toe flashes orange, "Same place, but its toes point the other way. Look at the toes," one miss. Tapping walk-back's Play: the ghost starts at the far end and walks left. Lifting walk-back, Check: six greens, "It walked that way, toes first!" Finish.
- **Level 3 (`L3-pause`, cards walk-stand-walk, hop, walk, Can't tell):** eight columns with a puddle over 3–4; marks in 0–2 and 5–7, one foot then the other. The pet: "A puddle covers the middle. Can the marks still tell us how it moved?" Tapping walk's Play: a zigzag all the way, passing under the puddle. Tapping walk-stand-walk's Play: the same zigzag, with the ghost pausing to look around in the puddle, leaving nothing there. Lifting walk, Check: every visible mark lights green, then the walk-stand-walk card lights too and replays beside it: "The walker fits. But look, this one fits too. Standing still leaves no mark. Can we tell which?" No miss; the Can't tell card wiggles. Lifting Can't tell, Check: both fitting cards replay side by side, every mark lights green under each, "Two ways fit these marks. We can't tell which, and that's a real answer." The board stamps "two fit: can't tell". Finish.

## Accounting, help and finishing

- **Never a miss:** any number of replays or scrubs, opening or moving the magnifier, lifting, swapping or returning a card before Check, Check with an empty slot (the slot wiggles, "Pick a card first," nothing counted), repeated taps during feedback (ignored), repeating the instruction, and, at level 3, Checking a card that fits when another card also fits (the pet shows the other fit and the Can't tell card wiggles; this is the teaching moment, not an error, [decision 4](#open-decisions)).
- **A miss:** a Check whose card does not fit (the first mismatching column flashes orange and the pet names the kind of difference), or a Check of Can't tell when exactly one card fits ("Look after the puddle: both feet together. Only one card does that. The marks *can* tell," and the mismatch of each wrong card is shown in turn as the clue).
- **Glow and hints:** after two misses in a round the correct card glows (the one fitting card, or Can't tell) and the informative segment is played: the lowest column where the remaining cards' stamps differ is ringed on both strips and each remaining card replays slowly across just that column; `hints++` once per round. **Show a hint** in the pause sheet (`Scene.pauseActions`) does the same and counts at most once per round; asked again it only repeats the words. Start again rebuilds the same seeded round and keeps misses and hints.
- **Assisted finish:** after the glow, tapping the glowing card and Check finishes the round with the ordinary green sweep. If she keeps checking other cards, each gets its orange clue; nothing locks.
- **Finish:** `ctx.finish({ misses, hints })` after the board is stamped, with or without help: one ordinary sticker from the shell. No creation, discovery or journal entry is filed in this build ([decision 7](#open-decisions)); the study board is a drawing, not a save.

## Rule tests (`logic.test.ts`)

- **Stamping:** `stamp` is deterministic and total for every authored movement on 4, 6 and 8 columns; `walk` alternates sides with no two consecutive marks on one side; `hop` puts exactly `L` and `R` in each hop column and none in the skipped one; `stand` adds no mark and changes no column; `walk` and `hop` leave the same number of marks on the same column span; a heading of -1 reverses the column order and the toe of every mark.
- **Fitting:** for every authored case, `accepted(case)` equals the intended set in the table (exactly one card at levels 1–2, exactly two at `L3-pause`, exactly one at `L3-switch`); `fits(observedFrom, observed)` is always true; `firstMismatch` is defined for every non-fitting card and names a column inside the observed span, never inside the puddle; the mismatch kind at `L2-left` for `walk` is a reversed toe, and at `L1-walk` for `hop` an extra mark.
- **Puddle:** removing the puddle columns from the observation never removes a card from `accepted` that the full trackway accepted, and `L3-pause` becomes ambiguous *because* `stand` leaves no mark, not because of the puddle (the test asserts `walk` and `walk-stand-walk` stamp identically on 8 columns with no puddle).
- **Can't tell:** the correct answer is Can't tell exactly when `accepted(case).length ≥ 2`; across the authored cases it is right in exactly one case and wrong in exactly one, so it is never an always-right button.
- **Rounds:** over 500 seeds per level, the round's case belongs to that level, the card order is a permutation of the case's cards, the pre-magnified mark (levels 2–3) is an observed mark; the same seed gives the same round; both cases and every card order appear across the seeds.
- **Hints:** `informativeColumn(case, remaining)` returns a column where at least two remaining cards stamp differently, and playing it eliminates at least one non-fitting card; following the glow finishes the round.
- **Accounting:** a scripted level-1 round of hop, Check, walk, Check gives misses 1, hints 0; three wrong Checks then the glowing card gives hints 1 and a second help request leaves it at 1; at `L3-pause`, walk, Check, Can't tell, Check gives misses 0; at `L3-switch`, Can't tell, Check gives misses 1; an empty-slot Check changes nothing.
- **Layout:** `trackLayout` at the four views for all three levels: every target ≥ 100 units, the pet's corner clear, both strips inside `view` with eight 70-unit marks fitting their columns, the magnifier inside `view`, the cards not overlapping the strips or the study board.
- **Registry, lands and how-to tests** pass with the new game, including the home land still fitting on one screen at every band ([decision 2](#open-decisions)).

## Check tier

New-game tier at build time (deep-time D5). Typecheck and unit tests (the rule properties above plus registry, lands and how-to tests).
