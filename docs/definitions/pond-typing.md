# Pond Typing 1–6: find the key, then mend a line

KE2 definition, 2026-10-22, branch `claude/define-pond-typing`. Based on the [keyboarding-editing study](../deep-dives/subjects/keyboarding-editing.md) and its [recommendation packet](../deep-dives/subjects/RECOMMENDATIONS.md#keyboarding-and-editing-recommendations). **Docs only; no game, registration, code or device evidence.** The rules below are a proposal for developer review. The build waits on decision 4 (registration and land, [Waiting row](../WAITING-ON-DEVELOPER.md)) and on the ordinary content review of the word pool and lines (decision 5). Every code fact below was read from the tree on this date; nothing was run.

## Scope and identity

Fun action: a big bubble drifts in and **stops**; she presses the key it shows and it pops with a note. Learning purpose: find a letter on a real keyboard, then use the space bar, a capital, a full stop, Backspace and the arrow keys to put text on a line and mend it. Accuracy only: **no timer, no drift, no words-per-minute, no boss list** for a child (study decision 3, lean). The game is a **keyboard-only couch game** (`inputs: ['controller']`), offered on the couch's *Laptop with keyboard* setting; on a TV with a controller it cannot be played and is not offered (see Registration). Names, words and lines are never stored: the typed line lives for one round.

Overlap test: Word Monsters owns choosing and placing letters into a word on glass; Letter Trails owns forming letters; `spelling-sentences` (unstarted) owns which letters, capitals and stops as rules. Pond Typing's rule is **the letters to press are shown or spoken** (copying), so it never tests spelling. Level 4 and 5 teach the *key press* for a capital and a stop only; the rule stays with `spelling-sentences`. Excluded: KE1 Mend the sign (Read & Do), KE3, KE5 key aliases on other games, KE6 key smash, KE7 grown-up cards, an on-screen QWERTY, the system keyboard or any DOM text input in a child's game, a grown-up drift mode, and any posture or finger-placement advice (needs a named reviewer; not drafted).

> **Built 2026-10-24: levels 1–3** (typed-letter channel, couch registration, the Bubble Pond course). The land is **Treehouse (Painted Canyon)**, not Story Grove: Story Grove failed its drawing test at school landscape with 13 games. Letter groups are ABCD to UVWX and WXYZ (four consecutive letters each). Levels 4–6, pictures for words and the "Show the key" toggle are not built.

## The typed-letter channel

Today `CouchInput` listens only for `code` in `ArrowLeft/Right/Up/Down, Enter, Space, Backspace` (and, in couch play, `Escape, KeyW, KeyS`) and skips events with Meta, Ctrl or Alt; no letter key reaches a game. The build adds **one optional field to `CouchControls`**, `typed: string[]` (the letters or characters pressed this frame, in order, from `KeyboardEvent.key`), and a **Pond-Typing-only opt-in** so no other game, and no touch play, ever sees it:

- Letters are read from `key`, never `code` (a Dvorak or AZERTY player presses the letter they mean). `key` values that are a single character are accepted; `Shift` is tracked for the sticky-Shift rule below; Caps Lock is treated as Shift for letters (the `key` already arrives capital). Space and Backspace keep their current handling; the arrow keys are `direction`.
- W and S stay the second player's keys everywhere else. While Pond Typing is the round's game, W and S arrive as **typed letters, not paddles**, and the second player's controls are idle (the game is `play: 'turns'`, one typist at a time). The existing `typing(e)` guard (form fields) stays.
- Meta, Ctrl and Alt chords are never intercepted, so the browser's shortcuts keep working; this is why a baby cannot be made safe to mash on (KE6 stays unproposed). Auto-repeat events are dropped so a held key is one press. A press during the scene's first 0.35 s is ignored like every other input.
- A rule test pins that a game without the opt-in sees `typed` empty, and that the existing paddle games' key handling is unchanged. `Game.control()` receives `typed`; `controllerPlays`, how-to and pause (Esc) are unchanged.

## The bubble, the coach and accounting

Scene: a pond with a lily-pad stage. One large bubble (at least 200 logical units across) holds the target drawn big; the pet beside it says the letter, word or line (`ctx.instruct` for the goal, `ctx.say` for the rest). Below, a strip of key caps from `prompts()` lights the key to press (the coach), hidden when the player turns **Show the key** off in the pause menu (default on, study lean). Pop: pentatonic note from `src/audio/notes.ts` and the bubble sails off; the next bubble drifts in and stops. A round is **eight bubbles at level 1, six at level 2, five at 3–6** (a line counts as one bubble at levels 5–6); the last pop starts the pet's read-back and the finish.

- **Wrong key:** the bubble wobbles and the pet names what was pressed (“That was M. Find D.”). The first wrong key on a bubble is free; each later wrong key on that bubble counts **one miss**. After two wrong keys on one bubble its key glows once (one hint, counted once per round). The pause menu's *Show a hint* (`Game.askForHint`, `COUCH_INFO.hint`) glows the key too and counts the same hint.
- **Never misses:** Backspace, the arrow keys, Shift, Space where Space is not the target, and any key at the very start of a bubble within 0.35 s. Space where Space is wanted is a letter-like target; a Space where a letter is wanted is a wobble with “that was space; the next letter is r”.
- **Rewards:** one sticker per completed round (through the shell); nothing drifts away, no bubble times out, no life, no timer. Couch rules allow a personal best in its own unit: *letters first try*, calm, shown only as a number.

## The six levels

All levels are `school`. A round's content comes from the seed; the sets below are fixed. Case: levels 1–3 accept a letter with or without Shift and show it in lower case; level 4 onward case matters where a capital is asked.

| Level | Name | First round | What she decides | Mistakes and hints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | *One letter* | Eight single letters from a seeded alphabetical group of four consecutive letters (the seed picks the group: A–D, E–H, I–L, M–P, Q–T or U–Z; a round uses one group), each its own bubble, key lit | Which key is this letter | As above: wobble and a name; glow on the second miss on one bubble |
| 2 | *My name* | The player's name from the couch save (`names[0]`, normalized as Letter Trails does: NFD, strip accents, upper-case, A–Z only, at most 40 letters; falls back to the pet's name, then `PIP`), a letter at a time; then the name read back with a picture of the pet | The letters of a name, in order | As level 1; the letters must be pressed in order, an out-of-order correct letter is a wobble |
| 3 | *Words and a space* | A picture and its word as a bubble per letter group: a three-to-five-letter word (“hat”), then in later bubbles a two-word phrase (“red hat”) where the bubble shows a **space bar** key cap between the words | Letters in order, and the space | A space where a letter belongs is a wobble (“that was space; the next letter is r”); a letter where a space belongs says “press the space bar” |
| 4 | *A big letter* | The first word of a short line and a name begin with a capital: the bubble shows a big letter and the Shift key lights beside the letter key; Shift is **sticky for exactly one letter** (tap Shift, then the letter), and a held Shift chord is also accepted | When a letter is big | A small letter where a capital is asked: the bubble wobbles once (“it starts with a big letter”); **the first miss is free**; two glow the Shift key |
| 5 | *A line and a stop* | A short line typed on a visible line, e.g. “Pip has a hat.”, capital first, spaces, a full stop at the end; a picture is revealed as the line completes and the pet reads it | Capital, spaces and a stop in one line | A wrong letter **appears** on the line and **Backspace removes it, never a miss**; a wrong *kind* of key counts as in levels 1–4; the picture reveals only on the exact line |
| 6 | *Mend it* | A finished line has **one wrong letter** in the middle (“Pip has a hqt.”); the arrow keys move a caret, Backspace removes the letter before it, a typed letter goes in at the caret | Where the caret is, what to remove, what to put back | Caret moves and removals are never misses; typing a wrong letter first is free; two wrong removals (removing a correct letter) glow the wrong letter once |

Level 3's two-word phrases and level 5's lines come from the reviewed pools below. Level 6 reuses level 5's lines with one seeded letter swapped (never the first letter, never the stop).

## Word, name and line pools (for ordinary content review)

- **Words (level 3 and 4):** `hat, cat, dog, pig, hen, cow, bee, net, sun, mud, log, fox, pip, bug, nest, frog, duck, pond, leaf, moon, rain, cake, boat, tree`. Pairs for the phrase bubbles: `red hat, big dog, wet duck, hot sun, green frog, soft mud, tall tree, little bee`. Words are picture-backed objects and animals; no word is a name, a brand or a slang term, none has an unusual spelling a copier could not copy letter by letter (spelling is not tested).
- **Lines (levels 5–6):** `Pip has a hat.`, `The duck is wet.`, `A frog sat on a log.`, `We ate a big cake.`, `The sun is hot.`, `A bee is in the tree.`, `Mud is soft.`, `The moon is up.` Each line has at most one capital (its first letter, plus “Pip” where named) and ends in a full stop; each has a picture the build draws from existing code art.
- **Names:** the player's own typed or chosen couch name (never checked), the pet's name, or `PIP`. The game never judges a name; a name with no A–Z falls back as above.

## Layout, art and sound

The couch is a plain DOM overlay for its chrome; the game's stage is Pixi. Targets are not touch targets (couch stages take no pointer input), but sizes still honor the shell's 100-unit rule for any control drawn on screen. Landscape (1024×768 design area, expanded): the bubble is centered at about x512, y300 (200–260 across); the line (levels 5–6) is a single row of letter boxes 72 units wide centered at y560, wrapping to a second row at 14 letters; the key-cap strip runs along y680 and is hidden when **Show the key** is off; the pet sits in the bottom-left clear corner (x0–160, y500–768), the picture reveal at the right of the bubble. Portrait: the same stack with the bubble at y260, the line at y620 and the strip at y1100; resizing mid-round keeps the bubble, line, caret and position. A build asserts no overlap of bubble, line, strip, picture, pet and navigation bounds at the harness's portrait and landscape sizes and takes one portrait screenshot. This is a **layout specification, not a screenshot or fit**. Art is code-drawn (a bubble, key caps from `prompts()`, lily pads, existing critter drawings); sound is synthesized with the pentatonic notes; meaning survives sound off. Music: a quiet style (`silenced(STYLES.quiet)` if the game becomes a registry-tested “makes the music” game; otherwise the island loop).

## Voice, how-to and bot

Planned ids, not yet in `voice-script.ts`: `pt.title` “Pond Typing.”; `pt.one` “Press the key on the bubble.”; `pt.name` “Type your name, one letter at a time.”; `pt.word` “Type the word. Use the space bar between words.”; `pt.capital` “Start with a big letter. Tap Shift, then the letter.”; `pt.line` “Type the whole line. Finish with a full stop.”; `pt.mend` “One letter is wrong. Use the arrows to find it and mend it.”; `pt.that` “That was {key}. Find {target}.”; `pt.spacewant` “Press the space bar.”; a read-back line per word and line (plain `ctx.say`). The couch how-to lives in `src/couch/catalog.ts` (`controls` rows: *Type the letter on the bubble* — any letter key; *Space* — the space bar; *Backspace* — take a letter out; *Left/Right* — move the caret; *Shift* — a big letter; *Esc* — pause: show the key, hint). The island how-to card in `src/content/howto.ts` is required by the registry test and is **per-level `LevelLine` text with no level-relative wording**; because the game is couch-only there is no touch ghost finger, so the card uses the controller demo (`demoFor()`), and the build confirms that path accepts it.

Bot (`autoplay`/`control()` with a public `finished` flag): levels 1–3 type the lit key, one press per frame with a 0.1 s gap; level 4 presses Shift then the letter; level 5 types the line exactly; level 6 types the line with the wrong letter, then moves the caret with the arrow keys, Backspace removes the wrong letter and the correct one is typed. A seeded variant makes one wrong key per round (never on level 6's mending) so the free first miss and the glow are exercised.

## Rule tests the build needs

Every level-1 group is four consecutive letters of the alphabet and no round mixes groups; each bubble has a lit key unless the player turned the coach off; the bot completes any seed at every level; a name normalizes to A–Z with the Letter Trails function and falls back to the pet's name, then `PIP`; level 2 enforces order; the word and line pools hold only the listed entries (no entry outside, no duplicate in a round); the space step appears only in two-word rounds; a capital is required only for first words and names; sticky Shift applies to exactly one letter; the first wrong key on a bubble is free, later ones count one miss, the glow lists only that bubble's key, hints count once; Backspace, caret moves and Shift never count; the caret index is always within the line; every level-6 line has exactly one wrong letter and exactly one mend path (move, remove, type) that the bot finds; typed letters are read from `key` and Meta/Ctrl/Alt chords are ignored; `typed` stays empty for every other game; descriptions for grown-ups name no grade and no speed (“finds keys”, “types a name”, “mends a line with the arrow keys”).
