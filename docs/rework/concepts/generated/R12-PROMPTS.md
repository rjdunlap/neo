# R12 exact generation prompts

Built-in `image_gen.imagegen`; model undisclosed by the tool. All calls used `transparent_background: false` (white fallback). Repository images are untouched original PNGs.

Base candidates 1–3 used [concept sheet](../art-directions-2026-10-16.png) then [approved glider](glider-base-3.png) as explicit references. Candidate 3 was refined with candidate 3 as its sole reference to add second eye highlights; that refinement is base 4. All moods, curled pose and portrait used only [chosen base 4](bramble-base-4.png). No runner-up kept, no rejected mood edits and no missing assets.

## base1

```text
Style: warm children's storybook illustration for a toddler's iPad game, matching the "Illustrated adventure" column of the attached concept sheet. Chunky, rounded, friendly creature with baby proportions: big head, big glossy near-black eyes with a large white highlight at the upper left and a small one at the lower right, a small dark nose, a short gentle closed smile, soft pink blush under the eyes, and a warm cream belly or muzzle. A closed dark plum-ink outline (#2B1B2E) goes all the way round the creature, slightly heavier on the outside edge and thinner for the few inner lines. Soft cel shading with light from the upper left: one base colour, one soft shadow tone and one highlight per material, and gentle gradients only inside shapes. No cast shadow, no ground, no texture noise, no realistic fur, no 3D render, no plush or felt, no pixel art, no sparkles or motion lines, no text.

Format: exactly one creature and nothing else, on a perfectly flat plain white background (#FFFFFF) with no shadow, gradient or paper texture (or a transparent background if available). Landscape 1536x1024. The creature fills about 85% of the width, with a clear empty margin on every side; no part touches or crosses the image edge. The body faces the viewer's right. A relaxed neutral pose. Keep small gaps between parts that would move separately, such as legs, tail, ribbons or leaves, wherever the anatomy allows.

Draw a new creature for the same cast as the attached approved glider, in exactly the same style, outline and shading: a small round hedgehog in a gentle three-quarter view turned toward the viewer's right. Its "spikes" are soft, rounded tufts, like a fluffy hairdo, covering the back and the top of the head in berry purple (#9A6CF0, shadow #6C45C4). A cream face and belly, small round ears, a small dark nose, big glossy eyes, blush and a happy closed smile. Tiny legs set a little apart, tiny hands held in front, a stub tail. Nothing sharp anywhere. Relaxed neutral pose, standing on nothing.

Reference 1 is the concept sheet's middle Illustrated adventure style, reference 2 the approved glider. All purple tufts must end in broad ROUND lobes, never points or sharp quills. Exactly two small hands in front and two short feet, all visibly distinct. Tail only a tiny stub. Eyes have TWO WHITE highlights each. Opaque plain white fallback, no ground shadow, clear empty margins all around, landscape 1536x1024.
```
