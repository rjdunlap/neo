# Needs a person or a device

**Parked (the developer, 2026-11-09).** There is no iPad yet and the developer's daughter is not old enough to play yet, so checks that need an iPad, a child, a TV or a real controller are not being collected. The 616 items listed here up to that date are in [the archived backlog](archive/DEVICE-CHECKS-2026-11-09.md), grouped by game, for the day a device and a child are at hand.

Until the developer says otherwise:

- **Do not add items** here or to the archive, and do not write "device check open", "not judged on an iPad", "person check remains" or similar into roadmap rows, READY lines, verification entries, coverage rows, batch plans or reports. It is true of everything built so far, so it is said once, here.
- **Nothing waits on a parked check.** A slice is not blocked, gated or given status `person` because an iPad or a child has not tried it. Build on the default its dive or definition gives.
- **A question only a child's play can answer** stays in the game's own dive or definition, as one line where a builder would read it. It is not copied anywhere else.
- **Older text** that says a device, person or child check "is open" or "remains" means this page: parked, not pending.
- **Do not claim what was not seen.** Headless Chrome still establishes nothing about a real screen, speaker, hand or child; a doc must not say it did.

## What the phone playtest can answer now

The [current focus](ROADMAP.md#direction) is a good phone experience, played by two grown-ups (the developer and their wife) on their own phones. These are the questions only a real phone answers. An answer arrives as a playtest note in [OBSERVATIONS](OBSERVATIONS.md); delete the line here when it is answered. Keep this list to about ten lines: a phone slice may replace its own line, and nothing else is added.

- **A phone is Today's six.** Does "Who's playing?" open the six, does a new player hatch without turning the phone, are the cards and level lines readable, do Play all and the next-of-six tile carry a sitting through, does the notes sheet scroll with the keyboard up, and does Copy all notes reach the clipboard (Safari, Chrome and the installed app)?
- **Upright games, after each [batch](definitions/phone-portrait.md#game-batches).** Does each newly upright game read and play comfortably with a thumb (a 100-unit target is about 51 CSS pixels; a grown-up board's 64-unit minimum is about 33), does turning mid-round keep the round, and does a not-ready card's turn badge make sense?
- **Batch 2 in particular:** Hat Band, Crossword, Picture Logic, Tile Pairs, Gem Swap and Lantern Lights upright; is Hat Band's encore finishable to its sticker?
- **Edges of a real screen.** Does a notch, rounded corner or home indicator cover anything (the safe-area insets are only faked in Chrome)?
- **Sound.** Does the first touch wake sound, does it play through the silent switch (iOS 16.4 and later), and how do the phone's own speech voices and the 215 rendered clips sound? The rest of the script is rendered once someone has listened ([a better voice](ROADMAP.md#a-better-voice)).
- **Installed app.** Add to Home Screen on iPhone and Install app on Android; does the installed app turn, work offline, and pick up an update after it is closed and reopened?
