## Answer these first

Six calls that unblock the most work, chosen 2026-10-17 by what waits on them; everything else is in the table below. Each links to its row's source.

1. **Who reviews content?** About fifteen rows below wait on "name the reviewer" (genetics, zoo animals, deep time, cooking, movement, self-care, the sensitive history subjects, word lists, science and scale facts). One standing rule answers them all: for example, the developer reviews factual content before a build and no outside reviewer is required, or a named person per area.
2. **A middle band (about 10 to 14) and where parents' content lives**, with whether Wonder Woods grows before a band above it. Every subject packet's older-placement row and every "8–11" idea waits on this one call.
3. **Welcome a New Friend's hello-song level** (RW07's `song` step): play Song Maker level 2 as a stated exception, or build a new in-range compose level. This is on the creature-world critical path.
4. **The physics approach** for ropes, blocks, drawn lines and circle piles: Vine Snip, Block Topple, Draw a Ramp and Pond Merge wait on it (chunks 25 and 27).
5. **A second seat's contract and competition on the island**: Secret Code's maker and breaker, Row Together and the family table (chunks 16 and 23) wait on it.
6. **May the island ship AI voice clips?** Better voice V1–V3 (chunk 29) waits; V0 does not.

Also wanted by the developer but waiting on a name: **which language first, and its reviewer** (chunk 15, stage 1 is defined).
