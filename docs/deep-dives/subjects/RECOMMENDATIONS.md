# Subject study recommendations

The definition packets recommended by the [subject studies](../subjects.md), one section per researched subject. They lived in the roadmap until 2026-10-17, when the developer asked for the roadmap to be shortened; the [roadmap's table](../../ROADMAP.md#subject-study-recommendations) lists them. They are **proposals and docs-first definitions, not a dispatch queue**: a routine reaches one only through a `define` row in the roadmap's [Next up](../../ROADMAP.md#next-up) or when the grown-up selects it. A new subject study adds its section here, at this heading level, and a row to the roadmap's table.
