## What fits

**Seed Travelers earns a new interaction:** inspect a structure, place the seed at a carrier, trigger the event and compare transport. Mail Carrier/Treasure Map move a chosen destination through a route; this game asks how structure and a carrier interact. ## Seed Travelers: first game's proposed ladder

**Common first-round model:** a tabletop diorama; the departure patch is on the left, arrival tray on the right. Enlarged dandelion fruit/plume and burdock burr are *stylized dispersal units*, not drawn as bare seeds. Their detailed biology/art needs review. The furry carrier is a fictional passing critter, not a pet-care instruction. Show origin, carrier contact, transit and release. The trial's “arrived” means reached that tray in this toy; it says nothing about germination or the probability of establishment.

**Planning IDs 1–7 below are provisional, not saved levels.** Proposed windows: toddler 1–2, preschool 1–3, pre-K 2–4, school 3–7; only define/build 1–3 first. No grownup window or middle band is approved. Older route puzzles need their own complete packet and registration/land decision. Keep children’s controls at least 100 logical units after scaling; grownup-only controls may use 64.

| Rung / first round | What the player decides | Experiment, feedback and help | Meaningful future rule check / cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — A seed rides the breeze** · T/P | One plume already placed, one large breeze control. Tap to move it to the tray; Replay resets it; Finish becomes available after one complete observed trip | Repeat freely; no wrong choice, miss or hint. The picture trail and arrival remain until replay/finish | One source becomes exactly one arrival; replay restores the same initial state; no second award from repeated runs. | **2 — A hitchhiker** · T/P/K | One burr beside one furry carrier. | **3 — Try both** · P/K/S entry | Two units, two carriers. L: carefully authored case pool/review |

**Completion/accounting:** rung 1 finishes after an observed trip and explicit Finish; rung 2 after its trip; rung 3 after both deliveries, with requested assisted completion available. Rungs 1–3 have no misses: testing is the learning action. Count explicit requested demonstrations/assists as hints once per distinct support task, never count repeated free playback as hints. A supported solution earns the normal one sticker through `ctx.finish({ misses, hints })`. Later evidence checks distinguish a deliberate answer from a prediction; define their exact miss/help policy before implementation. No hints simply for opening the how-to card.

**Model boundary:** a deterministic table drives both the visible result and rule tests. It deliberately omits turbulent wind, dispersal distances, mortality and germination probabilities. Do not sell its binary outcomes as measured biology. Expand toward variability only with data and explicit uncertainty, not invented percentages. Tests prove internal rules, never biological truth; review the species facts and corresponding voice/art before release.

## Experience and presentation

- **First unsupported action:** rung 1 opens with the plume and oversized breeze control already visible; a touch compresses its bellows immediately, then the plume lifts. Rung 2's burr brightens on touch-down before moving to the carrier. Use `onTap`; later placement can offer tap-select/tap-place beside standard drags. No blow detection or microphone.
- **Concrete feel:** palette cream plume threads fan outward around a brown fruit; a short synthesized airy noise accompanies a sideways drift, then a soft landing squash. A fuzzy critter briefly shows the burr enlarged at contact and release. The plume, trail and captions/shapes communicate without color or sound alone. Never make a moving seed a required tiny target.
- **Replay variety:** seed the scene dressing and left/right arrangement only where reading direction remains clear. Change one biological variable at a time. Seed identities, compatibility and trial records cannot silently change on replay or resize; reveal variety through a reviewed species pool later.
- **Thinking versus precision:** choosing a carrier and explaining a comparison is the challenge. Taps snap to generous regions; no aiming, rapid swiping, timed catches or failed fine-motor drops. Keep the guide pet's lower-left space free in both orientations.
- **Support and stopping:** permanent replay/reset, instruction repeat on the guide, one useful demonstration through the real input path and an assisted ending; no abandoned organism, progress bar that drains, hunger or requirement to return. Use shell celebration/home/replay and cleanup scene-owned animation.
- **Family role:** prediction chooser and carrier operator swap after one scene, with either person allowed to watch. No compulsory private clues, two-player controller system or seat-credit change in the first touch slice.
- **Off-screen companion:** connect to the existing off-screen-companions study. Candidate is a shared “what changed / what do we wonder?” drawing or observation, not a printed seed-collection, growing or wildlife-feeding recipe. Define practical materials/access/safety with a named reviewer before publishing a real activity card.

Person/device review still needs to establish whether young players connect the structure with the journey, understand replay and Finish, and can distinguish model outcomes from universal claims. Try rungs 6–7 with older people before expanding their content pool; bot completion is no proof of fun, inference skill or transfer.
