## What fits

The first unsupported action stays the one she knows: uncover a friend or feed it. Touch-down still moves/glows immediately; speech follows without holding input hostage. An English-first line gives away the referent, so **stage 1 is exposure, never an independent Portuguese comprehension test**. More names, quicker speech, a bigger memory deck or an accuracy score would not fix that.

Receptive play later should let the language make a difference: a friend requests one of two familiar things, and an optional English explanation/picture cue always remains available. That changes the stage-1 contract and needs its own older-band mode definition/decision; it cannot be smuggled into `language2`. Avoid ranking languages, flags as language identities, assumed family relationships, national costumes as proof of culture, generic Arabic, or judging an unrecorded spoken answer. Family/community material must be supplied or checked by the people concerned. No camera, microphone, online chat or remote service is proposed.

## Experience and presentation

- Keep familiar sound/motion and immediate feedback. One second-language word follows English; repetition does not double every counting step. Never require a child to wait for speech to keep exploring.
- A missing or rejected pt-PT voice leaves English play usable. The grown-ups' page explains the unavailable word voice; it does not claim pronunciation was checked merely because a voice exists.
- Replay variety comes from existing seeded animals/scenes and meaningful request contrasts. No daily word, streak, urgency or disappearing vocabulary.
- Navigation and how-to remain English and spoken. A later bilingual text card must fit 100-unit controls and reserve pet/corner space in portrait and landscape; extra text is not permission to shrink.
- Off-screen: family point-and-name proposal above, no objects required and no printable translation supplied. Refusal, pointing, gesture, silence and mixed languages all remain welcome.

## Decisions and acceptance gates

1. **Already decided:** European Portuguese first (`pt-PT` vocabulary/spelling/device voice); English then second-language word, per profile, default off; whole-voice switching only a later older-band option. Do not reopen those decisions or select a reviewer here.
2. **Person gate:** developer names the family reviewer ([Waiting](../../WAITING-ON-DEVELOPER.md)). They review every Portuguese entry/line, meaning, article, number/gender form, spelling, context and acceptable alternatives, with version/date and explicit acceptance. A vendor course, web source, assistant or generic native-speaker label cannot supply this approval.
3. **Device gate:** actual iPad listening by that reviewer accepts each shipped pronunciation at real game rate/pitch and voice identity, including offline/replay/interruption. Installed `pt` or `pt-BR` is not acceptance of `pt-PT`; no same-language-prefix fallback across varieties. Evidence belongs in [DEVICE-CHECKS](../../DEVICE-CHECKS.md), with observations only after a person actually performs it.
4. **Later policy/placement:** request-only/comparison/whole-voice modes need separate older-band definitions, decisions where necessary, current creature-world contract audit and actual land/layout evidence. Stage 1's setting must not silently become one of those modes.
5. **Later languages/content:** no Arabic variety/script conventions, French/Spanish variety, reviewer or ordering chosen here. Arabic needs reviewed RTL/bidi/shaping and speech acceptance before written content. Songs/rhymes/phonics need their own language-specific source/rights/cultural review. Language is not nationality or one uniform culture.
