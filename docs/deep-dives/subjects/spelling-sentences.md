# Spelling and sentences: hear a word, build it; prepare a sign for its reader

*Subject `spelling-sentences` · 2026-10-22 · worker `01a16686-48ca-7bc3-affa-74cfe99a5bde` · `codex/spelling-sentences-research` · [PR #838](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/838). One subject, research and proposals only. Audiences L/T/P/K/S/U/M/G. Nothing implemented or played.*

**Define first SS1 Capital and stop**, a Read & Do extension taking the [keyboarding study's KE3 handoff](keyboarding-editing.md#candidates). Prepare a short sign with a capital at its beginning and a full stop at its end, then send it to the pet to act out. This is a convention for a reader, not a claim that an unpunctuated sign has no meaning. Replay can act out either version freely. A bounded definition may proceed now; implementation waits for reconciliation with Read & Do's queued game dive (saved IDs, ranges and the neighbouring Make/Mend the sign proposals), a reviewed sentence/feedback table and proved touch layout. No new game, keyboard, free text, land or registration is proposed for this first slice.

## First mode's proposed ladder: SS1 Capital and stop

**These A–C labels are study steps, not saved level IDs.** Definition writes one bounded packet for all three; the Read & Do dive later assigns IDs and order without renumbering built levels 1–3. Proposed pre-K A–B, school A–C; no change to current ranges is authorized.

A sign stands beside an actor. Each round uses a reviewed three-word clause such as *the duck jumps*. Its speaker reads each word; a free preview acts out its content even before editing. The sign's first word is a large toggle tile, and the full stop is a separate 100-unit stamp placed into the final socket. Send checks only the convention requested in that step. Printed glyph size is independent of hit size. The sign is not decorated with the answer. Punctuation is named explicitly; TTS prosody is not the rule checker.

| Step | First round / decision | Mistakes and hints | Rules to test in a future build | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A, *Start the sign* | `the duck jumps.` already ends correctly; tap *the* to choose `The`, then Send | Only a failed Send is a miss; explain where a sentence starts. Editing and replay are free | End mark remains fixed; only the initial letter changes; every reviewed clause has one accepted capital state | First-word toggle and a visible pressed state |
| B, *Finish the sign* | `The cat sleeps` already starts correctly; drag or tap the stop into its end socket | Send without the stop: gentle boing and name the end; wrong drop returns freely, not an error | Initial capital fixed; exactly one end socket; duplicate punctuation impossible | Stop stamp, drag cleanup and tap alternative |
| C, *Ready for a reader* | `the cat jumps` needs both edits; either order, then Send | Name the first missing convention consistently, leaving edits intact | Both orders succeed; all intermediate states replay safely; unchanged words and actor meaning; no exact gesture sequence required | Combine the same two actions, not a new editor |

Proposed complete round: three seeded signs, all supported completion available, one shell sticker through `ctx.finish`. Two failed Sends on one sign glow the needed control once and count one hint; requested help counts once per sign and shows the same action; help after the glow adds no second charge. After help she can apply the shown change and Send. A supported-finish button may apply the remaining convention with an explicit hint charge if none was charged; **definition must settle its wording and exact accounting**, including `askForHint` and how supported rounds interact with adaptation. No penalty for preview, listening, toggling back, dragging, cancellation, undo or an empty gesture. Conventional correctness, not fast completion, is the finish condition.

Future rule tests enumerate the small edit-state space, prove each sign completable and hint-directed, preserve content/actor mapping and check finite alternative acceptance and one hint per sign. Seeded selection must cover the reviewed pool without one fixed first answer; replay never changes the puzzle. Definition must specify portrait/landscape at 100-unit targets and pet/corner clearance, voice-script IDs, the how-to and ghost-finger bot. Checks then follow the new-interaction tier, not today's docs tier.

## Experience and presentation

First unsupported action: tap the first word; its tile depresses immediately and its first glyph changes size/case, then the pet names the convention. Full-stop stamping has a soft pentatonic click, not a victory explosion. Send lets the actor perform, with a short read-back and a comfortable ending. Replays vary the actor/action from a tiny reviewed pool rather than length, speed or obscure words. A compare-before/after view never calls the original unreadable. Co-play/off-screen is SS6; WC's reader-question loop stays separate.
