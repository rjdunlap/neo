# Networks and digital citizenship: how a message travels, who can see it on the way, and how to be kind with it

**Subject:** `networks-citizenship` · **Audiences:** L, T, P, K, S, U, M and G as [defined in the tracker](../subjects.md#the-audiences) · **Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Session:** `claude/subject-networks-citizenship`, [PR #577](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/577). The tracker row names "networks, the internet and being kind and safe online, taught with stories and unplugged games (nothing connects to a real network)".

**Research and proposals only.** The island has no network, no chat and no camera, and this study keeps it that way: every "message" is a picture one creature sends another across the island, carried hop by hop by friends. That one fiction carries all three halves of the subject. **How a message travels** (a chain of carriers, more than one way round, a blocked path, a big picture cut into numbered pieces that arrive mixed, a lost piece asked for again) is the networks half. **Who can see it on the way** (a postcard is read by everyone it passes, an envelope is not, a lock only the receiver's key opens, a picture of a friend needs the friend's yes) is the privacy half. **How to be kind with it** (a picture makes the receiver feel something, an unkind one has a reply that helps, a surprise on the screen is shown to a grown-up) is the citizenship half. Neighbouring studies already hold the pieces this one must not duplicate: the [how-computers-work study](how-computers-work.md) owns codes and binary (Picture Bytes, Memory Desk), the [social-emotional study](social-emotional.md) owns feelings, acted requests and different accounts (Two Viewpoints), the [data-and-AI-literacy study](data-ai-literacy.md) owns examples and model limits, the [community-civics study](community-civics.md) owns Mail Carrier's helpers (*Who can help?*), the `media-literacy` row owns "is it real?", and the `safety` row (needs an expert reviewer) owns anything about contact with people who mean harm. What is left, and claimed here, is **the relay**: what happens to a message between the sender and the receiver.

## What fits

- **One fiction, no network.** Creatures carry pictures between stumps. Nothing connects, nothing is typed, no one is online. The word "internet" appears only in grown-up text and only from school.
- **The relay is a new action, not a Mail Carrier mode.** The overlap test: Mail Carrier's decision is *which house* (the address, read from a key); the relay's decision is *which friend next* (the route), and from level 5 *which piece, in which slot, and again*. Same woods, same Hazel, different decision. A new game, placed beside Mail Carrier if the land fits.
- **Pieces, not bytes.** The picture splits into three or four numbered pieces; the how-computers-work study's Picture Bytes owns the encoding. Here a piece is just a piece of the picture, which is what 1B-NI-04 asks and what Puzzle Pals' render-texture pieces already draw.
- **The transmission principle drawn, not stated.** Every carrier a postcard passes gets a thought bubble of it; an envelope gives them a blank bubble. That is the whole privacy lesson at 7, and it is a picture, not a rule.
- **Kindness through the social-emotional contract.** An unkind picture arrives, the pet's face shows a feeling (never prescribed as the right one), and the helpful choices are a kind reply, a hug, or showing a grown-up. No bully character, no punishment, no "report" button that pretends to work.
- **Help is a grown-up, said once.** The spoken line "if a picture surprises you, show a grown-up" belongs in the card and the story, never as a quiz.
- **What does not fit.** Strangers who mean harm (the `safety` row and its expert reviewer); a stranger-danger premise; screen-time coaching inside the app (the device's job); any password the child types; any score, certificate or countdown from the apps above; cyberbullying roles acted out with named culprits; "fake news" (the `media-literacy` row).

## A first game's ladder: Relay Woods

### The model, stated once

A **map** is a small graph: five to nine **stumps**, each with a friend, joined by **paths**; one stump is the **sender** (the pet) and one the **receiver** (named and pictured in the request). The pet's **picture** sits at the sender. A **hop** is a tap on a friend joined by a path to whoever holds the picture; the picture flies to them (0.4 s) with a rising pentatonic step per hop. The picture **arrives** when the receiver holds it; they hold it up, the request is answered, the round finishes. A **log** on a path blocks it. From level 5 the picture **splits** into numbered **pieces** (three, then four), each routed separately; the receiver has **slots** numbered 1–n and the pieces must be dragged into them (Story Steps' reorder with a number). A **lost** piece is drawn falling in the pond; a **tick** is a small check that flies back along the route when a piece arrives. From level 8 a message is a **postcard** (every carrier shows a thought bubble of it) or an **envelope** (blank bubbles); a **lock** closes an envelope and only its owner's **key** opens it. All of it is `Graphics` and Puzzle Pals-style render-texture pieces; the friends are the island's critters or, after the rework, her creatures.

### Proposed levels and bands

Bands: toddler 1–2, preschool 1–4, pre-K 2–6, school 4–10 (`BandLevels` with `rangeFor`; no renumbering). Lap: not supported. Two smooth rounds step up, two struggling step down, as the shell does.

### A rule test

`logic.ts` generates maps from the seeded RNG and exposes pure rules. Tests: every generated map is connected and the receiver is reachable from the sender; level 3's log leaves at least one clear route; level 4 has a *unique* shortest route at least one hop shorter than the next; level 5's piece routes produce an arrival order that differs from 1..n at least half the time over many seeds (so the slots mean something); level 6's lost piece is unique; level 7's unticked piece is unique; level 8 marks exactly one request private; level 9's valid lock is the receiver's; level 10 has at least one single added path after which the graph has no bridge, and `hint()` names an endpoint of one; `hint(state)` at every level returns a friend on a valid route from the holder (BFS), never the holder, never an unjoined friend; a `nextHop` answer is accepted if and only if it lies on *some* valid route (levels 2, 5, 10 accept every equivalent solution); miss counting ignores level 1. Historical truth is not at issue; a human review covers wording about privacy and the "show a grown-up" line.

### Cost

M. Ten levels, four bands, a graph model, piece textures, two layouts (the map must keep 100-unit stumps in portrait: seven stumps fit on a 3×3 lattice with one spare), voice lines, a how-to card with a ghost finger that routes by BFS, the game-reviewer pass. Comparable to Mail Carrier 6–8 plus Story Steps 10.

## Experience and presentation

- **The first unsupported action** is a tap on the next friend, who is the only one lit and nodding at level 1. The picture hops; the chime rises; the receiver holds it up. No reading, no instruction needed, the pet repeats "tap the next friend" if she waits.
- **Touch-down feedback:** the tapped friend bounces on press; a valid hop flies the picture; an invalid tap gets the shrug or boing within the frame.
- **Replay variety:** seeded maps, five pictures (a flower, a cake, a kite, the pet's portrait, a tune picture from Song Maker if the creations slot allows), the receiver drawn from the friends present, the log and the cloud placed by seed.
- **Off screen:** the whispering game around the family circle, then "what arrived?"; for five or more, the Orange Game.
- **Rework hooks:** her creatures are the carriers; a bird species' talent is *carrying* (one talent per species, per the [pet-integration review](../../rework/PET-INTEGRATION.md)); a picture that arrives at her creature home's post stump is a kept thing only when she asks, and nothing waits or expires there.

## Decisions for the developer

1. **A new game.** Confirm Relay Woods as a new registration (the decision is the next carrier, not the house) rather than Mail Carrier levels. Lean: new, beside Mail Carrier.
2. **Land.** Cozy Village holds twelve games and Mail Carrier; Story Grove nine; Tinker Lab thirteen. Place after the `landLayout` capacity check at every band; lean Cozy Village (Hazel's post), else Story Grove. Storybook Square stays proposed.
3. **Relay Cases' band.** `grownup` (Lantern Lake) with the two-locks and deduction cases, or a `school`-top registration too? Lean: `grownup` first; no new band.
4. **Toddler accounting.** Level 1 counts no misses (exploration) but a glow after two unjoined taps counts one hint. Confirm, or make it miss-free entirely.
5. **The safety boundary.** No character who pretends to be a friend, no contact scenario, until the `safety` row's expert reviewer exists. Confirm that Relay Woods 8–9 (a postcard anyone can read; a lock) is below that line.
6. **The family card.** Approve the four-line card for the practical-card review, including the line addressed to grown-ups about sharing her pictures, and the AAP video-chat lap line. Decide whether a link to the AAP Family Media Plan belongs on the grown-ups' page.
7. **The story's home.** The Mixed-Up Invitations as a story blanket on the map like the picnics, or as Storybook Square's first errand. Lean: define it only after Relay Woods 1–7 exist.
8. **Received pictures at home.** Whether a picture that arrives in the story or a round becomes a kept item at her creature home (RW05's collection rules), with the usual no-obligation rule.

**Decided 2026-10-23 (on the developer's delegation):** every lean: Relay Woods is a new game in Cozy Village beside Mail Carrier if `landLayout` passes, else Story Grove; toddler level 1 counts no misses and the glow after two unjoined taps counts one hint; levels 8–9 (a readable postcard, a lock) are below the safety line, and no pretend-friend or contact scenario is drafted for now; Relay Cases is `grownup` only; *Our Family Message Rules* ships after the building session's source check against the AAP sources, and the AAP Family Media Plan is linked from the grown-ups' page; the Mixed-Up Invitations is defined only after Relay Woods 1–7 exist; an arrived picture becomes a kept item at her creature home under RW05's rules.
