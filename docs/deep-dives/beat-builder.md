*ID `beat-builder` · bands preschool, pre-K, school · levels 1–5 on 2026-10-12 · session `codex/beat-builder-deep-dive` / [PR #190](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/190)*

## The game today

She turns squares on and off in a looping percussion grid. The satisfying action is hearing her arrangement play; the learning purpose is connecting sound with a visible sequence, listening for instrument placement and recognizing a repeated unit. It does not measure whether her finger lands on a beat. Source review: [index](../../src/games/beat-builder/index.ts), [rules](../../src/games/beat-builder/logic.ts), [rule tests](../../src/games/beat-builder/logic.test.ts), [how-to](../../src/content/howto.ts), [speech](../../src/content/voice-script.ts), [GAMES](../GAMES.md#beat-builder), [registry](../../src/games/registry.ts), [lands](../../src/content/lands.ts), [couch catalog](../../src/couch/catalog.ts), [idea audit](../IDEAS.md#short-ladder-audit-2026-10-09) and [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md#next-up). **Pools and sound.** No authored beat library exists. `makeBeat` chooses one to three distinct hit positions per row, forces drum at step 0 (so a drum row may have four hits), duplicates the first half for level 4, and rejects columns containing all three instruments only on three-row plans. Two-row plans allow simultaneous drum/clap despite the broad comment/test title. The four-step two-row generator can reach 128 different matrices (eight possible drum rows containing step 0 × fourteen clap rows with one to three hits). Level 4 has the same 112 first-half possibilities, duplicated. This is an analytic support count, not a frequency guarantee: forcing the opening drum weights outcomes unequally. Eight-step drum rows have 64 possibilities and other rows 92 each before rejecting all-three columns. Targets are sampled independently, so a round can repeat a target. The free target is generated but unused; her eight cells admit 256 compositions, with the current finish gate requiring at least three active cells. The bot makes alternating drum/clap.

Each step is 0.32 seconds (1.28/2.56-second loops). Drum is `sfx.drum`, clap is `sfx.knock`, and bell uses pentatonic `sfx.bell(7 + step % 3)` for both playback and touch. Music is `STYLES.quiet`; retain its live clock. There are no imported assets, kept beats, discoveries, picnic use, `control()` or couch registration. The sticker's three-by-four random drawing and icon are independent of target generation: preserve their existing seeds/art.

**Mistakes/support today.** Free toggles are welcome and never misses. At 2/4 an empty cell outside the target stays off, boings and counts one miss; existing on-cells can be turned off freely. At 3/5 every edit is free; a wrong check is one miss and replays the target. After two misses, 4 and ear modes count one automatic hint per target and show glowing plus marks for missing sounds and minus marks for excess sounds (only editable cells). Marks update after edits. Ear replay plays two complete target loops before returning to her beat; editing/checking waits for that boundary, and the note can freely restart replay. Level 2 already shows the target throughout. No replay counts as a hint. Round totals feed adaptation; copying with revealed help is supported completion.

**Built support:** slices 1–2 ([PR #192](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/192), [PR #194](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/194)) repair scoped cards, complete target replay, edit/check boundaries, add/remove markers, one hint per target and the bot's removal of extra hits. Rule tests trace first/last columns in both loops and both repair types. The level-5 filtered play reproduces missing/excess-hit recovery; restoring the old replay rule fails its sound-call trace. Bell touch/playback shares one function. See [verification](../VERIFICATION.md).

**Open gaps:** targets remain independently sampled and may repeat in one round; phrase library is slice 3. Eight 100-unit cells leave little spare width in a 1024-unit view; four-view review remains later work. Frame-driven audio can stretch a sequence on long frames. Actual iPad/Bluetooth sound, speech, touch comfort and child fit remain unjudged; browser sound-call traces do not establish acoustic timing.

**Learning references.** [NAEYC's music/math article](https://www.naeyc.org/our-work/families/support-math-readiness-through-music) distinguishes the steady pulse from varying rhythm and connects repeating patterns to early math. These family guidance sources support co-play and visible/replayable patterns; they do not set a validated timing window, prove this grid suits a one-year-old, or turn successful copying into independent musical mastery. Our ladder is a design inference. The current fixed loop is a steady stepping pulse, not a test of tapping in time.

## What makes sense here

Keep one grid as the durable score and grow what she decides: edit a faulty score, retain one layer while varying another, then arrange contrasting sections. Rests matter as places without sound, not as failed taps. Use short, original percussion phrases with explicit replay and optional instrument isolation; avoid hidden long sequences. Preschool sees and makes; pre-K copies and repeats; school listens, revises and arranges. Later elementary horizons can compare shifted phrases or compose for a fictional event, after a band/order decision. No new band exists by virtue of this plan.

Song Maker owns pitched melodies and kept tunes; Jelly Drums owns note echoes; Sound Garden owns forgiving live rhythm echoes; Rhythm Neighbors owns call-and-response timing; Pattern Train owns visual repeating units. Beat Builder earns depth through editing an inspectable percussion score. Its proposed verse/chorus slice from IDEAS becomes **A and B sections**, without requiring literacy, vocals, lyrics or a claim that every musical form has a verse and chorus. No Suno/music-generation skill or external audio is needed.

2. **Built phrase library at 2–5** ([PR #196](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/196)): twelve original four-step two-row phrases and eight original eight-step three-row phrases, authored as on/off matrices. Example four-step phrase: drum on 0/2, clap on 1/3; another has drum 0/3 and clap 2, making silence audible. Level 4 duplicates a four-step unit. Select without replacement within each three-/two-target round; preserve row presence, opening drum and the existing three-row collision rule. Random generation remains available as a later source, but does not need to compete with authored phrases in the first slice. Instrument pictures and speakable phrase nicknames come from the same data. Check cardinality, shapes, distinctness and hint repairability. No level-1 target rule imposed on her creation.
3. **Content after support:** the built woodland stage and a proposed picnic stage with the same grid and input bounds; characters respond to the currently sounding row. Instrument identity stays drum/clap/bell. More sound families need separate synthesis and icon clarity work; do not call a knock a culturally specific instrument without an accurate drawing/sound. Sticker seeds fixed. No extra targets per round.

### Younger-band openings

**Toddler 1–1, decision:** same two-row four-step free composition and input, but Done available after the first active sound has been heard. First round taps a drum cell, hears its next visit, then can finish; co-play may add a clap. Every creation welcome, including a one-hit loop; no misses/hints. Tests assert first-hit finish availability and that removing all hits returns to a clearly explained unfinished state. No new level needed, but a band-aware gate must be explicit; preschool/pre-K gates stay unchanged. Registry/lands, inventory counts and demonstration behavior need checks when built.

**Lap 1–1, decision:** do not assume an eight-cell score is comfortable for lap play. Candidate: a grown-up chooses the row, each large pat plants the next sound in the four-step score, and Done appears after hearing one. All pats valid; tap cancellation/palm rejection preserved. The bot pats once then Done, never waits for three hits. This changes interaction and overlaps Jelly Drums: developer should choose whether seeing her pats come back around earns an opening, or leave lap there. Test bounded step advance, audible finish and no miss/hint penalty; portrait and real child observation open. A plain range addition to unchanged level 1 is not this definition.

### Left out on purpose

Speed rounds, tight timing windows, lives, streaks, long hidden phrases, forced memorization, shrinking cells, more simultaneous instruments as a substitute for depth, competition/rankings, recordings/imported songs and copyrighted samples. Composition never grades taste, mood or “good music.” No automatic filling of a wrong score, no obligation to save, and no growing collection while away. A beat keepsake needs its own bounded data/repair/backup/playback design; current Song Maker tune data is not a percussion matrix. Couch needs an explicit control/bot/fair seeded plan and remains absent. Picnic does not gain a typed beat result by this proposal.
