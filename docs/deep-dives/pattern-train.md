# Pattern Train deep dive

*ID `pattern-train` · bands preschool, pre-K, school · levels 1–12 on 2026-10-11 · session `claude/pattern-train-deep-dive` ([PR 139](https://github.com/rjdunlap/neo/pull/139))*

## The game today

A train of cars rolls along a track with one or two empty cars marked `?`. She looks at what repeats and taps the choice under the train that fits the glowing car. A right pick drops into the car with a bell; a wrong one boings and says "Try another one. Look and listen to the pattern!"; after two misses on a car the right choice glows green (one hint per car). Tapping a shown car plays its bell and is always free. Puzzle Peaks (with Memory Match, Size Parade, Puzzle Pals and others). `skills`: patterns, sequencing, listening. Music `STYLES.jelly`. Off-screen: a clap–tap pattern with a beat left for the child. The ladder, level by level (`PATTERN_PLANS` in `logic.ts`; the rest of the round is in `buildTrain`):

| Level | Plan | Cars (with `?`) | Bands whose window includes it | What she does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AB shapes | 5 | preschool | continue the last car |
| 2 | AAB shapes | 7 | preschool | the same |
| 3 | ABB shapes | 7 | preschool, pre-K | the same |
| 4 | ABC shapes | 7 | preschool, pre-K | the same |
| 5 | AB animals (cat, cow, duck) | 5 | preschool, pre-K | the same with critters |
| 6 | ABC animals, **gap in the middle** | 7 | preschool, pre-K | fill the third car |
| 7 | AB bells | 5 | pre-K | hear the notes (tap a bell, then the green arrow) |
| 8 | ABC bells | 7 | pre-K, school | the same |
| 9 | AAB shapes, **two** empty cars | 8 | pre-K, school | fill the last two cars in order |
| 10 | "Find the seventh car from two repeats" | 7 | school | see below |
| 11 | "Follow color and shape together" | 7 | school | see below |
| 12 | numbered cars by 2, 5 or 10 | 7 | school | the next number |

Ranges (`LEVELS` in `index.ts`): preschool 1–6, pre-K 3–9, school 8–12. `lap` and `toddler` also have 1–1 entries, but `bands` lists only the three above, so those two entries never run. Choices are always the three symbols (`[0, 1, 2]`, one of each) in fixed screen order, except at level 12.

**What a round draws from.** Almost nothing. `tokens = rng.shuffle([0, 1, 2])` assigns the unit's letters to three fixed pieces: a red circle, a blue triangle and a yellow square (color and shape travel together as one thing), three animals (cat, cow, duck) or three bells (`4 + index * 3` steps). Level 12 draws a step from {2, 5, 10}. That is the whole content pool: no other shapes, colors, animals, sizes, turns or sounds.

**Couplings.** How-to: one step line per kind, the last open-ended (`{ from: 12 }`). Ghost finger: `touchDemo` and `autotouch()` (taps the wanted choice, then the green arrow on bell levels; waits for the notes to be played). Browser: `BROWSER_SUITE=… patterns` loops `Array.from({ length: 12 })` and asserts one hint after two wrong picks at every level; `fingerdemo` plays the top level. Couch: a face-off "team" stop, `band: 'prek'`, `demoLevel: 6`, stops at levels 4, 6 and 9 (`level: (stop) => …`), controls `control()` and `autoplay()`; appended levels leave trips and courses alone. No journal entry, treehouse slot or picnic step. The sticker is a fixed code-drawn train.

**Known gaps** (read from `logic.ts` and `index.ts`; none seen on a device).

1. **Level 10 plays exactly like level 4.** `buildTrain` never reads `plan.far`. Both are ABC shapes, two repeats and one empty car (7 cars), so "the seventh car" is just the next car; only the spoken line (`pattern.far`) and the card text differ. A test would show the two plans produce identical trains for the same seed.
2. **Level 11 is also level 4, with a longer name.** `trainSymbol` for `pair` uses `colors[value % 3]` and `shapes[value % 3]`, the same pairing `symbol('shape', …)` draws. Color and shape never vary independently, so "follow color and shape together" is one thing to follow. The existing tests pass because nothing asserts two attributes.
3. **School's window of 8–12 is thinner than five levels suggest.** Level 8 is bells, level 9 is a two-car AAB, levels 10 and 11 are reruns of level 4 and level 12 is the only new decision. A school player's ladder holds one genuinely new idea.
4. **The answer is almost always "copy the first car".** The train is two full repeats plus one car and the empty car is last, so its slot is always slot 0 of the unit: it is the same piece as the first car. At level 9 the two empty cars are slots 0 and 1, the same pieces as the first two cars. The one-in-three chance of guessing aside, a child who matches the first car passes levels 1–5 and 7–11 without finding the unit. The test checks a clue exists, not that the clue is not the first car. (Level 6's gap is always index 2, so it never moves either.)
5. **Level 12 can be answered without counting.** The sequence is `step * (i + 1)`, so the answer is only ever 14, 35 or 70; the choices are `[answer, answer + step, answer + 2 * step]`, so the answer is **always the smallest**. The train always starts at the step (never 12, 15 or 35). A child who always taps the smallest passes. There is also no gap in the middle, no counting back and no stepping by 3 or 4.
6. **The train never ends mid-unit and never continues far.** The 7-car limit is a layout fact too: cars sit at `(view.w − 110) / n` pitch with 100-unit tiles, which is at most about 9 cars at `view.w` 1024 (portrait keeps `view.w` at 1024 and grows `view.h`). An AABB or ABBC train with two repeats and one empty car is 9 and just fits; anything longer, such as a growing pattern or a genuinely far car, needs a different approach.
7. **One content set per kind.** A shape round is always red circle, blue triangle and yellow square; only the order of the roles changes. Pattern research says to vary the objects, sizes, orientations, actions and sounds ([NCETM](https://www.ncetm.org.uk/classroom-resources/ey-pattern)); here only the roles shuffle.
8. **A distractor is never a near miss.** Under AB the third choice (a piece that is nowhere on the train) is easy to rule out. The informative wrong answers, such as the other piece in an AAB train (an AB alternation), are 1 in 3 at best and are not chosen on purpose.
9. **Dead and drifting entries.** `LEVELS.lap` and `LEVELS.toddler` are unreachable (above); `LEVELS.school.max` is hard-coded at 12 and does not follow `PATTERN_PLANS.length`; the how-to's last step is `{ from: 12 }` and would describe level 13 and above wrongly; `browser-check.mjs` hard-codes `length: 12` and `level === 12`.
10. **The hint says "this one"**, not why. It points at a choice (`pattern.hint`) and never replays the train, which is what the research says helps most (say the pattern aloud, below).
11. **Person checks open** ([roadmap](../ROADMAP.md#needs-a-person-or-a-device)): none are recorded for this game. Whether she can use level 1 at three, whether the bell levels (confirm arrow) read on an iPad and whether the numbered cars (31-point text on level 12's tens) are legible have not been looked at on a device.

## What makes sense here

The mechanic (look, find the repeat, fill the car) is right and the three kinds (see, hear, count) are right. The ladder's problem is that it asks one question, "what is the next car?", in twelve costumes, and the question can be answered by copying the first car. The audit's test, is there something new for her to **decide**, gives this order of value:

1. **Make each existing level ask what its name says** (gaps 1–5): the train ends in different places in the unit; the "seventh" car is far along, behind a tunnel; color and shape change on their own cycles; the number train starts anywhere and its answer is not the smallest. No new levels, no renumbering, and the school window gets three real levels.
2. **New decisions, from the research:** spot and mend a wrong car; say which train has the same pattern in other pieces; build the same pattern in other pieces; make her own pattern and see it go round; growing patterns as stairs; then the number levels (start anywhere, count back, add three) that lead to 4.OA.5.
3. **More to play** (the developer's 2026-10-10 direction): a real pool of car kinds and objects so a level never looks the same twice.

Not a fit: a train that must be finished before it leaves, a count-down to the station, speed or a second train to race, and any score or streak. The pet does not wait on her. Letter symbols (AB…) on screen stay out for now. Her long-term arc: **preschool** copies a repeating thing and finds the next; **pre-K** finds the unit, hears it, and mends a train; **school** states the rule, carries it far, builds it in other pieces and grows it by counting. The couch stays at its three stops.

## Proposed ladder

### Fresh content inside existing levels

Each is a change to `buildTrain` or a pool, with a rule test that fails on today's code.

- **The train ends anywhere in the unit.** Cars shown = `repeats × unit + phase`, the empty car is the next one, and its slot cycles through the unit's slots instead of always 0. AB trains run 5–6 cars, AAB/ABB/ABC trains 7–9 (9 is the limit: AABB and ABBC would be exactly 9). At level 9 the two empty cars start at slot 0 or 1 (8 or 9 cars; slot 2 would need 10). Level 6's gap (an index-2 car in the first repeat) can sit in either repeat and any slot except the first. Preschool level 1 keeps phase 0 or 1 only. *A rule test asserts* that over 200 seeds the target's slot takes every value in the unit and the answer is not the first car in at least a third of rounds beyond level 1, and that the shortest-repeat check in the existing test still equals the unit (it does when at least one full repeat plus the first car of the next shows).
- **Objects drawn from a pool, not three fixed pieces.** A `CAR_KINDS` table: six shapes in three colors (color and shape as separate attributes at levels 11 and later), animals (the critters already in `CRITTERS`; no new art), fruit, flowers, boats and cars (code-drawn), **sizes** (one shape small, medium, large) and **turns** (an arrow up, right, down) on later levels, and **actions** (the pet acting a clap, a stamp or a jump, as in Pet Says) as the movement medium. A round draws three that look clearly different (a distinct-silhouette rule, a test like Opposites' pair guard) from one kind; a car's tap plays its own sound (an animal's voice from `sfx.ts`, a bell for others). *A test asserts* every kind has at least three members, every draw is three distinct pieces, and every piece fits a 100-unit tile.
- **Wrong answers that mean something.** At level 3 (ABB) one distractor is the AB alternation; at level 2 (AAB) the ABAB continuation; at level 4 the swap of two neighbors. *A test asserts* the choices still hold exactly one correct answer and, where the pattern allows, include the near-miss.
- **A better hint.** After two misses the pet first **reads the train** (each car lights and sounds in turn, then the unit hops once), and a third miss lights the choice. Still one hint per car. *A test asserts* a hint is counted once per car.
- **Level 10, the far car, made real.** The train rolls into a **tunnel** after two repeats (6 cars), the tunnel is a tile that says how many cars are hidden (dots and a spoken count, from 4 to 7, never fewer than a unit), and the car that rolls out is the empty one (8 tiles in all). The decision is jumping whole units: with k hidden cars and a unit of 3 the answer is slot `k mod 3`. After two misses the hidden cars light up one by one. *A test asserts* the answer slot equals `(shown + hidden) mod unit`, the answer is not the car just before the tunnel in at least half the rounds, and `far` plans differ from level 4's for every seed.
- **Level 11, color and shape on their own cycles.** Color cycles red, blue (period 2) and shape cycles circle, circle, triangle, triangle (period 4), so the combined pattern repeats every four cars (8 cars shown, the empty car is the ninth: 9 tiles). She chooses from **four** pieces: the right one, right color and wrong shape, right shape and wrong color, and neither; a wrong pick names which part was right ("The color is right! Look at the shape."). Four choices at 120 units with 170 spacing sit centered with the pet corner clear. *A test asserts* the color cycle and the shape cycle have different lengths, exactly one of four choices fits both, and the half-right choices fit exactly one attribute.
- **Level 12, numbers that need counting.** The train starts at a number that is not the step (12, 14, 16… by 2; 15, 20, 25… by 5; 30, 40, 50… by 10), the answer is not always the smallest (choices include the previous number, the answer, and a wrong step or off by one: 20, 25, 26 for a train of fives), the empty car can sit in the middle, and values stay to 100. *A test asserts* the answer's position among the choices varies, the answer is not the smallest in more than about half the rounds, at least 20 distinct answers occur over 200 seeds and every sequence is exact arithmetic from its start.

### Windows and the younger bands

- **School 8–21.** The new levels 13–21 are all in school (6–8): they are a mix of easier and harder than 10–12 (13 odd car out is easier than 11), so as in every other deep dive some of them are **inversions**: "step up" can lead to an easier level. The cheap rule is to place it with **`order`** (built). Nothing below level 8 moves, so no saved level or pin shifts.
- **Pre-K 3–9, with later additions.** Levels 13, 15, 17 and 18 are suitable for pre-K, but a pre-K range must be contiguous (3–9 then 13 would skip 10–12). They use `order`, which may skip IDs (built). Until then they sit in school.
- **Preschool 1–6 stays.** The phase change in fresh content makes levels 1–6 harder in a good way; confirm with a person that a three-year-old can use level 1 with the gap in the second slot.
- **Toddler (opening from level 1).** `ctx.band` exists, so a toddler opening can be level 1 itself: three repeats of AB shown, **two** choices (no stray third piece), the glow after the first miss, and each pick sung as it falls in. No new level and no ordering decision. It is a first look at "pick the one that goes next", for a child of two or three; check with a person. Whether `toddler` joins `bands` with a 1–1 window is the only change; the lap and toddler entries in `LEVELS` today are dead and should be removed or used.
- **Lap.** Nothing in this game is a lap game. The idea in IDEAS, **Color Train** (a color-matching train from JumpStart Baby: any tap sends the next object to the car of its color), is a different game shape; it waits on the decision about lap levels outside a ladder's order ([Waiting on the developer](../ROADMAP.md#waiting-on-the-developer)).

### Levels below level 1 (waiting on the level-order decisions)

- **Level 0, Roll along** (sketch): a track of two alternating colored cars and no question; a tap anywhere sends the next car out, and the pet names each color. Exposure to alternation before a choice. Waits on the level-order decisions (a level below 1 cannot be appended).
- **Level −1, Ding-dong** (sketch): a bell train of one note that she taps again and again; the third time the second note joins. Same wait.

### Left out on purpose

- A race, a clock, a station countdown, a score, a streak, lives: ground rules.
- A **repeat the whole tune from memory** level: Jelly Drums owns copy-by-ear, and a bell train more than 7 cars long is memory load.
- **Letters for the pattern** (AABB on screen): not reading; the pet says the pieces.
- **A random hidden rule** ("find the rule from the first three cars"): a guess, not a decision (Moofy's trap), unless the rule is stated in the instruction.
- **Counting by hundreds**, negative numbers and a "what do you notice" odd-or-even level (4.OA.5's second half): it waits on [whether Wonder Woods grows](../ROADMAP.md#waiting-on-the-developer) before a band above it; the school ladder stops at 21.
- **Two trains side by side to compare** as a race: level 15 asks the same without a race.

## Decisions for the developer

1. **Make the school levels do what their names say?** Lean yes: levels 10–12 are the audit's "toy-sized top" in a literal sense (two are level 4 again). This is the first slice and needs no new level.
2. **Accept the inversions in 13–21** (13 and 15 are easier than 11 and 12), or fold them into the level-order decision. Lean: accept and say so on the card.
3. **Level 17, Make your own, climbs fast** (its rounds are always 0 and 0). Lean: accept, as Stamp Studio's invitations do, or put it at the top.
4. **A toddler window on level 1** (two choices), and whether to remove the dead lap and toddler entries in `LEVELS`. Lean: yes to both.
5. **A kind pool with sizes, turns and acting.** Lean: yes; sizes and turns come after shapes and animals. The pet's pose art is the cost.
6. **Level 21 (add three, add four) and the 4.OA.5 "what do you notice" sketch** wait on a child playing 19–20 and on the Wonder Woods decision.

## Build slices, in order

Each is one session. Slices 1–6 are built (2026-10-11; [slice 1 build note](../archive/COMPLETED-2026-10-09.md#pattern-train-slice-1-the-train-ends-anywhere-2026-10-11)); the rest are not.

1. **Built.** **Make levels 4–9 and 12 ask what they say** (`logic.ts` only, with its rule tests, which must fail on today's code): the train ends anywhere in the unit (levels 1–5, 8, 9 and the middle gap of 6), level 12's starts, answer position and middle gap, near-miss distractors, `LEVELS.school.max` following the plan table, a pinned sticker test, a bounded how-to.
