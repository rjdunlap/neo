## The game today

She makes a pretend week unfold: hear and select a weekday, tap the placing arrow, then match a visible sky to record its weather. One flower opens per recorded day. The learning purpose is weekday order, the repeating week, relative-day language and counting observations. All seven days happen within one round; the real date is irrelevant. The garden counts days, not the biological effect of a particular weather.

| Level | Plan name | Built band window | What she decides / finish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Place the days and record a pretend week | pre-K 1–2 | Monday first, then an explicitly named next day from three spoken-on-tap cards. Confirm with the arrow, match the visible weather. Seven records finish |
| 2 | Tomorrow and yesterday, including the week turning | pre-K 1–2; school 2–3 | Monday is named; the other six prompts ask what follows the preceding day. After the week, answer **tomorrow after Sunday** and **yesterday before Monday**, always those two questions |
| 3 | Yesterday, tomorrow and a sunny-day tally | school 2–3 | Level 2, then count sunny records and choose one of three numbers. Record taps mark/unmark freely and replay day/weather; marks need not be correct or complete to submit the total |

Source audit: [logic](../../src/games/day-by-day/logic.ts), [game](../../src/games/day-by-day/index.ts), [rule tests](../../src/games/day-by-day/logic.test.ts), [how-to](../../src/content/howto.ts), [voice](../../src/content/voice-script.ts), [GAMES](../GAMES.md#day-by-day). No couch catalog entry, controller/bot controls, saved creation, journal discovery or picnic step. Touch demo uses `autotouch()` to select/confirm, match weather, mark every sun and answer. Its total comes from the records, not from the marks.

**Pools and replay.** Seven fixed weekday names, three weather pictures (sunny/cloudy/rainy), three plans. `makeWeek()` shuffles one of each weather plus four independent draws: seven records, every category present, each count 1–5. There are 1,806 possible ordered weather strings containing all three categories (not asserted equally likely by this generator). Day choices are the answer plus two distinct distractors from the remaining six; number choices are the answer plus two from 0–7. Weather choices stay in sunny/cloudy/rainy order. Flower colors repeat pink/purple/orange, and backdrop seed 72 is fixed. The two relative questions and sunny tally category never vary. Seed tests cover repeatability and over 70 distinct weeks in 100 seeds, wrap arithmetic, choices, reversible marks and layout bounds; they were read, not rerun here.

**Accounting and support.** Naming/selecting day cards is exploration; only pressing the arrow commits. Weather and total buttons submit immediately. Wrong submissions add a miss, boing and spoken answer explanation; two misses on one question add one hint and glow the right choice. The counter resets for each question, so a week can accumulate several hints. Replay and marks are free; no mark is judged. Instructions are repeatable through the shell pet, and input waits for speech. A completed week earns one shell-awarded sticker with help allowed. Home can leave at any time; there is no unfinished-week obligation.

**Known gaps and observations needed.** Monday-first layout is a convention, not a universal calendar rule. The straight seven-record row has no drawn wrap connection despite the spoken explanation. Relative prompts test only the boundary, not transfer to a different anchor day. Every total is nonzero and only sunny is requested. Three similar printed day labels require sequential audio exploration for a nonreader; spoken support exists but actual device pronunciation and the select/arrow distinction remain [unchecked](../DEVICE-CHECKS.md#needs-a-person-or-a-device). Each level already needs at least 21 deliberate taps to record the week, plus four for relatives and one for the tally at level 3, before free replays or marks. More questions must earn their length. The code does not establish appeal, independent weekday understanding, or transfer to real observations.

Neither standard establishes that weekday labels alone demonstrate temporal understanding. ## What makes sense here

Deepen the same action: place a time label, record what a picture shows, then use the record to answer. Keep every record visible or available on an explicit page; audio replay removes a reading requirement and markers remove a memory requirement. Broader relative-day anchors and counting a different category offer transfer without a larger screen or faster pace. Comparing two finite weeks is the natural Weather Window extension, alongside Picture Graph's reusable chart-reading ideas; it should not become another graph game detached from the weeks.

Preschool could begin with matching three pictured skies, without pretending to teach weekday order. Pre-K can grow from explicitly named days to a relative day within the visible cycle. School can count categories, repair a week and compare records. Months and daylight belong only after their representation and placement decisions; causal seasons, biological life cycles and weather-making remain in their own reviewed games. No grown-up-only ladder is needed for this small activity; co-play is enough.

## Experience and presentation

The first unsupported action today is tapping a day card: it speaks the name and selects it without judging. A player can then press the arrow or listen to another. Retain the shared `onTap` down feedback. Slice 1 should make the consequence easier to see: on a correct placement, briefly trace a code-drawn teal thread into its slot; on recording, a cream weather stamp settles into that slot and the next flower opens. Use palette colors, decorative drawings with `eventMode = 'none'`, and the existing synthesized pop/sparkle. Never make the weather prettier only for correct first attempts.

Add a short dotted return path beneath Sunday and Monday, highlighted while the boundary question is spoken. The seven existing 108 × 112 records and 170 × 110 answer targets keep their sizes. For newer two-week comparisons, show one full-size seven-card page at a time with large A/B tabs, a persistent pictured question and replay; do not put fourteen shrunken targets side by side. Layout and a portrait view must prove page controls, records and corner pet stay separate. Do not attach semantic meanings to flower color; three seeded garden palettes/planter outlines can vary appearance without changing the record or answer.

The challenge is selecting the relevant day or evidence, not remembering speech, reading weekday text, or landing a precise drag. Day selections continue to speak before confirmation; new summary cards speak before a separate confirm too. Record pages and marks remain free. Wrong submissions get a specific explanation, two misses glow a useful record and the answer once for that question. Supported completion gives the same sticker, then the shell offers replay/next/Home. Leaving halfway saves no debt or unfinished task.

Solo can do every action. In ordinary co-play one partner names/places, the other records or points to evidence, then swap after a day; this needs no second-seat infrastructure or separate mastery claim. Off screen, retain the existing seven-card pretend-week suggestion; a real observation activity would need its own reviewed practical card and must never feed a streak.

Person/device questions: does listening then pressing the arrow feel natural; can she locate a spoken day in the row; does the wrap path explain repetition; is seven days too long; does each garden bloom read as a day counter; can she distinguish cloudy from rainy on iPad; can a nonreader revisit the right A/B week; does a partner have a real role? Bots cannot answer these. Device speech, actual touch feel, child appeal and independent transfer stay open.

### Left out on purpose

Real-date calendars, attendance, daily gifts, streaks, seasonal scarcity, round timers, holiday quizzes and remembering hidden records. No weather prediction is scored from a seven-day sample. No saved calendar/journal format, online forecast, location collection or two-seat credit system. No claim that sunshine alone grows the flowers. A caterpillar does not transform in seven weekdays: the notebook's former Caterpillar Week now belongs to the separate [Life Story Lab](subjects/life-science.md), with biological observation-time data. Weather Wardrobe retains clothing choices; Clock Tower retains clock time; Picture Graph retains general graph construction.
